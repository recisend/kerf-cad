## Geometry (optimized for Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle)
- Footprint 95.5 x 92.2 mm, height 90 mm (fits U1's 270 mm bed with huge margin; could batch 4+ per plate)
- 7 hex cells, heights 90 / 70 / 60 mm stepped
- Walls 1.6 mm = exactly 4 x 0.4 mm perimeters; floor 1.6 mm = 8 layers @ 0.2 mm
- All Z heights are multiples of 0.2 mm; flat base, vertical walls, **no supports, no overhangs, no bridges**
- Cell inner flat-to-flat 29.7 mm (fits pens, brushes, markers, makeup brushes, cables)
- Est. 94.6 g PLA solid-equivalent (real use is less with infill)

## Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca / U1 0.4 profile)
- Layer 0.2 mm, 4 walls, 3-4 top/bottom layers, infill 10-15% gyroid (walls do the work)
- No supports, brim not needed (large flat base); PLA ~220 C nozzle / 60 C bed
- Vase-mode is NOT suitable (shared inner walls)
- Tip: for the U1 multi-tool head, assign a different filament per cell for a multicolour version (colour up-charge)
