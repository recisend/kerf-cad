# Faceted Phone Dock + Catch-All Tray (original design)

Printer: Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle (build volume 270^3 mm; part is 100 x 80 x 50 mm)
File: faceted-phone-dock-snapmaker-u1.3mf (mm units, one part)

## Slicer settings
- Orientation: as exported, flat on the bed. No supports needed.
  - Phone slot leans ~22 deg from vertical, so no overhang exceeds 45 deg.
  - Cable channel is a 10 mm round bore (about 1 mm overhang at top, bridges fine).
- Layer height 0.2 mm (50 mm height = 250 layers, clean multiple)
- Line width 0.42-0.45, walls 3 (1.2-1.35 mm), top/bottom 4 layers
- Infill 15% gyroid/cubic (stand needs weight and stiffness)
- Material: PLA / PLA+ (silk or matte both work)
- Suggested first run: 0.2 mm Standard, ~6-8 h, estimate 110-140 g with infill
  (solid mass in CAD is 372 g, the slicer will use far less)

## Design notes
- Slot width 14 mm fits most phones with a case. Tray 80 x 32 x 16 mm for keys/earbuds.
- 6 mm vertical corner radius. Bottom edge is sharp: add 0.6 mm elephant-foot compensation in slicer.
- Fully original geometry, no brands, characters or real-item likenesses.
