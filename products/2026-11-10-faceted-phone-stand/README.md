# Faceted Phone Stand (2026-11-10)

Original design, not based on any real product, brand or character. Built in Kerf CAD (`faceted-phone-stand.kerf.json`), exported as `faceted-phone-stand.3mf`.

**Status: draft only. Nothing has been published to Etsy or anywhere else.**

## Why this product
Third-party 2026 guides (not official Etsy data, treat as loose signals) repeatedly list phone/desk accessories and home organisation among steady 3D-print sellers on Etsy. A plain, unbranded stand avoids licensing problems that hit light-up signs, figures and logo items. Cable-slot stands are a low-risk everyday item with room for colour variants.

## Dimensions
- 100 x 75 x 72 mm, flat base, one part, no supports.
- Phone slot: 13.8 mm wide, ~59 degree backrest. Fits phones with cases up to ~12 mm thick.
- 18 mm wide cable notch in the front lip for charging.
- 0.6 mm chamfer on bottom edges (reduces elephant foot), 3 mm fillets on top and back edges.
- Triangular window through the side saves material and gives the stand its look.

## Snapmaker U1 print settings (0.4 mm nozzle)
- Model is already centred on the 270 x 270 mm bed (X 85-185, Y 97.5-172.5, Z 0-72). Print as oriented, base on the bed.
- Layer height 0.20 mm, line width 0.42 mm (Snapmaker Orca "0.20mm Standard" is a good base).
- 3 walls, 4 top / 4 bottom layers, 15-20% gyroid or cubic infill.
- No supports, no brim needed. Every overhang is 45 degrees or steeper, the slot floor and lip are flat and open on top.
- Material: PLA or PETG. Roughly 90-110 g of filament and about 2.5-3 h at standard speed (estimate, confirm in the slicer).
- The 3MF from Kerf carries geometry and colour only, not slicer profile settings. Pick the U1 0.4 mm profile in Snapmaker Orca and apply the values above.
- Multicolour option (U1 has 4 toolheads): a second colour for the lip and top face works well as a listing variant.
