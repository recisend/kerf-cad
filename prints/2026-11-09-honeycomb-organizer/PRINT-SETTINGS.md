# Honeycomb Desk Organizer (original design, 2026-11-09)

Original geometric design, not based on any real product. 7 hex cells, staggered heights
(55 to 100 mm), pen / brush / tool holder. Status: DRAFT, not published anywhere.

## Why this one
Desk organizers, honeycomb/modular storage and gift-ready desk items are consistently
cited as strong Etsy 3D-print categories for 2026, and the holiday gifting season is
starting. Demand figures in those sources come from seller blogs, so confirm in Etsy
search before listing.

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle settings
- Footprint 124.7 x 120 mm, height 100 mm, fits the 270 x 270 x 270 mm bed.
- Printed upright, flat base, no supports, no overhangs. Volume 80.4 cm3, about 100 g PLA.
- Walls 1.6 mm (4 perimeters of 0.4), floor 2.0 mm (10 layers at 0.2 mm).
- Layer height 0.2 mm, 4 walls, 3 top / 4 bottom layers, 10-15% gyroid infill (mostly walls).
- PLA: 210 C nozzle, 60 C bed. Brim optional, 5 mm.
- Single colour; to sell as colourful, print in a silk or gradient PLA.
