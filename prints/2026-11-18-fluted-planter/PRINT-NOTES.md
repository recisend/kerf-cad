inference from vendor blog posts, not verified Etsy sales data — check Etsy search/Marketplace Insights before listing.

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle fit
- Fits the 270 x 270 x 270 mm volume: planter 83.8 x 83.8 x 92 mm, saucer 76 x 76 x 9 mm.
- Walls and floor 1.6 mm = 4 x 0.4 mm lines. Drain hole 8 mm.
- Vertical flutes only (grooves are cut, nothing added), so no overhang beyond about 12 deg off vertical: no supports.
- Flat base on both parts, no bridges.
- Mass at PLA density: ~50 g planter, ~12 g saucer.

## Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca, set these yourself — the 3MF carries geometry only)
- 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layer, 4 walls, Arachne, 3-4 top/bottom layers, 10-15% infill, no supports, no brim needed.
- Use textured PEI, PLA/PETG. Check the first print for the flute shading and the fit of the planter in the saucer.
