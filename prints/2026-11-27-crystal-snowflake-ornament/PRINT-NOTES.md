# Crystal Snowflake Ornament (2026-11-27)

Original geometric design. It is not based on any real product, brand, character or licensed item.

## Files
- `crystal-snowflake-ornament.3mf` is the print file (mm, 2 colour-tagged parts: white body, blue gem).
- `crystal-snowflake-ornament.kerf.json` is the editable Kerf CAD source.
- `preview-top.png` and `preview-iso.png` are previews.

## Model facts
- Size is 92.5 x 74.5 mm. The body is 3.0 mm thick and the gem rises to 4.2 mm.
- It prints flat with no supports and no overhangs (every layer is an extrusion of the layer below).
- Smallest features:
  - Arms are 3.5 mm wide and branches are 3.0 mm wide (roughly 9 and 7 extrusion lines at 0.4 mm).
  - The loop wall is 2.75 mm and the string hole is 3.5 mm.
  - The gem is 1.2 mm tall (6 layers at 0.2 mm).
- Mesh check on the exported 3MF: 0 non-manifold edges, and the body has 2 boundary loops (outline and string hole).
- Weight is about 6 g of filament (body 4574 mm3, gem 132 mm3 at PLA density).

## Snapmaker U1 settings (0.4 mm nozzle)
- Layer height 0.2 mm, 3 walls, 4 top and 4 bottom layers.
- Infill 15% (gyroid). It is nearly solid anyway at this thickness.
- No supports, no brim. Use the textured or smooth PEI plate (smooth gives a glossy underside).
- Tool 1: white or silver PLA for the body. Tool 2: a blue, gold or any contrast PLA for the gem.
- Layer 16 (z = 3.0 mm) is where the gem starts. No filament swap is needed on the U1 because it is a separate toolhead.
- The 3MF part colours are only a starting point. Re-assign filaments per part in the slicer (Orca-based Snapmaker slicer) and slice once to confirm the estimate.
