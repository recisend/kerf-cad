## Printer optimization — Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle
- Size 156.7 x 98 x 90 mm (U1 bed 270 x 270 mm): fits with room to spare.
- Walls 2.0 mm (5 x 0.4 mm lines); shared walls 4.0 mm; floors 2.4 mm (12 layers @ 0.2 mm).
- Flat bottom, 0.6 mm chamfer on the bottom edge against elephant foot. No supports needed.
- Phone slot leans 22 degrees from vertical: worst overhang is 22 degrees (limit ~45).
- Suggested slicer: 0.2 mm layers, 3 perimeters minimum, 12-15% gyroid infill, no supports, no brim.
- 3MF export is in millimetres. Open it in Snapmaker Orca and apply your own U1 filament profile.

## Contents
Three hex pen/tool cups (90 / 70 / 55 mm tall) plus a 14 mm wide phone slot at a 22 degree recline.
Slot fits most phones in a normal case up to ~13 mm thick. Not tested on a physical print yet.
