## Parts (both in one 3MF, laid out side by side on the bed)
- Planter: tapered hexagon, 92 x 80 x 75 mm, 2.0 mm walls, 8 mm drain hole, flat base. ~42 cm3.
- Saucer: hexagon, 88 x 76 x 9 mm, 2.0 mm walls/floor. ~13.6 cm3.
- Combined footprint ~ 145 x 92 mm — well inside the U1's 270^3 mm volume.

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle settings (Snapmaker Orca / OrcaSlicer)
- Nozzle 0.4 mm, layer 0.20 mm, line width 0.42 mm
- Walls: 3-4 loops (2.0 mm wall = ~4 lines); top/bottom layers 4 / 5; infill 10-15% gyroid
- No supports needed: flat bottom, outward taper only (~7.6 deg), no overhangs
- Print as-is (planter open end up). Material: PLA/PETG. Silk or dual-colour PLA looks best.
- Consider "spiral vase"-style or fuzzy-skin only if you want a different texture.

## Caveats
- The loft gives a taper with a very slight skew, not a strong twist.
- Model not yet test-printed; sanity-check in the slicer preview before listing.
- Drain-hole planter is not watertight by design; saucer catches water.
