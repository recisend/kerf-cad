# Honeycomb Desk Organizer (original design)

Seven interlocking hexagonal cups of stepped heights (40/55/70/80 mm) for pens, brushes,
makeup tools or desk odds-and-ends. Original geometry, not based on any real product, brand or character.

- Size: 90.4 x 87.3 x 80 mm (fits the Snapmaker U1's 270 mm cube with room to spare)
- File: `honeycomb-desk-organizer.3mf` (mm), source: `honeycomb-desk-organizer.kerf.json`

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle settings
- Orient as exported (flat floor on the bed). No supports needed: all walls are vertical.
- Wall 1.6 mm (= 4 perimeters at 0.4 mm), floor 1.6 mm (= 8 layers at 0.2 mm). Shared walls are single 1.6 mm walls.
- Layer height 0.2 mm, 4 wall loops, 4 bottom / 3 top layers, 10-15 % infill (walls are almost all perimeters).
- Open in Snapmaker Orca or OrcaSlicer, pick the Snapmaker U1 0.4 mm profile. Use PLA, or one color per cup via the U1's tool changer.
- Est. ~70 g solid PLA volume (less with sparse infill).
