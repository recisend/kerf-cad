# Honeycomb Desk Organizer (original design, 2026-11-12)

Generic 7-cell hexagonal pen/tool cup with staggered heights. Original geometry, not based on any real product or brand.

## Model
- Footprint: 84.4 x 81.5 mm, height 95 mm (fits Snapmaker U1's 270 x 270 x 270 mm volume with lots of room, so several can be printed per plate)
- Cell inner flat-to-flat: 26 mm. Shared walls: 1.6 mm (4 x 0.4 mm lines). Floor: 2.0 mm (10 layers at 0.2 mm)
- Cell heights: centre 95 mm, three at 75 mm, three at 55 mm
- Single solid body, vertical walls and flat base: no supports, no overhangs
- Volume about 63.2 cm3 (roughly 80 g in PLA at 100% shell-heavy settings; actual weight depends on slicer)

## Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle, Orca-based Snapmaker slicer)
- Layer height 0.2 mm, line width 0.42 mm
- Walls: 4 (the 1.6 mm shared walls are solid perimeter), top/bottom layers: 4/5, infill 10 percent (almost no infill exists)
- Supports: off. Brim: optional (0-3 mm) if the base lifts at corners
- Multi-colour option: the U1 is a 4-toolhead printer, so assign a different filament per cell height tier in the slicer via a colour change or by painting, for a trending multi-colour look
- Material: PLA or PETG
