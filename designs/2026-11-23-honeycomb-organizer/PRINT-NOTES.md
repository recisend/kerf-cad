- 7 hexagonal cells (1 centre 100 mm, 3 at 80 mm, 3 at 60 mm), stepped honeycomb pen / tool / brush holder

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle slicer settings (Orca-based Snapmaker Orca)

- Walls: 4 loops, Arachne wall generator; top 0 (open cells), bottom 5 layers
- Infill 10 % gyroid (little infill to place — mostly walls); no supports; brim not required (large flat footprint)
- PLA ~215 C nozzle / 60 C bed; vase mode NOT suitable (shared walls)
- Multicolour idea (U1 has 4 toolheads): colour the three height tiers differently by layer-height colour change at 60 mm and 80 mm
