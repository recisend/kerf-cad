- Size: 70.0 x 85.9 x 3.0 mm, ~5.8 g PLA (~4.7 cm3)

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle optimisation
- Fits easily in the 270 x 270 x 270 mm volume; a 3x3 plate holds 9 ornaments, so one plate is a full batch
- Thickness 3.0 mm = 15 layers at 0.20 mm; no supports, no overhangs, flat face on the bed
- All arm and branch widths are 4.0-4.8 mm (10-12 perimeter lines of 0.4 mm); the narrowest feature is the pointed tip
- Eyelet ring wall is 2.3 mm (about 6 perimeters), so it won't tear when hung
- Suggested: 0.20 mm layers, 3 walls, 3 top/bottom layers, 15% gyroid (or 100% - it is only 3 mm thick), no supports, brim not needed
- Multicolour idea (U1 has 4 toolheads): swap filament at layer 14 for a two-tone top, or print in white / silver / ice-blue PLA for bestseller colours
