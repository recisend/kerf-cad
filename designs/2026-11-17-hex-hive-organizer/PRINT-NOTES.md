# Hex Hive Desk Organizer (2026-11-17)

Original design, not based on any real product, brand, character or licensed item.

- Four honeycomb pen/tool cups, heights 100 / 80 / 60 / 45 mm, 50 mm across flats, one-piece.
- Size 122.6 x 99.6 x 100 mm (fits the Snapmaker U1's 270 mm cube easily).
- Volume ~81 cm3 (about 100 g of PLA at typical infill).

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle optimisation
- Walls are 1.6 mm = exactly 4 perimeters of 0.4 mm. Shared walls between cups are also 1.6 mm.
- Floor is 2.4 mm = 12 layers at 0.2 mm (or 8 at 0.3 mm).
- Every wall is vertical and the floor is flat: no supports, no overhangs, no bridges.
- Flat bottom sits directly on the bed.
- Export: 3MF (mm).

## Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca)
- Profile: Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layer.
- 4 walls, 5-6 top/bottom layers, 10-15% infill (walls are nearly solid anyway).
- Seam: aligned or back. Print in PLA or PETG. Tip: use a silk or dual-colour filament for the shelf look.
