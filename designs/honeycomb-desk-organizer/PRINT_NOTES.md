# Honeycomb Desk Organizer (original design, 2026-11-09)

Three fused hexagonal cups (60 / 90 / 120 mm tall) for pens, brushes and small tools. Original geometry, not based on any real product, brand or character.

## Why this design
Seller guides for 2026 repeatedly list desk organizers and geometric desk decor among consistent Etsy sellers (see sources in the run summary). Those guides are mostly seller blogs, not Etsy sales data, so treat "trending" as a reasonable signal and not a guarantee.

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle settings
- Size 98.4 x 99.6 x 120 mm, well inside the 270 x 270 x 270 mm build volume.
- Wall thickness 1.6 mm = exactly 4 perimeters at 0.4 mm line width. Floor 2.0 mm.
- No overhangs, no bridges: print as-is, no supports.
- Single colour (PLA, #e8a33d in the file). Orientation: as exported, flat base on the bed.
- Suggested: 0.2 mm layer height, 4 walls, 15% gyroid infill (walls are nearly solid anyway), ~92 g of PLA.
- Verify in Snapmaker Orca before selling. I could not run the slicer here.
