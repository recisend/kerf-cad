# Hex Desk Organizer (original design)

Original geometric three-cell desk organizer (pens, brushes, tools, makeup brushes, small plants).
Not based on any real product, brand, or character.

## Why this design
Seller guides for 2026 consistently list desk organizers / functional decor as steady Etsy sellers.
Note: no hard Etsy sales data was available for this run; trend evidence is directional only (seller blogs).

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle settings
- Size: 103.9 x 105 x 95 mm (U1 build volume 270^3 - fits easily). Print upright, no supports needed.
- Walls: 1.6 mm = 4 perimeters of 0.4 mm. Floor: 2.0 mm = 10 layers @ 0.2 mm.
- Layer height 0.2 mm (0.16 for a nicer finish), 4 walls, 3 top / 10 bottom layers, 10-15% gridfill.
- Vertical walls only, 0.8 mm bottom chamfer to avoid elephant-foot flare; no overhangs.
- ~75 cm3, ~93 g PLA.
- Export: 3MF, mm units, single part (colour #3f8f7a is a preview hint; pick any filament).
