# Honeycomb Desk Organizer — print notes (original design, no licensed/real-world items)

**Printer:** Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle (270 mm build volume; this part is 96.4 x 93.1 x 95 mm)
**File:** honeycomb-desk-organizer.3mf (mm, single part, flat base, no supports needed)

## Design choices for 0.4 mm nozzle
- Walls 1.6 mm = exactly 4 perimeters at 0.4 mm line width (shared inner walls too)
- Floor 2.0 mm = 10 layers at 0.2 mm
- Cell opening 30 mm flat-to-flat (pens, brushes, makeup brushes, cables)
- All walls vertical, no overhangs, no bridges -> prints clean with no supports
- Seven cells at three heights (95 / 75 / 55 mm)

## Suggested slicer settings (Orca/Snapmaker Orca)
- 0.2 mm layer, 4 walls, 3 top/ 0 top needed (open top), 5 bottom layers, 10-15% infill (nearly all walls anyway)
- Brim optional (small 3-4 mm) for tall-thin tips; PLA ~ 92 g, roughly 6-7 h
- Silk PLA / multi-color filament changes at layer heights 55 and 75 mm make nice banding
