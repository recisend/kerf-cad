# Honeycomb Desk Organizer (2026-11-11)

Original geometric design, no brands, characters or real-world products.
Seven hexagonal cells for pens, brushes, tools or makeup brushes, stepped in height (80 / 68 / 55 mm).

Files
- `honeycomb-desk-organizer_snapmaker-u1.3mf` is the print file (2 parts: `Base` dark, `Walls` amber).
- `honeycomb-desk-organizer.kerf.json` is the editable Kerf source.
- `hex-organizer-iso.png` is the preview render.

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle settings
| Item | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Size | 118.8 x 114.5 x 80 mm | Fits the 270 mm bed with room for a skirt or brim. |
| Walls | 1.2 mm (3 perimeters) | An exact multiple of the 0.4 mm line width, so there are no gap-fill artifacts. |
| Floor | 1.6 mm (8 layers at 0.2 mm) | Solid bottom with no infill dependence. |
| Layer height | 0.2 mm (0.16 mm for a finer finish) | Standard 0.4 mm nozzle profile. |
| Overhangs | None; all walls are vertical and the bottom is flat | No supports needed. |
| Orientation | As exported (flat on the bed) | |
| Infill | 10 to 15 percent (walls are almost all perimeter) | |
| Multicolour | Assign `Base` and `Walls` to different U1 toolheads | Optional; a single colour works too. |

Weight is roughly 85 g of PLA. Estimate print time in Snapmaker Orca, since I have not sliced it here.
