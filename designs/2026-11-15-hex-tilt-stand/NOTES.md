## Geometry
- Footprint 75 x 80 mm, height 80 mm (fits Snapmaker U1 270 x 270 x 270 mm with huge margin).
- Backrest leans back 18 deg from vertical; phone slot 14 mm wide (fits phones in slim cases; not thick tablet cases).
- Honeycomb: 23 hexagons, 2.4 mm struts (= 6 perimeters at 0.4 mm), cut perpendicular to the slanted face.
- Cable slot 8 x 20 mm through the slot floor; 6 mm wide x 5 mm tall groove open to the underside, exits at the rear.
- Corners R4 on the outer corners only.

## Print settings (Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle)
- Print flat as exported (base on the plate). **No supports needed**: the steepest overhang is the 18 deg backrest lean
  and the groove is open to the bed.
- 0.20 mm layer height, PLA, 3 walls (1.2 mm), 4-5 top/bottom layers, 15 % gyroid infill.
- Struts (2.4 mm) and lip (7 mm) are multiples of the 0.4 mm line width, so no thin-wall gaps.
- Material volume is 87.4 cm^3 solid; actual filament use depends on infill. Check the slicer estimate before pricing.
