# Faceted Hex Succulent Planter + Drip Saucer

Original geometric design (no real-world products, brands or licensed characters).
Files: `faceted-hex-planter.3mf` (print file), `faceted-hex-planter.kerf.json` (editable Kerf source), `preview.png`.

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle settings
- Build volume 270x270x270: plate layout is ~160 x 96 mm, both parts flat on the bed, no supports needed.
- Walls 1.6 mm (4 perimeters), planter floor 1.6 mm (8 layers @ 0.2), saucer floor 2.0 mm, saucer wall 3.2 mm.
- Wall taper ~8 deg from vertical, so no overhangs; drain hole 6 mm (bridging-safe).
- Layer height 0.2 mm, 3 mm-class top layers: 5 top / 8 bottom layers suggested. Infill 10-15%.
- Two parts, two colours (planter sage #6f9a7a, saucer cream #e8dcc4) -> map to two U1 toolheads, or print one colour.
- Material: PLA for dry/decor use; PETG recommended for sold items that will hold soil/moisture.

## Dimensions
- Planter: 83 x 96 mm footprint at the rim, 70 mm tall. Saucer: 77 x 89 x 10 mm.
- Estimated filament: planter ~42 g, saucer ~21 g (solid-volume estimate; actual lower with infill).
