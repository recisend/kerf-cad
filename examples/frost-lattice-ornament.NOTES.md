## Size
99.99 x 90 x 3 mm, about 26 g in the CAD material (about 12 g of PLA at ~1.24 g/cm3; volume 9.7 cm3). It fits the Snapmaker U1 bed (270 x 270 mm) with room for a batch of about 6 per plate.

## Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle settings
- Lay flat as modelled. No supports, no brim needed.
- Layer height 0.20 mm (15 layers). 0.16 mm gives a smoother result.
- Line width 0.42 mm; walls 3 (1.26 mm); top and bottom 4 layers each. At 3 mm thick this prints essentially solid.
- Infill is irrelevant at this thickness; use 15% gyroid if the slicer insists.
- Thinnest feature: branches about 2.1 mm wide, which is 5 lines. Spokes are 3.2 mm wide. Hanging hole is 4 mm.
- Multicolour: the U1's toolheads suit a colour change at layer 2 or 3, or a two-colour ring and spokes.
- Suggested materials: PLA or PLA Silk (white, silver, gold, ice blue).
