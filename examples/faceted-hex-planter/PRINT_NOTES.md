## Model
- Tapered hexagonal planter: 76 x 88 mm footprint (top), 70 mm tall, 6 mm drain hole.
- Matching hex drip saucer: 69 x 80 x 8 mm.
- Both parts laid out on the build plate in one 3MF (about 156 x 80 mm area; Snapmaker U1 bed is 270 x 270 mm).

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle settings
- Layer height 0.2 mm, line width 0.42 mm
- Walls: planter wall ~1.6 mm (4 perimeters), floor 2.0 mm (10 layers); saucer wall ~2.4 mm
- No supports needed (wall slope ~10 deg from vertical), flat bottoms print directly on the bed
- 2 bottom layers beyond the floor are solid; infill is irrelevant (walls are solid)
- Material: PLA / PETG; PETG preferred for a wet planter. Seal or use a liner for live plants.
- Suggested colours: the U1 can print each part in a different filament colour (planter and saucer are separate parts).
- Estimated filament: planter 29.7 cm3 (~37 g PLA), saucer 11.7 cm3 (~14.5 g PLA).
