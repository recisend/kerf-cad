# Facet Planter (original design, 2026-11-09)

Original hexagonal flared succulent planter + matching drip saucer. Not based on any real/branded item.

Files: `facet-planter.3mf` (2 parts, mm), `facet-planter.kerf.json` (editable), `facet-planter.png` (preview).

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle optimisation
- Plate footprint ~ 220 x 94 mm, 70 mm tall: fits the 270 x 270 x 270 mm bed.
- No supports needed: walls flare outward ~8 deg (planter) / ~27 deg (saucer) from vertical, well under the 45 deg overhang limit; flat bottoms.
- Walls 2.4 mm = 6 x 0.4 mm lines; floor 3 mm (planter), 2 mm (saucer); 8 mm drain hole is bridged only by the 3 mm floor.
- Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca, 0.4 mm nozzle): 0.2 mm layers, Arachne walls, 3 walls, 15% gyroid infill (mostly walls anyway), brim optional. Print each part in a different PLA colour (planter #4f9d8a, saucer #e8e2d4 in the 3MF).
- Est. filament: ~58 g planter + ~19 g saucer.
