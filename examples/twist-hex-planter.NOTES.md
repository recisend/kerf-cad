## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle settings
- Size: ~83 x 87 x 90 mm (U1 volume 270^3), prints upright, no supports needed
- Walls 2.0 mm (5 perimeters at 0.4 mm), floor 2.0 mm, 8 mm drain hole
- Max overhang from twist/bulge about 27 deg from vertical (< 45 deg)
- Suggested: 0.2 mm layers, 0.42 mm line width, 3-4 walls, 10% gyroid or 0% infill,
  vase-mode not needed. Use PLA / PLA silk; optional dual-colour via the U1 toolheads.
- Approx. 51 cm^3 of material (~64 g PLA at 1.24 g/cm^3 solid, less with sparse infill)
