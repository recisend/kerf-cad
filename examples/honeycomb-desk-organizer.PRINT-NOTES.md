# Honeycomb Desk Organizer (2026-11-11)

Original design, not based on any real-world product, brand or character.

**Why this one:** research (see sources below) shows desk organizers and personalised gifts as consistently strong Etsy 3D-print categories; honeycomb pen/tool holders are a gift-friendly, repeat-buy item. Treat the trend data as directional (vendor blogs), not verified Etsy sales figures.

## Model
- 7 hex cells, 22 mm flat-to-flat inside, three heights (70 / 55 / 40 mm), overall 70.8 x 68.4 x 70 mm
- Volume ~30.1 cm3 (~37 g PLA solid-equivalent; actual use less with 15% infill walls-only)

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle optimisation
- Walls 1.2 mm = exactly 3 perimeters at 0.4 mm line width; floor 1.6 mm = 8 layers at 0.2 mm
- Zero overhangs, no supports, flat base: prints upright
- Fits well inside the U1 270 x 270 x 270 mm build volume
- Suggested: 0.2 mm layer height, 3 walls, 15% gyroid infill (floor/top only matter), PLA, brim not needed
- Exported as 3MF in mm (`honeycomb-desk-organizer.3mf`); source `.kerf.json` included
