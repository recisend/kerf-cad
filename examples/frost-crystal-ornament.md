# Frost Crystal Ornament (original design, 2026-11-23)

Original six-arm snowflake hanging ornament. Not based on any real product, brand or character.

**Why this one:** it is late November, so holiday ornaments and stocking stuffers are in season on Etsy. Web search found no hard Etsy sales rankings (only seller blogs and listings), so "trending" here means seasonal fit plus listing-based signals, not verified sales data. Check Etsy search volume before listing.

## Model
- Size: 78.2 x 97.5 x 3.2 mm, volume about 6.4 cm3 (about 8 g in PLA)
- Single solid, one part, flat on the bed, no supports, no overhangs
- Hang hole: 4.4 mm, 2.8+ mm wall around it

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle optimization
- Fits the U1 bed (270 x 270 x 270 mm), so a plate of 9 or more fits in one print
- Thickness 3.2 mm = 16 layers at 0.2 mm
- Arm width 4.0 mm and branch width 3.2 mm, both multiples of 0.4 mm, so walls fill cleanly with no gap-fill
- Slicer settings: 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 3 top and 3 bottom layers, 15% infill (barely matters at this thickness), no supports, no brim
- Material: PLA or PLA Silk, or two colours using the U1's multi-tool head
