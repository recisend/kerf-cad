## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle settings
- Parts: planter 85.4 x 85.4 x 85 mm, saucer 92 x 92 x 9 mm. Both sit flat on the bed, well within the U1's 270 mm build volume. Re-arrange the plate in the slicer as needed.
- Layer height 0.20 mm, line width 0.42 mm, 4 wall loops (about 1.7 mm, matching the 2.0 mm modelled wall).
- Top/bottom layers: 5 bottom (1.0 mm), 0 top (open vessel). Infill is not needed; the walls are almost solid.
- No supports. Steepest overhang on the ripples is about 36 deg from vertical, which is under the 45 deg limit.
- PLA about 58 g (planter) + 21 g (saucer), at 1.24 g/cm3.
- Print the saucer separately if you want a different colour. The 3MF contains two separate objects.
- The 3 mm radius drain hole in the planter floor is 6 mm wide, so there is no bridging at the hole.

## Checks done
- Both meshes are watertight (0 non-manifold edges); unit = millimetre.
- Planter outer radius at the base is 34 mm and max 42.7 mm; the saucer inner radius is 44 mm, so the planter fits.
