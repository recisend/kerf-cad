## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle settings
- Size: 125.2 x 120.6 x 100 mm (fits U1 270 mm bed; print flat on the base, no supports needed)
- Nozzle 0.4 mm, layer height 0.20 mm (all heights are multiples of 0.2 mm)
- Outer walls 1.6 mm = 4 perimeters; shared inner walls 2.8 mm
- Floor 2.0 mm = 10 layers (5+ bottom layers, 0% infill needed - walls solid-ish)
- Suggested: 3 wall loops min (4 recommended), 0 sparse infill is fine, 15% gyroid if you prefer, top layers 5
- Material: PLA/PETG; multi-colour via U1 toolheads by painting cells per colour in the slicer
- Estimated model volume ~126.8 cm3 (~157 g PLA at 100% - expect ~100-120 g with slicer settings)
