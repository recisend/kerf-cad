## Snapmaker U1 print settings (0.4 mm nozzle)
- Build volume 270 x 270 x 270 mm. This plate is 174 x 92 mm, tallest part 70 mm.
- Layer height 0.20 mm (all Z dimensions are multiples of 0.2: base 2.0, height 70, saucer 8).
- Walls 1.6 mm shell = 4 perimeters of 0.4 mm; base 2.0 mm = 10 layers; rim 2.0 mm.
- No supports. Saucer wall flares ~26 deg from vertical, under the 45 deg overhang limit.
- Top/bottom 5 layers, infill 10-15% gyroid (walls are mostly perimeters).
- Suggested: PLA, or PETG for wet use. Slicer: Snapmaker Orca. Verify layer-height range in your slicer; sources disagree (0.08-0.24 vs 0.05-0.35 mm).

## Dimensions
- Planter: 76 mm diameter incl. ribs, 70 mm tall, 24 half-round ribs, 8 mm drain hole.
- Saucer: 92 mm diameter, 8 mm tall; fits the planter with clearance.
- ~86 g planter + ~19 g saucer in solid PLA (estimate; the slicer gives the real figure).
