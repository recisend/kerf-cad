# Honeycomb Desk Organizer (original design, 2026-11-24)

Three fused hexagonal cups of different heights (70 / 90 / 110 mm). Original geometry; not based on any real product, brand or character.

## Why this one
Trend research (see sources in the session report): original desk and workshop organizers, home decor and functional items are consistently listed as strong Etsy 3D-print categories; licensed/character items are excluded because they cannot be sold legally.
No Etsy-specific sales ranking was available, so the trend claim is supported by vendor guides and eBay data only, not by confirmed Etsy sales numbers.

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle optimisation
- Footprint 97 x 98 mm, height 110 mm; fits the 270 x 270 x 270 mm build volume with room to spare.
- Print as exported (flat base on bed): no supports, no overhangs, no bridges.
- Outer wall 1.2 mm = 3 perimeters at 0.4 mm; shared walls 2.4 mm = 6 perimeters; floor 2.0 mm = 10 layers at 0.2 mm.
- Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca, U1 0.4 mm preset): 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 3-4 top/bottom layers, 10-15 % gyroid infill (walls are almost solid anyway), PLA.
- Est. volume 63.9 cm3, about 80 g of PLA.
