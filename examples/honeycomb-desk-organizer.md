# Honeycomb Desk Organizer (original design, 2026-11-26)

Seven fused hexagonal cups in three heights (90 / 70 / 50 mm). Pen cup, brush holder, makeup organizer.
Original geometric design. It is not based on any real product, brand or character.

## Why this one
Functional desk/vanity organizers and geometric decor are among the steadier Etsy 3D-print categories
(sources: Snapmaker, Elegoo, hyper3d.ai guides; directional, no Etsy sales data was available).
Etsy also restricts character and licensed designs, so this is deliberately generic.

## Model
- Footprint 114.4 x 110.4 mm, height 90 mm. Fits the Snapmaker U1 bed (270 x 270 x 270) with room for 2 per plate.
- Wall 1.6 mm (4 x 0.4 mm perimeters), floor 2.0 mm (10 layers at 0.2), heights are multiples of 0.2 mm.
- Flat base, vertical walls, no overhangs, so no supports.
- Volume about 85.8 cm3, roughly 106 g of PLA if solid, less with infill.
- Files: `honeycomb-desk-organizer.3mf` (export), `.kerf.json` (editable), `.png` (preview).

## Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle)
- Layer height 0.20 mm, first layer 0.2 mm
- Walls: 4 perimeters, top/bottom 5 layers
- Infill 10-15 % gyroid (walls carry the strength)
- Supports: off. Brim: off. Seam: aligned at a back corner.
- Filament: PLA or PETG. A silk or dual-colour PLA works well for listing photos.
