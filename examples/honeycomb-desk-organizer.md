# Honeycomb Desk Organizer (Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle)

Original geometric design (7 hex cells, 3 heights). Not based on any real item, brand, character or product.

Files: `honeycomb-desk-organizer.3mf` (print file), `honeycomb-desk-organizer.kerf.json` (editable source), `honeycomb-desk-organizer-iso.png` (preview).

## Model
- Footprint 98.8 x 95.4 mm, height 90 mm (fits the U1's 270 mm cube with room to spare)
- Centre cell 90 mm, three cells 65 mm, three cells 45 mm
- Walls 1.6 mm (4 x 0.4 mm lines), shared between neighbouring cells; floors 1.6 mm (8 layers at 0.2 mm)
- Flat base, vertical walls: no supports, no overhangs, no bridging
- All heights/thicknesses are multiples of 0.2 mm layer height

## Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca / Orca-based slicer, U1 profile)
- Nozzle 0.4 mm, layer height 0.20 mm, first layer 0.20 mm
- Walls: 4 perimeters (1.6 mm), top/bottom layers: 5 / 6
- Infill: 10% gyroid (walls are nearly all perimeters, so infill barely matters)
- Supports: off. Brim: off (base is large and flat); add a 3 mm brim if edges lift
- Material: PLA (est. ~60-70 g, roughly 5-7 h; confirm in the slicer). Multicolour option: assign a different filament to each tier by height in the slicer for the U1's multi-tool setup.
- Verify these in your slicer, since I couldn't run it from here.
