## Design for Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle
- Size 105.6 x 101.8 x 100 mm, well inside the 270 mm build volume. Volume 66.7 cm3, about 80 g in PLA at typical settings.
- Walls 1.2 mm = exactly 3 perimeters at 0.4 mm; shared walls between cells are also 1.2 mm.
- Floor 2.0 mm = 10 layers at 0.2 mm.
- Flat base, vertical walls, open top: no supports needed.
- Cell inner width 33.6 mm flat to flat; holds pens, brushes, scissors, markers, small tools.
- 7 cells at stepped heights (40 / 55 / 55 / 70 / 85 / 85 / 100 mm).

## Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca)
0.2 mm layer, 3 walls, 4 top/bottom layers, 10-15% infill (barely used), no supports, optional 3-5 mm brim for the tall corner cell. Slow outer walls for a clean finish. Use a matte or silk PLA; two colours via the toolchanger would be a nice variant (change colour per cell).
