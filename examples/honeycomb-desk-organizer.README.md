## Print settings (Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle)
- File: `honeycomb-desk-organizer.3mf` (mm). Print upright as exported, flat 2 mm floor on the bed.
- Footprint 100.7 x 97.2 mm, height 90 mm (U1 volume 270^3 is fine).
- Layer height 0.20 mm, 4 walls (1.6 mm = 4 x 0.4 mm lines, matches the model wall), 0.4 mm line width.
- Top/bottom layers: 5 (floor is 2 mm = 10 layers at 0.2 mm, more than enough).
- Infill: 10% (walls are almost all perimeters). No supports, no brim needed (use brim 3 mm if the bed is tricky).
- Material: PLA / PLA+ (silk or two-tone looks best), ~78 cm3 of material, roughly 95-100 g.
- Printing the cell walls vertically needs no overhangs: all faces are vertical.
