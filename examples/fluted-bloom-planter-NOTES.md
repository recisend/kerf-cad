# Fluted Bloom Planter — print notes (original design, not based on any real/branded item)

Files: `fluted-bloom-planter.3mf` (print file), `.kerf.json` (editable source), `.png` (preview)

Size: 83.6 x 83.6 x 100 mm, ~59 cm3 of material (~75 g PLA at 15% walls-only-ish; slicer will vary). Fits Snapmaker U1 (270^3) with room for ~9 per plate.

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle optimisation
- Wall 2.4 mm (6 x 0.4 mm lines), flute floor wall 1.2 mm (3 lines); base 2.4 mm = 12 layers @ 0.2 mm.
- Max overhang 33 deg from vertical -> **no supports**, no brim needed.
- Flat 30 mm-radius foot prints directly on the plate.
- Suggested: 0.2 mm layers, Arachne walls, 4 walls, 15% gyroid infill (solid-ish walls anyway), 2-3 top layers (closed only at the base; top is open).
- Single-colour or silk/dual-colour PLA/PETG; mixed nozzle sizes are not supported on the U1, so keep all heads at 0.4 mm.
- Note: 3MF from Kerf contains geometry + colour only; select the Snapmaker U1 profile in Snapmaker Orca and re-sync nozzle info.
- No drain hole: sold as a cachepot / brush & pencil holder. Add a hole in Kerf if you want a true planter.
