# Honeycomb Desk Organizer (2026-11-27)

Original design: seven hexagonal cells in a honeycomb cluster, stepped heights (50/60/70 mm).
Footprint 105 x 109 mm, height 70 mm (fits the Snapmaker U1's 270 mm bed with room for many per plate).
No real-world product, brand or character is depicted.

## Why this one
Desk/drawer organizers are repeatedly named as the most consistent sellers for 3D printed goods
(see Snapmaker "Most Popular 3D Printed Items" blog and other 2026 roundups); they are also
original-design friendly under Etsy's Creativity Standards. These sources are blogs/vendors, not
verified sales data, so treat the trend signal as directional.

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle optimization
- Walls: 1.6 mm shared cell walls (4 x 0.4 mm lines, no gap-fill needed).
- Floor: 2.0 mm (10 layers at 0.2 mm). All heights are multiples of 0.2 mm.
- No overhangs, no supports, flat bottom: print upright.
- Suggested: 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 15% gyroid (or 2 top/bottom layers' worth; walls carry most
  of the strength), PLA or PETG. ~54 cm^3 solid volume (~67 g PLA).
- Idea for sale variants (U1 multi-colour): print each ring cell in a different colour.
