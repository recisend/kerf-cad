## Geometry
- Overall 147 × 84.8 × 95 mm (fits the Snapmaker U1's 270 mm bed with wide margin).
- Large cup: ⌀≈85 mm outer, 95 mm tall. Small cup: ⌀≈61 mm outer, 65 mm tall.
- Walls: 2.4 mm everywhere (= 6 perimeters at 0.4 mm line width). Floor 3.0 mm (15 layers at 0.2 mm).
- Flute valleys are the thinnest outer point, and the wall is still 2.4 mm there.
- Joined by a 9 × 12 × 43 mm web, with a 1 mm gap between the cups' rib peaks.
- Prints upright on the flat base. No supports needed. The web underside bridges a ~6 mm span, which is fine at 0.2 mm layers.

## Print settings (Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle)
- Nozzle 0.4 mm, layer height 0.2 mm (0.16 mm for a smoother finish), line width 0.42 mm.
- Walls: 6 loops, or 5 if the slicer rounds line width up. Top/bottom layers: 5 bottom. Infill: 0–10 % (the part is almost all wall).
- Material: PLA (or PETG for a durable desk piece). Silk or matte PLA looks best for fluted shapes and sells well in photos.
- Seam: aligned/rear, or set the seam to "random" to hide it in the valleys.
- Estimated filament: ≈ 172 cm³ → about 213 g PLA (at 1.24 g/cm³). Lowering walls to 1.6 mm would save about a third of the material; the design variables are in the Kerf file.
- Confirm U1 build volume and your slicer profile before the first print.
