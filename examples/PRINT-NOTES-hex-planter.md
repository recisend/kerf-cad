# Hex Planter + Saucer — print notes (Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle)

Original geometric design, not based on any real product, brand or character.
File: `hex-planter-set.3mf` (2 parts: planter 90 x 78 x 65 mm, saucer 96 x 83 x 10 mm; fits the 270 mm bed). Source: `hex-planter-set.kerf.json`.

Design-for-print choices
- Walls 1.6 mm = 4 perimeters of 0.4 mm; floors 4.0 / 2.0 mm (multiples of 0.2 mm layers).
- No overhangs, no supports. Both parts print flat-side-down as exported.
- 0.8 mm bottom chamfer to hide elephant foot. 8 mm drain hole (bridges fine).
- Saucer has about 0.9 mm clearance per side around the planter base.

Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca): 0.2 mm layer, 4 walls, 15% gyroid or 2-3 top/bottom layers, PLA or PETG,
vase-friendly. Planter ~58 g, saucer ~19 g (solid-wall estimate, less with sparse infill).
Etsy tip: sell in several colours; trending categories per web research: home decor/planters, desk organisers, fidget toys.
