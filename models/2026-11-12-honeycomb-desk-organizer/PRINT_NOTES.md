# Honeycomb Desk Organizer (2026-11-12)

Original design, no real-world product, brand or character references.
A three-cell hexagonal pen / tool / brush holder with stepped heights (70 / 95 / 120 mm).

Size: 88.4 x 89.5 x 120 mm. Fits the Snapmaker U1 (270 x 270 x 270 mm) with lots of room.
Orientation: print upright as exported, flat floor on the bed. No supports needed.

## Optimized for Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle
- Wall 1.6 mm = exactly 4 perimeters of 0.4 mm lines. Shared walls between cells are a single 1.6 mm wall.
- Floor 2.0 mm = 10 layers at 0.2 mm.
- All walls vertical, floor is flat: zero overhangs, no supports, no bridging.
- Suggested: 0.2 mm layer height, 4 walls, 3 top / 10 bottom layers, 10-15% infill (walls carry the part), PLA/PETG.
- Multi-color idea: the U1 multi-toolhead lets you do a gradient or a different color per cell by splitting parts in the slicer.
