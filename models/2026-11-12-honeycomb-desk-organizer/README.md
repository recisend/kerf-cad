# Honeycomb Desk Organizer (original design, 2026-11-12)

Original geometric design — not based on any real product, brand, character or licensed item.
Status: DRAFT. Nothing has been published or listed anywhere.

## Model
- 7 interlocking hexagonal cells (1 centre + 6 around), heights 50 / 60 / 70 / 90 mm for pens, brushes, scissors, tools.
- Footprint 120 x 115.5 mm, height 90 mm (Snapmaker U1 bed: 270 x 270 x 270 mm).
- Volume ~93.9 cm3 (~116 g PLA at 100% density; far less with 15% infill-free thin walls).
- Walls: 1.2 mm outer (3 x 0.4 mm lines), 2.4 mm between cells (6 lines), 2.0 mm floor.
- 0.6 mm bottom chamfer to avoid elephant's foot. Flat on bed, no overhangs, no supports.

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle settings (Snapmaker Orca)
- Nozzle 0.4 mm, layer height 0.20 mm (first layer 0.2), line width 0.42 mm
- Walls 3 (outer), top/bottom layers 5/5 (floor is 2 mm = 10 layers; use 10 bottom layers)
- Infill 10-15% gyroid (walls are nearly all perimeters), no supports, brim not needed
- PLA 215 C nozzle / 60 C bed. Colours: multi-colour via the U1's 4 toolheads (e.g. paint each cell a different colour from the slicer).
