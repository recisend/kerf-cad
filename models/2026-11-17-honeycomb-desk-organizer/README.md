# Honeycomb Desk Organizer (2026-11-17)

Original, generic design (not based on any real product or brand). Three fused hexagonal cells of different heights (100 / 70 / 45 mm) for pens, brushes, scissors and small supplies.

## Why this design
Desk organizers / modular organizers and home-office gifts are repeatedly listed as steady Etsy 3D-print sellers in 2026 seller guides. These sources are vendor blogs, not verified sales data, so treat "trending" as directional. Etsy requires original designs for 3D-printed items, which is why this is an original model.

## Printer fit: Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle
- Footprint 120 x 121.2 mm, height 100 mm (U1 build volume 270 mm cube).
- Wall thickness 1.6 mm outer (4 perimeters at 0.4 mm), 3.2 mm shared wall between cells (8 lines).
- 2.0 mm floor (10 layers at 0.2 mm; use 0.2 mm layer height).
- 0.6 mm chamfer on the bottom edge to reduce elephant foot.
- No overhangs, no supports. Print upright, flat on the bed, no brim needed.
- Suggested: 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 3-4 top/bottom layers, 10-15% infill, PLA.
- Estimated material at 100% fill is about 107 g; real print will use less.
