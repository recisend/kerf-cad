# Frost Lattice Snowflake Ornament

Original, abstract six-fold geometric design. It is not based on any real object, brand or licensed character.

- Size: 77.6 x 91.5 mm, 3.0 mm thick, with a 1.2 mm raised hex accent in the centre. The accent is a separate part so it can be printed in a second colour.
- Printer: Snapmaker U1 (270 x 270 x 270 mm build volume, multi-tool), 0.4 mm nozzle.
- Format: 3MF (`frost-lattice-snowflake-ornament.3mf`), two parts: "Part 1" body, "Part 4" accent.

## 0.4 mm nozzle design rules applied
- Minimum feature width is 3.5 mm (about 8 line widths), so there are no thin-wall or gap-fill issues.
- Hang hole is 3.4 mm diameter, with a 2.8 mm wall around it.
- Everything is flat on the bed, so no supports are needed. The only overhang is the accent sitting on the body.

## Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca)
- Nozzle 0.4 mm, layer height 0.20 mm (accent starts at layer 16, z = 3.0 mm)
- 3 walls, 4 top and 4 bottom layers, 100% infill (the part is too thin for sparse infill to matter)
- No supports, no brim (a 3 mm brim is optional for the thin arm tips)
- PLA: nozzle ~215 C, bed 60 C. Roughly 17.4 g, about 35-45 min.
- Colours: body white or ice blue, accent silver or gold.
