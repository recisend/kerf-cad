# Hex Honeycomb Desk Caddy (2026-11-12)

Original design: three fused hexagonal cups (95 / 75 / 55 mm tall) in a honeycomb cluster.
Fits pens, brushes, markers, small tools. Not based on any real product, brand or character.

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle optimisation
- Size 102.3 x 103.6 x 95 mm, well inside the U1 270 mm build volume.
- Wall 1.6 mm = exactly 4 perimeters at 0.4 mm line width; shared walls are 1.6 mm too.
- Floor 2.4 mm = 12 layers at 0.2 mm.
- Flat bottom, vertical walls, open top: **no supports needed**.
- Volume 68.8 cm3, about 85 g of PLA at the default 1.24 g/cm3.
- Suggested slicer settings: 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layer height, 4 walls, 15% gradient/gyroid infill
  (mostly walls anyway), no supports, brim optional. Use a different color per cup via the slicer
  if you want a multi-color U1 print.
