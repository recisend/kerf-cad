# Frost Lantern (hex, LED tealight) — original design

Seasonal home-decor piece (winter/Christmas gifting window). Original geometry, not based on any real product, brand or character.

- Size: 69.3 x 80 x 80 mm (flat-to-flat x corner-to-corner x height), open top, 1.6 mm walls (4 perimeters at 0.4 mm), 1.6 mm floor.
- Fits any standard LED tealight (up to ~65 mm wide inside). LED only — no real flames.
- Window diamonds are ~59 deg from horizontal at the lower edges, so no supports are needed.

## Print settings (Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle)
- Orientation: as exported (open top up, flat on the bed). No supports, no brim.
- Layer height 0.20 mm, line width 0.42 mm, 4 walls, 4 top/bottom layers, infill 0-10 % (walls are solid).
- Material: PLA (matte white / silk / translucent for glow). ~12-14 g, roughly 1 h 15 min.
- Single part, fits one plate; can print 4-6 per plate (270 x 270 mm bed) for batch sales.
