# Honeycomb Desk Organizer

Original design (not based on any real product, brand or character). Seven hexagonal cells of graduated height (45–105 mm) share single walls, for pens, brushes, scissors, tools and small clips.

Files: `honeycomb-desk-organizer.3mf` (print file, mm), `honeycomb-desk-organizer.kerf.json` (editable Kerf source), `preview.png`.

## Geometry (checked)
- Footprint 130.3 × 125.8 mm, height 105 mm. Fits the Snapmaker U1's 270 × 270 × 270 mm volume with a wide margin.
- Prints flat on the bed in its natural orientation: no supports, no overhangs, no bridges.
- Walls 2.4 mm (6 × 0.4 mm lines), floor 3.0 mm (15 layers at 0.2 mm). Every dimension is a multiple of the 0.4 mm nozzle and 0.2 mm layer height.
- Closed manifold mesh (V − E + F = 2), single part, ~172 cm³ solid.

## Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca, U1, 0.4 mm nozzle)
| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Layer height | 0.20 mm |
| Walls | 3 (outer wall + 2) |
| Top / bottom layers | 4 / 4 |
| Infill | 10 % gyroid (walls carry the load) |
| Supports | Off |
| Brim | Optional, 5 mm if the bed is not well adhered |
| Seam | Aligned or back, rear-facing corner |
| Material | PLA, PLA+ or PETG; use a silk or two-tone filament for the best listing photos |

These settings are my recommendations, not a Snapmaker-provided profile. Check them against the Snapmaker Orca U1 preset and print one test piece before selling.
