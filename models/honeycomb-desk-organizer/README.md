## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle settings
- Size: 81.1 x 116.5 x 95 mm (U1 bed is 270 x 270 x 270 mm, so it fits with margin).
- Orientation: flat on the bed as exported. No supports needed (no overhangs; bottom edge has a 0.6 mm chamfer against elephant foot).
- Walls: 2.0 mm = 5 perimeters at 0.4 mm lines; floor 2.4 mm = 12 layers at 0.2 mm.
- Suggested: 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layer height, 3 to 5 walls, 15% infill (barely any infill, since walls are nearly solid), 4 top/bottom layers. PLA or PETG.
- Estimated volume 73.5 cm3, about 91 g of PLA (about 12 to 14 h; check in Snapmaker Orca).
- Multi-colour (U1 has 4 toolheads): assign a different filament per cell in the slicer, or switch colour by height.
- Profile settings are my recommendations from the printer/nozzle you specified; I could not test-slice here, so slice once before selling.
