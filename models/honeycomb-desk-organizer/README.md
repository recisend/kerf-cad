# Honeycomb Desk Organizer

Original parametric design (not based on any real product). 7 fused hexagonal cells, stepped heights (100 / 78 / 58 mm), for pens, brushes, tools.

- Size: 110.8 x 106.9 x 100 mm. Volume ≈ 90.9 cm³ (≈ 113 g PLA at 100% density; far less with 15% infill).
- Fits the Snapmaker U1 build volume (270 x 270 x 270 mm) with large margin.
- Files: `honeycomb-desk-organizer.3mf` (print), `honeycomb-desk-organizer.kerf.json` (editable source), `preview.png`.

## Optimized for Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle
- Walls are 1.6 mm = 4 perimeters at 0.4 mm line width. Floor is 2.0 mm = 10 layers at 0.2 mm.
- Flat bottom, vertical walls, open top: **no supports, no brim needed**. Print upright.
- Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca): 0.2 mm layer, 4 walls, 3-4 top/bottom layers, 10-15% infill (walls carry the strength), PLA ~220 °C / bed 60 °C. Optional: Arachne walls, ironing off.
- Multi-colour idea for the U1 toolchanger: paint different cells in slicer for a colour-blocked look, no model change needed.
