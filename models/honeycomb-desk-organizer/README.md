# Honeycomb Desk Organizer

Original design (not a copy of any real product, brand or character). Four hexagonal cups of stepped height (100 / 80 / 60 / 40 mm) that share walls like a honeycomb: pens, brushes, tools, cables.

- Size: 117.6 x 95.6 x 100 mm (fits the Snapmaker U1 270 mm build volume with room to spare)
- Volume 73.5 cm3, about 91 g of PLA (before infill/wall adjustments)
- Files: `honeycomb-desk-organizer.3mf` (print file), `honeycomb-desk-organizer.kerf.json` (editable source), `preview.png`

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle optimisation

- Wall thickness 1.6 mm = exactly 4 perimeters of 0.4 mm lines. Shared walls between cups are also 1.6 mm.
- Floor 2.0 mm (10 layers at 0.2 mm).
- All walls vertical, flat base: no supports, no overhangs, no bridging.
- Printed upright, no rotation. Suggested: 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 15% gyroid or 2 top / 4 bottom layers, no supports, brim not needed.
- Single body, so any single filament works; on the U1 a colour change at a layer height (for example per cup height) gives a two-tone look. Colour in the 3MF is amber `#e8a33d` as a placeholder.
