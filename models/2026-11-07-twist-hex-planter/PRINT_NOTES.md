# Twist-Hex Planter + Saucer (original design)

## Printer: Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle
- Plate: planter (75 x 79 x 75 mm) and saucer (80 x 92 x 10 mm) fit on the 270 x 270 mm bed with room to spare.
- Supports: none needed. Max overhang is about 14 degrees from vertical, flat 2.0 mm floor on the plate.
- Walls: 1.6 mm planter wall (4 x 0.4 mm lines); saucer wall about 1.65 mm and floor 2.0 mm. Use 0.2 mm layers.
- Suggested slicer settings (starting point, calibrate on your machine): 3-4 walls, 15% infill (or 3 walls, 2 top/bottom layers is enough here), Arachne wall generator. Vase-mode style spiral is not used: the part has a drain hole.
- Drain hole: 6 mm. Saucer pocket fits the planter base with about 10 mm clearance.
- Print time/filament estimate: about 53 g PLA (34.7 g planter + 18 g saucer, solid-model mass before infill/walls differences).
