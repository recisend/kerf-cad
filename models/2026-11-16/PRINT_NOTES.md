- Floor 2.0 mm (10 layers at 0.2 mm).
- Flat bottom, vertical walls, no overhangs: **no supports needed**.
- No features smaller than 1.2 mm.
- Three cups: 100 / 75 / 55 mm tall (pens, brushes, small tools).
- Estimated volume 54.6 cm3, about 68 g PLA.

- Seam: aligned/back, brim not needed. Print upright.
