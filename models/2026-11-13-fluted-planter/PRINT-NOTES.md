# Fluted Planter + Saucer (2026-11-13)

Original design, not based on any real product or brand. Not published anywhere; listing is your call.

**Why this model:** planters/vases and novelty desk decor are listed as steady Etsy sellers, while generic phone stands and flexi dragons are saturated (sources are SEO blog posts, so treat as directional, not proof).

**Files:** `fluted-planter.3mf` (2 parts: planter, saucer), `fluted-planter.kerf.json` (editable in Kerf).

## Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle
- Plate footprint ~218 x 108 mm, height 95 mm (U1 bed is 270 x 270 x 270 mm). Both parts fit on one plate.
- Walls 1.6 mm = 4 perimeters at 0.4 mm. Floor 2.4 mm = 12 layers at 0.2 mm.
- 20 vertical ribs (7.2 mm wide), no overhangs, no supports needed. Drainage hole 8 mm.
- Suggested: 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 15% gyroid infill (or 0 top layers; planter is open), PLA/PETG.
- Planter ~95 cm3, saucer ~27 cm3 of solid volume.
- Sizing note: slicer profile selection (Snapmaker Orca/U1 0.4 profile) must be done in the slicer; the 3MF carries geometry only.
