## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle optimisation
- Walls are 2.0 mm (5 perimeters at 0.4 mm lines). Floor is 2.4 mm. Saucer walls and floor are 2.0 mm.
- No overhangs and no supports: every surface is vertical or flat on the bed. Ribs are vertical cylinders, so they print cleanly.
- Flat bottoms for bed adhesion. Drain hole is 8 mm diameter, vertical.
- Planter ø90 x 70 mm, about 118 g PLA. Saucer ø104 x 10 mm, about 27 g PLA. Planter fits inside the saucer rim with about 5 mm clearance.
- Suggested slicer settings: 0.2 mm layer height, 0.42 mm line width, 5 walls, 15% gyroid infill (mostly walls), no supports, PLA.
- The 3MF was exported by Kerf as a plain mesh with part colours. I did not slice it, so run it through the Snapmaker slicer and check the preview before selling.
