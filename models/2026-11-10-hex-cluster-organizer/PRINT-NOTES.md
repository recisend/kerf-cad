# Hex Cluster Desk Organizer (original design)

Four hollow hexagonal cups fused into a honeycomb cluster (heights 90 / 70 / 55 / 40 mm).
Generic geometric design, not based on any real product, brand or character.

- Size: 85 x 122 x 90 mm (fits the Snapmaker U1's 270 x 270 x 270 mm bed with room to spare)
- Walls 1.6 mm (4 perimeters at 0.4 mm), floor 2.0 mm (10 layers at 0.2 mm), heights are multiples of 0.2 mm
- No overhangs, no supports, flat base: prints upright as exported
- Volume ~59.7 cm3 solid material (~75 g PLA at 100% density equivalent; real use is lower with these thin walls)

## Suggested U1 slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca)
- Nozzle 0.4 mm, layer height 0.2 mm, line width 0.42 mm
- Walls: 4 (outer wall 0.42), top/bottom layers: 5/5, infill 10% gyroid (walls are nearly solid anyway)
- Supports: off, brim: off (add a 3 mm brim if bed adhesion is an issue)
- PLA, 220 C nozzle / 60 C bed (adjust per filament)
- Tip: print in a two-colour or silk PLA for a premium look on listing photos
