# Hex Honeycomb Desk Organizer (2026-11-15)

Original generic design (no brand, character or real-product reference): four joined hexagonal cups of
different heights (80 / 70 / 60 / 50 mm) for pens, brushes, tools or makeup brushes.

Files: `hex-honeycomb-organizer.3mf` (print file), `hex-honeycomb-organizer.kerf.json` (editable Kerf source), previews.

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle optimisation
- Footprint 92.9 x 75.6 x 80 mm, well inside the U1's 270 mm cube build volume.
- Walls 1.6 mm = exactly 4 perimeters at 0.4 mm line width; shared wall between cups also 1.6 mm.
- Floor 2.0 mm = 10 layers at 0.2 mm; all heights are multiples of 0.2 mm.
- No overhangs, bridges or supports; flat base, print upright.
- Suggested: 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 2-3 % infill is irrelevant (walls only), 10 bottom layers.
  Multicolour option on the U1: change filament per cup or by layer height.
