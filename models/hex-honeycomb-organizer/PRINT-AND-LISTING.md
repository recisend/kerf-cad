## Model facts
- Footprint 98.0 x 99.3 mm, height 80 mm. Fits the Snapmaker U1 build volume (270 x 270 x 270 mm) with room to spare.
- Wall 2.0 mm (5 perimeters at 0.4 mm), floor 3.0 mm, shared walls 2.0 mm.
- Volume 71.8 cm3 (about 89 g of PLA at 100% density; real usage is lower with the settings below).
- Flat base and vertical walls only, so there are no overhangs and no supports.

## Snapmaker U1 slicer settings (0.4 mm nozzle)
- Nozzle 0.4 mm, layer height 0.20 mm, line width about 0.42 mm.
- Walls: 5 perimeters. Top and bottom layers: 4 / 15 (3.0 mm floor). Infill: 10% gyroid. The part is mostly walls.
- Supports: off. Brim: off. If the corners lift, add a 3 mm brim.
- Orientation: as exported (flat on the bed, opening up).
- Filament: PLA, nozzle about 215 C, bed about 60 C. Check your filament's own recommended range.
- Colour (optional): the U1 has 4 toolheads. A colour change at layer heights 50 mm and 65 mm gives a tiered look.
