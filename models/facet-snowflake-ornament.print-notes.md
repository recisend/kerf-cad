# Facet Snowflake Ornament — print notes & listing draft (DRAFT — NOT PUBLISHED)

Original geometric design (6-fold snowflake, not a copy of any real product, brand or character).
Season-timed for Q4 holiday ornament demand.

## Printer profile: Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle
- File: `facet-snowflake-ornament.3mf` (mm, single part, 70.9 x 83.0 x 4.2 mm)
- Orientation: flat on bed as exported, no supports, no brim needed
- Layer height 0.20 mm (3.0 mm base = 15 layers, 1.2 mm hub detail = 6 layers)
- Walls 3 (1.2 mm) — thinnest feature is a ~3.2 mm branch, hang-loop wall is 3 mm
- Top/bottom layers 4, infill 15 % gyroid (part is mostly solid anyway)
- PLA nozzle ~215 °C / bed ~60 °C; ~7 g, roughly 20-25 min
- Plate capacity: ~12 per 270 x 270 mm plate
- Colour ideas for the U1's multi-tool setup: white/silver base with a contrasting
  hub colour via a colour change at layer 16 (z = 3.0 mm) — hub detail is its own 1.2 mm step.
