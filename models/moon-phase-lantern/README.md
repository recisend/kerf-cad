- Wall 1.6 mm (= 4 perimeters at 0.4 mm), floor 2.0 mm, open top

- No supports needed: window ceilings are circular arcs (small bridges), nothing exceeds a safe overhang

- Profile: Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layer ("Standard")
- Orientation: as exported, open end up, flat floor on the plate
- Walls: 4 (line width ~0.42 mm); infill: 10-15 % (only affects the 2 mm floor); bottom layers 5; top layers n/a (open top)
- Supports: off. Brim: optional (large flat base, usually not needed)
- Cooling: part fan 100 % on bridges; PLA about 215 C nozzle / 60 C bed (use the filament's own recommendation)
- Seam: aligned/back so the vertical seam is on a hexagon corner
- Filament: PLA or PLA+ (matte or silk both look good). Single colour is fine; on the U1 you can swap a second colour in at the floor height for a two-tone look.
- Estimated filament: about 45-50 g (slicer figure will differ; check before pricing)

## Safety (put this in the listing)
Use **LED tealights only**. PLA softens at around 55-60 C, so no real flame.
