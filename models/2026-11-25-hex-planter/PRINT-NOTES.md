## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle settings
- Nozzle 0.4 mm, layer height 0.20 mm (0.16 mm for a smoother finish).
- Wall loops: 3 for the planter (about 1.2-1.3 mm each side of the 2.4 mm wall), top/bottom layers 5.
- Infill 10-15% gyroid (walls do the work). No supports, no brim. Both parts print flat on the bed.
- Walls flare outward at about 8 degrees from vertical, so no overhang needs support.
- Drain hole is 8 mm (floor 3.0 mm), saucer wall 2.8 mm, floor 2.4 mm.
- Print size: planter 72.7 x 84 x 70 mm, saucer 62.9 x 72.6 x 10 mm. Both fit one plate; the U1 bed is 270 x 270 mm.
- Material: PLA or PETG. Use a standard (non-vase) profile. Colours: two-tone (planter and saucer in different filaments) sells well.
- Saucer fits the planter base with about 1.3-1.5 mm clearance per side.
