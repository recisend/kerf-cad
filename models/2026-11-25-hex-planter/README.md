# Faceted Hex Planter + Drip Tray (original design)

Original geometric design. Not based on any real product, brand or character.

## Files
- `hex-planter-snapmaker-u1.3mf` – 2 parts on one plate (Hex Planter, Drip Tray), mm
- `hex-planter.kerf.json` – editable Kerf CAD source

## Printer optimisation (Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle)
- Plate use: ~191 x 96 mm of the 270 x 270 mm bed
- Walls 1.6 mm = exactly 4 perimeters at 0.4 mm line width
- Planter sides lean outward only ~5 deg from vertical: no supports, no overhang trouble
- Flat bases, so no supports or brim needed. Drain hole 6 mm, printed vertical
- Planter ~54 g, tray ~15 g of PLA (about 70 g total)

## Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca)
0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layer, 4 walls, 3 top / 3 bottom layers, 10-15% infill, supports off.
Optional: vase/spiral mode is NOT recommended since there is a drain hole and a floor.
PLA is fine for dry use. Use PETG if the planter will hold wet soil long-term.
