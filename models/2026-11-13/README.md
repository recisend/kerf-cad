# Facet Hex Planter + Saucer (2026-11-13)

Original design, not based on any real product, brand or character.

- Files: `facet-hex-planter.3mf` (2 parts: Planter, Saucer), `.kerf.json` (editable), `.png` (preview)
- Size: planter 80 x 92 x 80 mm, saucer 66 x 76 x 10 mm. Both sit side by side on the plate, well within the Snapmaker U1 270 mm bed.
- Designed for a 0.4 mm nozzle: 1.6 mm walls (4 perimeters at 0.4 mm), 1.6 mm floor, 8 mm drain hole, no overhangs and no supports. The planter prints open end up.
- Suggested settings: 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 15% gyroid infill (or 0 for vase-like walls), PLA/PETG, no supports.
- Est. weight: planter ~34 g, saucer ~9 g at PLA density (the 90 g/24 g shown by Kerf assumes aluminum).
