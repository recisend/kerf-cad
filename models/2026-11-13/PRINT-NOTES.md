# Hex Hive Desk Caddy (2026-11-13)

Original design: three interlocking hexagonal cups of different heights (75 / 55 / 95 mm) forming a honeycomb desk organizer for pens, brushes, tools. Not based on any real or branded item.

Files: `hex-hive-desk-caddy.3mf` (print file), `hex-hive-desk-caddy.kerf.json` (editable Kerf source), `hex-hive-desk-caddy.png` (preview).

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle settings
- Footprint ~76 x 77 mm, height 95 mm (well inside the U1 build volume). Print upright, flat floor on the bed, **no supports needed**.
- Walls 1.6 mm = 4 perimeters at 0.4 mm line width (3.2 mm shared wall between cells). Floor 2.0 mm = 10 layers at 0.2 mm.
- Suggested: 0.2 mm layers, 3-4 walls, 15% gyroid infill (mostly walls anyway), 4 top/bottom layers, brim optional.
- Volume ~51.9 cm3, about 65 g of PLA. Use PLA/PETG; silk or matte PLA photographs well for listings.
- Dimensions are driven by Kerf geometry (hex circumradius 22 mm outer, 20.15 mm inner).
