## Why this design
Research this run, from vendor blogs and live Etsy listings, found no hard sales counts. The signals were consistent:
- Modern fluted/ribbed desk organizers and pen holders are the most visible Etsy 3D-print desk category, at roughly $12-49.
- Desk and stocking-stuffer gifts are a recurring angle, which suits the December gift season.
- Geometric/parametric home decor is a stated trend.

Treat this as an informed bet, not a proven bestseller. Check Etsy's "Best selling" filter before committing to a big batch.

## Printer fit: Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle
| Item | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Size | 76 x 76 x 95 mm | Well inside the 270 mm build volume |
| Wall at flute valleys | 1.6 mm | 4 perimeters at 0.4 mm |
| Floor | 2.4 mm | 12 layers at 0.2 mm; first layer is a flat full-footprint base |
| Overhangs | None | All walls vertical, so no supports and no brim needed |
| Flute shape | Smooth sinusoid, +/-2 mm | Smooth curvature, no sharp cusps |
| Volume | 82.3 cm3 | About 100 g PLA, mostly walls |

## Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca, U1 0.4 mm profile)
- Layer height 0.2 mm, 4 walls, 5 bottom layers, 4 top layers, infill 10%
- Supports off, brim off, orientation as exported (flat base down)
- Vase / spiral mode is not suitable, because the closed floor is needed
- PLA suggested. Silk or two-tone PLA shows the flutes best.
- Single-colour as exported. Colour-change ideas for the U1's tool changer: a contrasting top 3 mm band.
