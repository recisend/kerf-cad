## Specs
- 7 hex cells, staggered heights 100 / 80 / 64 mm; footprint 114.4 x 110.4 mm, 100 mm tall (fits U1 270^3 bed)
- Walls 1.6 mm (= 4 x 0.4 mm lines), floor 2.4 mm (12 x 0.2 mm layers), shared 1.6 mm cell walls
- All vertical walls, flat base: **no supports, no bridges, no overhangs**
- ~100 cm3 solid volume, est. ~125 g PLA

## Snapmaker U1 slicing (Orca-based Snapmaker Orca, 0.4 mm nozzle)
- Layer 0.20 mm, 4 walls, top/bottom 4 layers, infill 10-15% (walls are nearly solid)
- Print as-is (flat side down), no supports, brim optional
- Single colour as exported (teal PLA); U1 can do a colour per cell / ring via painting in the slicer
