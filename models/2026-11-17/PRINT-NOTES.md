## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle settings
- Size 103.9 × 100 × 75 mm (fits 270³ mm bed easily); ~82 cm³ solid, ~221 g as aluminium placeholder material — real PLA use roughly 100 g at 15% infill, mostly walls.
- Print upright, no supports needed (all walls vertical, flat base, open top).
- Layer height 0.2 mm, line width 0.42–0.45 mm, 3 perimeters is enough for the 1.6 mm outer wall (4 × 0.4); the 3.2 mm shared walls = 8 lines.
- Floor 2.0 mm (10 layers @ 0.2), bottom edge chamfer 0.6 mm for elephant-foot relief.
- Infill 10–15% (walls dominate). Brim not needed (large flat base).
- Colour idea: multi-colour is possible on U1 (4 toolheads) — e.g. a different filament per cell height via a colour change at layer heights, or a gradient silk PLA.
- All features ≥ 1.6 mm, so nothing falls below the 0.4 mm nozzle's reliable resolution.
