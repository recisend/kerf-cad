# Facet Twist Planter + Saucer (2026-11-17)

Original geometric design. It is not based on any real-world item, brand or character.

## Files
- `facet-twist-planter.3mf` - print file (2 parts: Planter, Saucer), mm
- `facet-twist-planter.kerf.json` - editable Kerf source (variable `#wall`)
- `facet-twist-planter.png` - preview render

## Dimensions
- Planter: 80 x 69.3 x 70 mm, hex base R32 twisting 30 deg to hex top R40, 6 mm drain hole, 74 g solid-metal estimate (not filament weight)
- Saucer: 65.8 x 76 x 10 mm, 1.6 mm walls, planter base fits inside
- Both parts together span about 173 x 76 mm, well inside the Snapmaker U1's 270 x 270 x 270 mm volume

## U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle optimisation
- Walls and floor are 1.6 mm = 4 x 0.4 mm perimeters (saucer wall 1.645 mm)
- No supports needed: flare is about 6.5 deg from vertical, flat bottoms
- Suggested slicer settings: 0.2 mm layers, 0.4 mm line width, 4 walls, 3 top / 3 bottom layers, 10-15% infill (walls are nearly all perimeter), vase-friendly PLA or PETG
- Import the 3MF geometry into Snapmaker Orca and choose the U1 0.4 mm profile. Check the printer settings before printing.
