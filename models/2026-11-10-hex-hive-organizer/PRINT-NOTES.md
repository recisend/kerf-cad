## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle optimisation
- Size 114.3 x 110 x 90 mm (build volume 270^3): fits with room to batch 2-4 per plate.
- Wall design built on 0.4 mm line multiples: outer walls 1.6 mm (4 lines), shared walls 3.2 mm (8 lines), floor 3.0 mm.
- No overhangs, no bridges, no supports needed; flat bottom with 0.6 mm chamfer vs elephant foot.
- Suggested slicer (Snapmaker Orca): 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 15% gyroid or 2 walls + 0 infill is fine
  because walls are nearly solid, PLA/PETG. Multicolour: use paint/colour-change by height on the 4-toolhead U1.
- Volume ~103 cm^3 => roughly 125-130 g PLA.
