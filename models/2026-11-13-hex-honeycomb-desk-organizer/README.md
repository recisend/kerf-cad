# Hex Honeycomb Desk Organizer (2026-11-13)

Original design, not based on any real product, brand or character.

- File to slice: `hex-honeycomb-desk-organizer.3mf` (mm, one part, Z-up)
- Editable source: `hex-honeycomb-desk-organizer.kerf.json`
- Size: 110.9 x 160 x 100 mm (fits the Snapmaker U1's 270 x 270 x 270 mm bed)
- Four fused hex cups of different heights: 100, 80, 60 and 40 mm (pens, brushes, scissors, small tools)

## Why it suits the Snapmaker U1 with a 0.4 mm nozzle
- Outer walls are 1.6 mm (4 x 0.4 mm line width); walls shared between cups are 3.2 mm (8 lines). No thin-wall gap-fill.
- Floor is 2.0 mm (10 layers at 0.2 mm; 25 layers at 0.08 mm).
- All walls are vertical and the base is flat: no supports, no brim needed.
- 0.6 mm chamfer on the bottom edge to reduce elephant's foot.
- Fits the bed with lots of room, so you can print 2 at a time (about 250 x 111 mm side by side, check collisions with the toolhead rails).

## Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca, U1 0.4 mm profile)
- Layer height 0.20 mm (0.16 mm for a finer finish), PLA
- Walls: 3 loops (Arachne), top layers 0, bottom layers 5 (it is open-topped)
- Infill: 10% (almost all of the part is walls); seam: aligned/back
- No supports, no brim
- Multicolor option: the U1 has 4 toolheads. A layer-change color swap (e.g. at 40, 60, 80 mm) makes a nice "stepped gradient" look for the listing photos.
- These settings are suggestions. Check the printed result on your own machine and filament before listing, since I could not test-print here.
