Original abstract design, not based on any real product, brand or character.

**Trend basis:** planters/vases and personalised desk & home items are repeatedly listed as top 2026 Etsy 3D-print categories (sources: eufymake, hyper3d, insightagent, printpal guides). Those are seller-blog claims, not verified Etsy data. Check Etsy search autocomplete before listing.

## Files
- `faceted-hex-planter.3mf` - 2 objects (Planter teal, Saucer cream), mm units, both flat on the bed, ~158 x 84 mm footprint (fits U1 270 x 270 x 270 mm).
- `faceted-hex-planter.kerf.json` - editable source (open in Kerf).

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle settings
- Nozzle 0.4 mm, layer 0.20 mm, line width 0.42-0.45 mm.
- Walls: 3 perimeters (1.2-1.35 mm), 4 top / 4 bottom layers, infill 10-15 % gyroid. Wall thickness is 2.4 mm (planter and saucer), 2.4 mm floor on planter, 2.0 mm on saucer.
- No supports: flat bottoms, walls flare ~8 deg from vertical, no overhangs. Brim not needed.
- Material: PLA/PETG. Drain hole 8 mm dia. Mass: planter ~52 g, saucer ~13 g (PLA).
- Optional: 2 colours using the U1 toolchanger (teal planter / cream saucer), or vase mode is NOT suitable (closed floor + drain hole).
- Slice in Snapmaker Orca and set the printer profile to U1 0.4 mm before printing.
