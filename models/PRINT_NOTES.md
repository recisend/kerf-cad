## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle optimisation
- Size 78.4 x 79.4 x 100 mm (U1 bed 270^3); prints upright, flat base, no supports, no overhangs.
- Walls 1.6 mm = 4 x 0.4 mm perimeters; floor 2.0 mm = 10 layers @ 0.2 mm; heights 100/80/60 mm are multiples of 0.2 mm.
- 0.6 mm base chamfer for elephant-foot relief.
- Suggested: Snapmaker Orca, 0.2 mm layer, PLA, Arachne, 3-4 walls, 10-15% infill (walls are nearly solid anyway), no supports, no brim. Single colour; for multicolour, paint a colour change per cell in the slicer.
- ~63 g PLA at full density.
