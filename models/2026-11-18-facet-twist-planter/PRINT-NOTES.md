# Facet Twist Planter — print notes (Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle)

Original design (twisted hexagonal planter with drainage hole). Not based on any real product, brand or character.

- Size: 84 x 72.7 x 80 mm (fits U1 270 mm bed easily; ~4 per plate if batch printing)
- Est. PLA mass: ~55 g (solid walls) — check in slicer
- Wall ~2.1 mm = 5 perimeters at 0.4 mm line width; floor 3 mm = 15 layers at 0.2 mm
- Printed upright, flat bottom, no supports needed (max overhang ~ 7 deg flare + twist)
- Suggested: 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, 5 walls, 15% gyroid infill (mostly walls anyway), 4 top/bottom layers
- Drain hole: 7 mm through the floor (bridges nothing; hole is vertical)
- Tip for sale photos: silk PLA, one colour; add a saucer via a separate model

Not done: bottom elephant-foot chamfer (Kerf chamfer failed on the lofted bottom edge) — enable elephant foot compensation in the slicer.
