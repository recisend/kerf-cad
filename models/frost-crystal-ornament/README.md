# Frost Crystal Ornament

Original six-arm geometric snowflake ornament. Not based on any real product, character, brand or licensed design.

## Files
- `frost-crystal-ornament.3mf` - print file, 2 coloured parts (mm)
- `frost-crystal-ornament.kerf.json` - editable Kerf CAD source
- `preview.png` - top view

## Print profile (Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle)
- Size: about 77 x 91 x 3.8 mm (fits the U1 bed with a very large margin)
- Layer height 0.2 mm, 2-3 walls, 4 top / 4 bottom layers, 15% infill (part is almost solid at 3 mm)
- No supports, no brim needed (flat 3 mm base, all faces vertical or horizontal)
- Arms are 5 mm wide, branches about 4 mm wide, hanging-loop wall is 1.8 mm. Only the acute arm tips are narrower, so slicer thin-wall handling may trim them slightly
- Not yet test-printed or checked in a slicer; please preview in Snapmaker Orca before printing. Machine limits (bed size etc.) are from general knowledge, not verified here
- Two toolheads: "Snowflake Base" (white, 3.0 mm) and "Accent Medallion" (blue, 0.8 mm = 4 layers at 0.2 mm). Medallion sits on the base top face, so the colour change happens at layer 16
- Hanging hole: 3.6 mm diameter, fits ribbon, twine or a small hook
