Original geometric 8-point star Christmas ornament with a hanging loop. Not based on any real product, brand or character.

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle settings
- Print flat on the bed, no supports, no brim needed
- Layer height 0.2 mm (3 mm = 15 layers); 0.12 mm for a smoother finish
- 3 walls (1.2 mm), 4 top/bottom layers; infill is minimal at 3 mm thick
- Design rules used: hole/slot features >= 3.5 mm, wall between features >= 4 mm, tip radius 0.8 mm (fine tips print poorly at 0.4 mm), loop ring wall 2.75 mm, 3.5 mm string hole
- PLA / PLA+ at your usual U1 profile. Silk or metallic PLA looks best. Single color; multi-color via the U1 toolheads (e.g. 2 colors per star) is an optional listing upsell.
