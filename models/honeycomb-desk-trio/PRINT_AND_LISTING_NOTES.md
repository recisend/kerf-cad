## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle optimisation

- Flat bottom, vertical walls only: no supports, no bridges, no overhangs.
- Suggested: 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 15% gyroid infill (cups are mostly walls), 3 top/bottom layers on floor, PLA. Slice with Snapmaker Orca.
- Est. PLA use: about 55-80 g depending on infill/wall settings.
- Export: 3MF (mm), single body.
