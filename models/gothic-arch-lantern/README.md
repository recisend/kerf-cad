# Gothic Arch Tealight Lantern (original design)

Original, non-branded design (no real products, characters or logos). Built in Kerf CAD; `gothic-arch-lantern.3mf` is the print file, `.kerf.json` is the editable source.

**Trend rationale (sources checked 2026-11-28):** "Gothmas"/moody gothic Christmas decor is cited as a leading 2026 Etsy trend (SellerApp); home decor and desk/functional items are consistently listed as top 3D-print sellers (Snapmaker blog, JLC3DP, Etsy market pages). Trend data is from vendor blogs, not official Etsy sales data, so treat it as directional.

## Parts (2, plate-ready, 140 x 60 mm footprint, 72 mm tall)
| Part | Colour (U1 extruder) | Size | Est. mass |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lantern Body | charcoal #2b2b30 | 60 x 60 x 72 | ~43 g |
| Lantern Roof (fits inside body, 0.6 mm clearance/side) | burgundy #6e1423 | 60 x 60 x 34 | ~18 g |

Uses a battery/LED tealight only. Never use a real flame inside a PLA print.

## Snapmaker U1 settings (0.4 mm nozzle, 270^3 mm bed)
- 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layer, PLA
- Walls: 2.4 mm = 3 perimeters at 0.8 mm / 6 lines at 0.4; floor 2.4 mm
- Supports: none needed (pointed arches self-support, roof slope ~41 deg from vertical, 3 mm plug overhang bridges)
- Print upright; body and roof placed side by side, assign body/roof to different toolheads
- Verify in Snapmaker Orca before listing (slicing was not run in this session)
