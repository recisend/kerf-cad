## Geometry
- Size: 72.0 x 70.9 x 90.0 mm (fits the U1's 270 mm build volume; roughly 9 per plate in a 3x3 grid with spacing, 90 mm tall so print them one plate at a time)
- Hexagonal cup, 60° twist over 90 mm, 1.6 mm walls (4 x 0.4 mm lines), 2.0 mm floor (10 layers @ 0.2 mm)
- Volume ~36.5 cm³ (~45 g PLA at typical infill/perimeters)
- Max wall lean ~22° from vertical, flat base: **no supports, no brim needed**

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle settings
- Nozzle 0.4 mm, layer height 0.2 mm (0.16 mm for a smoother, premium finish)
- Walls: 4 perimeters (exactly fills the 1.6 mm wall), 0 % infill is fine for the walls; 5 top/bottom layers on the floor
- Seam: aligned at the back or random. Spiral vase mode is not recommended here (solid floor, 1.6 mm wall)
- Speed: outer wall ~100-150 mm/s on the U1; enable elephant-foot compensation ~0.1-0.15 mm
- Material: silk PLA (dual-colour/gradient silk shows off the twist best); the U1 multi-tool head can do colour changes per layer range for a gradient — set colour changes in the slicer
