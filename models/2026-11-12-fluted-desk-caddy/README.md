# Fluted Desk Caddy — 2026-11-12

Original design (no real-world product, brand or character). Minimalist ribbed desk organizer, 2 compartments.

## Why this product
Web research this run (directional, not hard sales data): minimalist/ribbed desk organizers and other
functional, problem-solving prints are a consistent Etsy category; practical tools are described as underserved
vs. character/fandom prints, which carry licensing risk. Etsy desk-organizer listings are crowded (~$13–49), so
differentiate with colour (Snapmaker U1 multicolour), photos and a personalised option.

## Geometry (Kerf CAD, mm)
- Ø80 body + 24 vertical ribs (Ø4) -> Ø85.6 overall, 100 high, one 1.6 mm divider (62 high)
- Wall 1.6 mm (4 x 0.4 mm perimeters), floor 2.4 mm (12 layers @ 0.2), divider 1.6 mm
- Ribs are vertical cylinders: no overhangs, no supports needed. Flat base on z=0.
- Footprint 86 x 86 mm — fits the U1's 270 x 270 x 270 mm volume with room for 4 per plate.
- Part colour set to sage green (#7a9e7e); 3MF carries part colour.

## Slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca, U1, 0.4 mm nozzle)
- Layer height 0.20 mm, 4 walls, 3 top / 3 bottom layers, no supports, no brim
- Infill 10% gyroid (walls do the work), PLA
- Print upright as exported. Estimated ~100 g of filament (Kerf mass at PLA density).
- Verify exact time/filament in the slicer — not estimated here.
