## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle optimisation
- Cluster footprint 77.9 x 75 x 110 mm, well inside the U1's 270 mm build volume. No scaling needed.
- Walls 1.6 mm (4 x 0.4 mm perimeters); floors 2.0 mm (10 layers at 0.2 mm).
- Flat base on the bed, vertical walls, no overhangs, no bridges, so no supports.
- Three separate parts with colours (orange #e0662b, amber #f2b632, teal #2f8f83) map to U1 toolheads for multicolour, with no purge-heavy mid-layer swaps in a single object.
- Suggested slicer settings: 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 15% gyroid infill or 0 top layers (open top), PLA.
- Estimated mass about 109 g total (PLA, solid-geometry estimate; slicer output will differ).
