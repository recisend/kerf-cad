# Reeded Planter + Saucer (2026-11-11)

Original design, no brand, character or real-product reference. Built in Kerf CAD (`reeded-planter-set.kerf.json`, parametric via `#R`).

**Files:** `reeded-planter-set.3mf` (2 coloured parts, laid out side by side on one plate), `preview.png`, `preview-top.png`.

## Why this product
Research this run (third-party seller blogs, so directional only, not official Etsy data):
- Home decor / planters, desk organisers and gifts are consistently listed among Etsy 3D-print bestsellers.
- Fluted / reeded surfaces are a current look in decor.
- Late Nov to Dec is gift season, and a plant-pot gift is low-risk and needs no licensed IP.
- "3d printed organizer" is crowded (about 54k listings) and its search volume was reported as falling, so I picked planters instead.
- Several sources say listings built on named characters are what Etsy policy and copyright rule out, which is why this design is fully original.

## Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle optimisation
| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Plate use | both parts span about 204 x 99 mm and are 85 mm tall, inside the 270 x 270 x 270 mm volume |
| Planter wall | 2.4 mm (6 perimeters at 0.4) |
| Saucer wall / floor | 3.0 mm / 2.0 mm |
| Planter floor | 3.0 mm, 8 mm drain hole |
| Ribs | 6 mm diameter, 16 around, vertical, no supports needed |
| Heights | planter 85 mm, saucer 12 mm, all multiples of 0.2 mm layers |
| Overhangs | none (all vertical walls, flat first layers), no supports |
| Smallest feature | 3 mm radius ribs, well above the 0.4 mm nozzle limit |

Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca): 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layer, 3 to 4 walls, 15% gyroid infill (walls are nearly solid), no supports, brim off, PLA or PETG. The 3MF carries part colours, so assign the planter to one extruder (cream) and the saucer to another (green) on the U1. Estimated PLA: about 112 g planter + 30 g saucer.

Note: the model is not watertight-tested in a slicer in this environment, so slice once and eyeball the preview before the first sale. A sealed plastic pot is not food-safe; sell it as decor, not for edible herbs without a liner.
