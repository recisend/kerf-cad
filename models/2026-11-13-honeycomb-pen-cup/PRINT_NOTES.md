## Designed for 0.4 mm nozzle

- Floor 2.0 mm = 10 layers at 0.2 mm
- 0.6 mm bottom chamfer to avoid elephant-foot flare
- No overhangs or bridges: print upright, no supports

## Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca, U1 + 0.4 mm nozzle profile)

- Material: PLA or PETG; add a drainage-free liner if used for liquids (not watertight-tested)
