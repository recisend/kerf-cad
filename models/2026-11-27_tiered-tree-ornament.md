# Tiered Tree Ornament — 2026-11-27

Original design (no licensed characters, brands, or real products). Made in Kerf CAD.

## Why this one
- Holiday ornaments / stocking stuffers are the strongest seasonal signal for late Nov–Dec.
- Etsy only allows 3D printed items from the seller's own design — this is original.
- Trend data came from vendor blogs, not Etsy sales data: check Etsy search/bestseller pages before pricing.

## Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle
- Size: 54 x 54 x 90 mm (U1 volume 270^3 — fits ~20 per plate if you want batch runs).
- Prints upright, flat base, **no supports**. Max overhang ~40 deg from vertical (tier ledges).
- Smallest feature: eyelet wall 3.5 mm (>= 8 perimeters at 0.4 mm) — no thin walls.
- Suggested: 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 10-15% infill, PLA ~215 C. Est. ~25-30 g filament (solid volume is 53.7 cm3 — estimate, check in slicer).
- Multicolor idea (U1 toolchanger): green body, gold tip/eyelet via a colour change at Z ~ 66 mm.
