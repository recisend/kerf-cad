## What it is
Phone/small-tablet stand with a cable pass-through slot and a hexagonal pen/tool cup, in one print.

- Size: 100 x 90 x 70 mm (fits the Snapmaker U1's 270 x 270 x 270 mm bed, centred in one print).
- Phone slot: 14 mm wide at the base, leaning back 15 degrees. Fits phones with a case.
- Cable slot: 25 x 9 mm through the floor of the slot.
- Pen cup: hexagon, 34.6 mm across flats, 67.6 mm deep, 2.4 mm floor.

## Printer optimisation (Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle)
- No supports needed: flat bottom, the slot leans only 15 degrees from vertical.
- Walls are multiples of 0.4 mm (2.4 mm). Smallest rim between slot and cup is about 2.9 mm.
- Suggested Snapmaker Orca settings: 0.2 mm layer, 3 walls, 15 to 20% gyroid infill, 4 top and 4 bottom layers.
- Single colour fits any one filament slot. For a 2-colour version, change colour at the slot/cup features.
- Print flat on the bed in the orientation of the 3MF. Rough solid volume is 469 cm3 before slicing.
