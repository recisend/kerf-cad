## Honeycomb Desk Organizer (2026-11-08)

Seven hexagonal cups in a honeycomb cluster, stepped from 100 mm at the back to 50 mm at the front: tall pen/brush
cups behind, short cups for clips, cards and small tools in front.

### Geometry
- Overall: 116.8 x 112.7 x 100 mm, one solid part, flat bottom, no overhangs, **no supports needed**.
- Cell: 40 mm flat-to-flat outside, 36.8 mm inside, centres 38.4 mm apart, so neighbouring cells share one 1.6 mm wall.
- Walls 1.6 mm (exactly 4 perimeters at 0.4 mm), floor 2.0 mm, cell heights 100 / 90 / 80 / 75 / 70 / 55 / 50 mm.
- Volume 97.9 cm3, about 121 g in PLA when printed as solid walls.
- Mesh verified: watertight, consistent winding, volume matches an independent manifold3d rebuild (97,931 mm3).

### Suggested Snapmaker Orca settings (U1, 0.4 mm nozzle)
- Layer height 0.20 mm, wall generator Arachne, 4 walls (1.6 mm), top/bottom 5 layers, infill 10-15% (barely used).
- Print flat on the base. Add 0.1 mm elephant-foot compensation if the first layer flares.
- A brim is optional; the footprint is large and flat, so it should stick without one.
- Not tested on a physical printer. Do a test print before listing.
