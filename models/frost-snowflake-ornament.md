# Frost Snowflake Ornament (original design)

Files: `frost-snowflake-ornament.3mf` (print file), `.kerf.json` (editable source), preview PNGs.

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle settings
- Size: 65 x 81.5 x 4.2 mm (fits U1 bed with room to batch ~12 per plate); ~11.8 g solid
- Orientation: flat on bed, hub side up - no supports, no brim needed
- Layer height 0.2 mm (0.12 mm for a smoother top), line width 0.42 mm
- Walls: 3 perimeters (thinnest feature is 4 mm; hanging-hole ring 2.7 mm), 100% infill is effectively automatic
- Top/bottom: 4 layers; hanging hole 3.6 mm (fits twine/ribbon)
- Suggested filament: white, pearl or silk-silver PLA; also nice in translucent PLA

## Why this design
Seasonal demand (Christmas ornaments / stocking stuffers) is peaking now; original geometric
design (no characters, brands or real products) satisfies Etsy's original-design rule for 3D prints.
Trend sources are seller-blog guidance, not hard sales data.
