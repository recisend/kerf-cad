# Hex-Cell Desk Organizer (original design, 2026-11-13)

Three fused hexagonal cups at stepped heights (90 / 70 / 55 mm) for pens, brushes, tools or makeup brushes.
Fully original geometry; no brand, character or real-product references.

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle optimisation
- Footprint 76.2 x 77 x 90 mm – fits the U1 bed (270 x 270 x 270) with room for batch printing (about 6 per plate).
- Walls 1.6 mm = exactly 4 perimeters at 0.4 mm line width; shared walls 3.2 mm = 8 perimeters.
- Floor 2.0 mm = 10 layers at 0.2 mm (also works at 0.16 / 0.24: use 2.0 / 2.0).
- All walls vertical, flat bottom: **no supports, no brim needed**, no overhangs.
- Suggested: 0.2 mm layer, 4 walls, 15% gyroid infill (mostly walls anyway), PLA/PETG, ~35-45 g per piece (estimate).
- Slicer: Snapmaker Orca. Select the Snapmaker U1 0.4 mm profile. Verify in the slicer; this was not test-sliced here.
