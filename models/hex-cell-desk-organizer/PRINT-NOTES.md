# Hex Cell Desk Organizer

Original honeycomb desk organizer (7 stepped hex cells, 90 / 65 / 50 mm tall). Generic geometric design, not a copy of any real product, brand or character.

## Snapmaker U1 settings (0.4 mm nozzle)
- Size 104.8 x 101.1 x 90 mm, well inside the 270 mm cube build volume. Prints upright on the flat base, no supports.
- Walls 1.6 mm = exactly 4 perimeters at 0.4 mm line width. Floor 1.6 mm = 8 layers at 0.2 mm.
- Layer height 0.2 mm, 4 wall loops, 3-4 bottom layers is enough (floor is solid), 15% infill (only the floor and base use it).
- Vertical walls only: no overhangs, no bridging, no supports, no brim needed.
- Colour idea (U1 has 4 toolheads): paint the cell rim or alternate cells in Snapmaker Orca for a premium look.
- Volume about 72 cm3, roughly 70-90 g of PLA.
