# Hex Planter with Saucer (2026-11-24)

Original design, not based on any real product, brand or character.

- Files: `hex-planter-with-saucer.3mf` (2 parts), `hex-planter-with-saucer.kerf.json` (editable source)
- Planter: 69.3 x 80 x 70 mm, 8 mm drainage hole. Saucer: 77.9 x 90 x 10 mm.
- Both parts are laid out flat on the same plate (planter at Y 0, saucer at Y 90), so they fit in one print on the U1 (270 mm bed).

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle settings
- Nozzle 0.4 mm, layer height 0.2 mm, line width 0.42-0.45 mm
- Walls: 2.4 mm = 6 perimeters at 0.4 mm. Floors: 2.4 mm (12 layers at 0.2 mm). Set 6 wall loops, 0 sparse infill is fine since walls are nearly solid.
- No supports needed: all walls are vertical, nothing overhangs.
- Orientation: as exported (open side up).
- Material: PLA or PETG. For planters with water, PETG is more durable; avoid food-contact claims.
- Drainage hole is 8 mm and prints as a clean vertical hole, no bridging.
