# Honeycomb Desk Organizer (original design, 2026-11-23)

Original geometric design, not based on any real-world product, brand or character.

**Why this item:** Desk/workshop organizers are consistently among the best-selling 3D-printed
categories (see Snapmaker's popular-prints guide and ZIK/eBay demand data). A stepped honeycomb
pen/tool cup is a gift-friendly desk item that needs no supports, no assembly and no license.
Etsy-side demand data (eRank/Marmalead) was not reachable from this run, so "trending" is based
on public category data only.

## Specs
- 7 hex cells, 109 x 105 mm footprint, 90 mm tall (staircase 40-90 mm)
- Wall 1.2 mm (3 perimeters at 0.4 mm), floor 1.6 mm (8 layers at 0.2 mm)
- Volume about 62 cm3, roughly 80 g PLA
- Fits the Snapmaker U1 bed (270 x 270 x 270 mm) with large margin

## Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle)
- Layer height 0.2 mm, 3 walls, 4+ top/bottom layers, 15% gradient/gyroid infill (mostly walls anyway)
- No supports, no brim needed; print flat on the floor face
- PLA, nozzle 215 C, bed 60 C
- Multi-colour option: use the U1's tool changer to alternate cell colours by splitting in the slicer
  (3MF exports as one part; split to objects/parts in Snapmaker Orca if wanted)
