## Geometry
- Planter: 70 mm tall, twisted hexagonal taper (bottom r32 -> top r42, 20 deg twist), approx 83 x 79 mm footprint
- Walls about 1.6 mm (4 perimeters at 0.4 mm line width), floor 2.4 mm, 6 mm drain hole
- Saucer: 10 mm tall, hex, approx 66 x 76 mm, 2.2 mm walls, 2.4 mm floor
- Both fit well inside the Snapmaker U1 270 x 270 x 270 mm volume
- Flat bottoms, outward taper only about 8 deg from vertical, so **no supports** required

## Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle, Orca-based Snapmaker Orca)
- Layer height 0.2 mm, line width 0.42 mm, 4 walls, 0 or 12% infill (walls carry the part)
- Spiral/"vase" mode NOT used (drain hole and floor), supports off, brim off
- Material: PLA or PETG (PETG preferred for planters)
- Print planter and saucer in one plate
