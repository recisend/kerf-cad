## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm optimisation
- Build volume 270x270x270. Layout is 206 x 100 x 85 mm, so both parts fit on one plate.
- All walls and floors are 1.6 mm = 4 perimeters of 0.4 mm. Ribs are 5.2 mm wide, well above the 0.4 mm minimum.
- No overhangs, bridges or supports. Both parts sit flat on the bed. Drain hole is vertical (8 mm).
- Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca): 0.2 mm layer, 4 walls (Arachne), 3 top / 4 bottom layers, 12-15% gyroid infill (mostly walls anyway), no supports, brim for toolhead rails per community advice. PLA or PETG. Estimated about 96 g planter + 22 g saucer (PLA, solid-volume estimate).
- Planter is 91.6 mm wide at the ribs, 85 mm tall. Saucer inner diameter 96.8 mm.
