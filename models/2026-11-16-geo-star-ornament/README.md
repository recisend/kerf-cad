# Geometric Star Ornament — 2026-11-16

Original abstract design (8-point star + octagon inlay). Not based on any real item, brand, character or product.

## Why this one
Research (seller-tool blogs, directional only, no verified Etsy sales data): ornaments / holiday decor peak Nov–mid Dec; personalised and gift-able decor, organizers and fidget items are the most-cited 3D-print categories. A two-colour geometric ornament is quick to print, sells as a set (multi-pack) and works for gifting.

## Files
- `geo-star-ornament.3mf` — 2 parts (white base, gold inlay), mm. Import to Snapmaker Orca / Luban.
- `geo-star-ornament.kerf.json` — editable Kerf source.
- `preview.png` — render.

## Printer: Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle
- Orientation: flat on the bed as exported (Z=0 face down). No supports, no brim needed.
- Layer height 0.20 mm (base = 15 layers, inlay = 6 layers). Walls 3 (1.2 mm), top/bottom 4, infill 100% (parts are 3–4.2 mm thick).
- Filament: PLA, 2 colours (base on T1, inlay on T2 — U1 tool changer; one swap at layer 16).
- Size: 80 x 85 x 4.2 mm — one build plate fits ~16 per run (270 mm bed); ~8.3 g incl. inlay.
- Hang hole 4.4 mm, wall around it 3.8 mm. Star tips are sharp (<0.8 mm for the last ~1 mm), the slicer will round them; acceptable.
