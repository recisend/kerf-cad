# Honeycomb Desk Caddy

Original design (no brands, logos or real-world products). Three hex cups of different heights (110 / 85 / 60 mm) sharing walls, for pens, brushes, tools or makeup.

**Files:** `honeycomb-desk-caddy.3mf` (print file), `honeycomb-desk-caddy.kerf.json` (editable Kerf source), `preview.png`.

## Why this one (research, 2026-11-27)
Sources are vendor blogs and Etsy listing pages, so the signal is directional, not sales data:
- Modular desk organizers / stationery caddies listed as trending desk accessories, roughly $14-$49 on Etsy.
- Personalized desk accessories and holiday gifts are cited as seasonal peaks around now.
- Brand-logo lightboxes and licensed items are the biggest sellers but cannot be sold without a licence, so they are excluded on purpose.

## Optimized for Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle
- Size 95.4 x 96.6 x 110 mm, well inside the 270 x 270 x 270 mm build volume.
- Walls 1.6 mm = exactly 4 x 0.4 mm lines (no gap fill, solid outer shell).
- Floor 2.0 mm = 10 layers at 0.2 mm.
- All walls vertical, flat base: **no supports, no brim needed**.
- 0.6 mm chamfer around the base edge to offset elephant's foot.
- Mesh checked: watertight, millimetre units, Z up, sits on Z = 0.

Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca): 0.2 mm layer, 4 walls, 15% gyroid infill (only the floor has infill), 3 top / 3 bottom layers, PLA ~220 C / bed 60 C. For colour, add a color change or use a second toolhead for the top 10 mm; the U1 has four toolheads.

Estimated: ~68 cm3, ~84 g PLA.
