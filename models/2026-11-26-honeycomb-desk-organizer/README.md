# Honeycomb Desk Organizer (original design, 2026-11-26)

Original geometric 7-cell pen / tool / brush organizer. No real-world product, brand or character.

- Size: 98.8 x 95.4 x 105 mm (fits Snapmaker U1 270 mm bed easily), ~98 g PLA (solid walls estimate)
- Files: `honeycomb-desk-organizer.3mf` (export), `honeycomb-desk-organizer.kerf.json` (editable Kerf source), `preview.png`

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle optimisation
- Walls 1.6 mm = exactly 4 perimeters of 0.4 mm lines (shared walls also 1.6 mm)
- Floor 2.0 mm = 10 layers at 0.2 mm; all heights (45/55/60/70/80/90/105) are multiples of 0.2 mm
- Flat bottom, vertical walls, no overhangs, no bridges -> **no supports**, no brim needed
- Single part, no tolerances/assembly; prints as one piece
- Suggested slicer: Snapmaker Orca, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layer, 4 walls, 0% infill (walls only) or 10% gyroid, top layers 0-2, 1 colour (or change filament at layers for a 2-tone look)

## Why this product
Functional desk organizers / geometric decor are consistently listed as steady Etsy sellers in 3D-print seller guides; hex/honeycomb is a popular, non-trademarked shape. Trend sources were vendor blogs, not hard sales data - treat as directional.
