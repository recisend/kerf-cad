# Honeycomb Desk Organizer (original design, 2026-11-10)

Original abstract geometric design - not modelled on any real-world product.
Three fused hexagonal cups at staggered heights (75 / 100 / 125 mm) for pens, brushes, tools.

Files: `honeycomb-desk-organizer.3mf` (print file), `.kerf.json` (editable source), `preview.png`.

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle settings
- Size 95.4 x 96.6 x 125 mm (U1 volume 270^3) - fits with room to spare; ~77.5 cm3 solid, ~95 g PLA.
- Wall = 1.6 mm (exactly 4 x 0.4 mm perimeters); floor = 2.0 mm (about 10 layers at 0.2 mm).
- Prints flat on its base, **no supports, no overhangs**. 0.8 mm bottom chamfer avoids elephant foot.
- Suggested: 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 15% gyroid infill (floor/top solid layers 5), no brim needed.
- Colour idea: a different PLA colour per toolhead (U1 is 4-toolhead) - e.g. swap filament at the
  heights of the cups, or print three colour-themed variants.
