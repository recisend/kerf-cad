# Honeycomb Desk Tidy (2026-11-15)

Original parametric design (no real-world product, brand or character). Draft only — NOT published anywhere.

## Why this item
Desk organizers / desk accessories and geometric-parametric home decor show up consistently in 2026 Etsy 3D-print seller guides (directional, vendor-blog data — not hard Etsy sales numbers). Holiday gifting season (Nov) adds demand for desk gifts.

## Geometry (3 hex cells with shared walls)
- Footprint 102.3 x 103.6 mm, height 90 mm — fits the U1's 270 x 270 x 270 mm volume with plenty of margin
- Walls 1.6 mm (= 4 perimeters at 0.4 mm line width), floor 2.0 mm (10 layers @ 0.2 mm)
- 0.6 mm chamfer on bottom edges (limits elephant foot)
- Flat base, vertical walls: no supports, no overhangs
- Volume ~62.8 cm3 (~78 g PLA at 100% density; less with sparse infill)

## Snapmaker U1 slicer settings (0.4 mm nozzle)
- Nozzle 0.4 mm, layer height 0.20 mm (Snapmaker's recommended; range 0.1-0.3)
- Wall generator: Arachne, 4 walls, 3-4 top / 4 bottom layers
- Infill: 10-15% gyroid (walls carry the load), supports: off
- Brim: optional small brim (Snapmaker suggests one for the toolhead rails on tall prints)
- Single-color works; U1 multi-color: change filament at layer ranges per cell for a gradient look
