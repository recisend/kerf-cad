# Hex Honeycomb Desk Organizer (2026-11-14)

Original, generic design (no real-world product, brand or character). 7 hexagonal cells at three heights (80 / 62 / 46 mm) for pens, brushes, scissors and tools. Footprint 108.4 x 104.6 mm, height 80 mm. Built with the Kerf CAD MCP server; the Kerf source is `hex-honeycomb-organizer.kerf.json`.

Files: `hex-honeycomb-organizer.3mf` (print file, mm), `preview-iso.png`, `preview-top.png`.

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle settings

- Fits the U1 bed (270 x 270 x 270 mm) with room to spare. Print one copy per plate, or up to 4 per plate for batch runs.
- Wall 1.6 mm = exactly 4 perimeters at 0.4 mm line width; floor 2.0 mm = 10 layers at 0.2 mm.
- All walls are vertical and the base is flat: **no supports, no brim needed**.
- Suggested: 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 3 top/bottom layers, 10-15% gyroid infill (walls are almost all perimeters anyway), PLA or PETG.
- Volume about 73 cm3 (about 90 g PLA at 100% solid; real usage is close to this because walls are mostly perimeters).
- Optional: print in a gradient / dual colour on the U1 by colour-changing at layer heights of your choice.
