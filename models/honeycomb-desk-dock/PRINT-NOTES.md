# Honeycomb Desk Dock — print notes

Original design (not based on any real product or brand): a phone dock plus three honeycomb pen/tool cups.

- **Printer:** Snapmaker U1 (270 × 270 × 270 mm build volume; part is 80 × 117 × 110 mm), 0.4 mm nozzle
- **Format:** 3MF (units mm), `honeycomb-desk-dock.3mf`
- **Orientation:** as exported, flat on the bed, no supports needed (max overhang ~13° from vertical)
- **Layer height:** 0.20 mm (floor is 2.4 mm = 12 layers; slot floor 4.0 mm = 20 layers)
- **Walls:** 5 perimeters × 0.4 mm = 2.0 mm (matches the 2.0 mm wall designed between cups); the shared cup walls are exactly 2.0 mm
- **Infill:** 10–15 % gyroid, 4 top/bottom layers
- **Material:** PLA / PLA+ (≈ 240 g solid-model estimate at 100 %; real usage is lower with 10 % infill)
- **Slicing:** open the 3MF in Snapmaker Orca, choose the U1 + 0.4 mm nozzle profile

Etsy research (directional, vendor blogs, not hard sales data): fidget toys, desk organisers, phone stands and personalised gifts show the most consistent demand. See the session summary for sources.
