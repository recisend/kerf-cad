# Honeycomb Desk Organizer — print notes (original design, 2026-11-14)

Original geometric design (3 interlocking hex cups: 90 / 70 / 50 mm tall). Not based on any real product, brand or character.

- Size: 102 x 103.3 x 90 mm (fits Snapmaker U1 270 mm bed easily). Solid volume ~76.9 cm3.
- Printer / nozzle: Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm. Format: 3MF.
- Walls 2.0 mm (= 5 x 0.4 mm perimeters, clean wall count). Floor 2.4 mm (12 layers at 0.2 mm).
- No overhangs, no supports, flat bottom, no bridging. Prints upright as exported.
- Suggested slicer settings (Orca-style / Snapmaker Orca): 0.2 mm layer, 0.42 mm line width, 5 walls, 0 infill needed beyond 12 top/bottom layers (use 10% gyroid), no supports, no brim, elephant-foot compensation 0.1-0.2 mm.
- Filament idea: matte PLA in 2-3 colours (print as one colour, or use the U1 toolchanger for colour-per-cup by painting regions in the slicer).
