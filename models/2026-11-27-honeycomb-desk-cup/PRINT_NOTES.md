# Honeycomb Desk Cup — original design (no real-world item/brand/character)
Date: 2026-11-27 · Files: `honeycomb-desk-cup.3mf` (print file), `.kerf.json` (editable Kerf source)

Size: 69.3 x 80 x 90 mm (hexagonal, honeycomb windows on all 6 faces) · ~58 g PLA · fits the Snapmaker U1 bed easily.

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle settings (Snapmaker Orca; slicer nozzle must match the touchscreen = 0.4)
- Layer 0.20 mm, line width 0.42 mm, 3 walls, top/bottom 4 layers, infill 10% gyroid
- The model wall is 2.4 mm (a clean multiple of 0.4 mm lines).
- Orientation: upright as exported, flat floor on the textured PEI plate. No supports needed (hex window tops are 30° from vertical). Brim not needed.
- Arachne wall generator. PLA ~220 °C nozzle / 55 °C bed. Multi-colour (up to 4 on U1): a layer-height colour change is an easy sellable variant.
