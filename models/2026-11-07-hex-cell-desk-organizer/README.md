# Hex Cell Desk Organizer (2026-11-07)

Original, non-branded honeycomb desk organizer: 7 hexagonal cells (1 centre at 95 mm, 3 at 75 mm, 3 at 55 mm). Holds pens, brushes, markers, scissors, cables. Not based on any real-world item.

- Footprint 87.9 x 84.6 mm, height 95 mm (fits the Snapmaker U1's 270 mm cube with room to spare).
- Walls ~1.6 mm (4 x 0.4 mm lines), 2 mm floor, cell opening 27.7 mm flat-to-flat.
- Prints flat on the bed, vertical walls, no overhangs, no supports.
- Volume 47.5 cm3 (~59 g PLA at 100% density; much less with sparse infill).

## Suggested Snapmaker U1 slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca, 0.4 mm nozzle)
- Layer height 0.2 mm, line width 0.42 mm, Arachne walls, 4 walls, 3 bottom / 3 top layers
- Infill 10% gyroid (walls do almost all of the work), supports off, brim 5 mm (U1 guidance)
- Multi-colour option: assign a different filament per cell height tier on the U1's 4 toolheads - not baked into the file, set it in the slicer.
