# Frost Crystal Ornament (2026-11-12)

Original design, not based on any real item, character or brand. Draft only, not published anywhere.

- Files: `2026-11-12_frost-crystal-ornament.3mf` (export), `.kerf.json` (editable in Kerf)
- Size: 65.9 x 82.5 mm footprint, 3.0 mm thick (+0.8 mm accent medallion = 3.8 mm total)
- Parts (2 colours for the 4-toolhead U1): `Snowflake Body` (white), `Gold Medallion` (gold, 0.8 mm raised hex)
- Mesh check: both parts watertight, consistent winding, no degenerate triangles

## Print settings (Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle)
- Layer height 0.20 mm (body = 15 layers, medallion = 4 layers), first layer 0.2 mm
- Walls 2 (0.8 mm min feature; narrowest arm branch is 4.4 mm), top/bottom 4 layers (0.8 mm)
- Infill 15-20% gyroid (or 100% - body is only 3 mm thick)
- No supports, no brim; lay flat, medallion up. Medallion colour change at layer 16 (z = 3.0)
- Est. filament: ~8.8 g white + ~0.13 g gold; fits one plate dozens of times (270 x 270 mm bed)
- Hanging hole: 3.6 mm, wall around it 2.7 mm. Thread ribbon/twine.
