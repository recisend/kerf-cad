# Honeycomb Desk Organizer (original design)

Generic seven-cell hexagonal desk organizer for pens, brushes, makeup tools and small tools. It's an original geometric shape and isn't based on any real product, brand or character.

- File: `honeycomb-desk-organizer.3mf` (source: `honeycomb-desk-organizer.kerf.json`)
- Size: 96.4 x 93.1 x 85 mm. This fits the Snapmaker U1's build volume with a lot of room to spare.
- Volume: about 84 cm³. That's roughly 100 g of PLA.

## Why it suits a 0.4 mm nozzle
- Every wall is 1.6 mm, which is 4 perimeters of 0.4 mm lines, so the walls fill cleanly with no gap-fill.
- The floor is 2.0 mm, which is 10 layers at 0.2 mm.
- Every surface is vertical or flat, so it needs no supports and has no overhangs or bridges.
- It prints flat on the bed as one solid part. It has no tiny details or thin features.

## Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca / Orca Slicer, Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle)
- Layer height 0.2 mm, 4 wall loops, 3 top and 3 bottom layers, 10-15% infill (nothing is hidden inside the walls, so infill barely matters).
- Supports off. Brim off.
- Turn on elephant-foot compensation, about 0.15 mm.
- Print in PLA or PETG. Silk or two-tone PLA looks good for photos.
- A vase-style variant isn't possible, because the cells share walls.
