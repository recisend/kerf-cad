# Honeycomb Desk Organizer (2026-11-14)

Original generic design, not based on any real product, brand or character.

- Files: `honeycomb-desk-organizer.3mf` (export), `.kerf.json` (editable source), `.png` (preview)
- Size: 88 x 88.9 x 95 mm, about 62 cm3 (about 77 g PLA solid-equivalent estimate; slicer will give real figure)
- Three fused hex cells (95 / 75 / 58 mm tall) for pens, brushes, scissors, small tools

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle optimisation
- Prints upright, flat on the plate, no supports, no overhangs (all walls vertical)
- Outer walls 1.6 mm (= 4 perimeters at 0.4 mm); shared walls 3.2 mm (8 perimeters)
- Floor 2.0 mm (10 layers at 0.2 mm); every dimension is a multiple of 0.2 mm layer / 0.4 mm line
- Fits the 270 mm build volume easily
- Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca): 0.2 mm layer, 4 walls, Arachne, 10-15% infill (walls make up almost all of the part), PLA/PETG. Single-colour or one colour per print; multi-colour is possible with the U1 toolheads.
- Note: the 3MF contains geometry only, not a saved slicer profile. Slice in Snapmaker Orca and verify on the U1 before listing.
