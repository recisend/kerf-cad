# Honeycomb Desk Organizer (original design, 2026-11-27)

Original geometric design — not based on any real product, brand or character.
7 hexagonal cells (1 tall centre, 6 staggered at 75 / 60 mm), pens, brushes, tools, makeup brushes.

- Size: 111.1 x 107.2 x 95 mm (Snapmaker U1 volume: 270 x 270 x 270 mm — fits easily, ~120 g PLA)
- Wall thickness: 1.6 mm (= 4 perimeters at 0.4 mm nozzle), floor 3 mm
- No overhangs, no supports needed; flat bottom, prints upright as exported
- Single watertight part, 3MF export: `honeycomb-desk-organizer.3mf`
- Kerf source: `honeycomb-desk-organizer.kerf.json`

## Suggested U1 slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca, 0.4 mm nozzle)
- 0.20 mm layer, 4 walls, 3 top / 4 bottom layers, 10-15% infill (walls are nearly solid), no supports, brim optional
- Looks great in 2-colour/multi-colour (U1 toolheads) — e.g. alternate cell colours via painted layers
