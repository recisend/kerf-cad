# Honeycomb Desk Organizer (2026-11-27)

Original, non-branded design (no real-world items, characters or logos).

**Why this product:** desk and drawer organizers are one of the most consistently listed 3D-print categories on Etsy, and it is a gift-friendly item for the holiday season. Sources are vendor blogs, not Etsy sales data, so treat the trend as directional.

## Geometry
- 3 interlocking hex cups: 90 / 70 / 55 mm tall, footprint about 74 x 75 mm
- Wall 1.6 mm (4 x 0.4 mm lines), shared walls 1.6 mm, floor 2.4 mm
- Flat base, vertical walls, no overhangs, no supports needed
- Volume 44.4 cm3, about 55 g of PLA

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle settings
- Orientation: as modelled (open end up)
- Layer height 0.2 mm, line width 0.4-0.42 mm
- Walls: 4 perimeters (1.6 mm), top/bottom layers 0.8 mm, infill 10-15% (barely used)
- Supports: off. Brim: optional, not needed
- Fits the 270 x 270 x 270 mm build volume with a lot of room; several can be batch-printed per plate
