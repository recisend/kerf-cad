# Honeycomb Desk Organizer (original design)

Date: 2026-11-22 · File: `honeycomb-desk-organizer.3mf` (mm, single part)
Source: `honeycomb-desk-organizer.kerf.json`

An original 7-cup hexagonal desk organizer for pens, brushes, tools and small
items. Not based on any real product, brand, character or trademark.

## Why this one (trend basis)
Desk/workspace organizers, personalized gifts and fidgets are the recurring
3D-print Etsy categories in current guides (see sources in the run summary).
Branded/licensed items are a takedown risk, so this is generic and original.

## Geometry
- Footprint ≈ 113.3 × 109.1 mm, height 84 mm (cups 84 / 68 / 52 mm)
- Outer wall 1.6 mm (4 × 0.4 mm perimeters), floor 2.0 mm (10 × 0.2 mm layers)
- Heights are multiples of 0.2 mm; all walls vertical, flat bottom: no supports,
  no overhangs, no bridges
- Fits Snapmaker U1 (270 × 270 × 270 mm) with plenty of margin
- Volume ≈ 103.7 cm³ → roughly 130 g PLA

## Snapmaker U1 print settings (0.4 mm nozzle)
- Layer height 0.20 mm, first layer 0.20 mm
- Walls: 4 perimeters (1.6 mm); top/bottom layers: 5 / 5
- Infill: 10–15 % (mostly walls; floor is solid)
- Supports: off · Brim: off (add 3 mm brim if bed adhesion is poor)
- Material: PLA; PLA+/PETG also fine
- Orientation: as exported (open end up)
- Multi-colour idea for U1's 4 toolheads: change filament by height/cup for a
  gradient (colour changes at layer 260 = 52 mm, 340 = 68 mm). This is an
  optional upsell variant, not baked into the file.
