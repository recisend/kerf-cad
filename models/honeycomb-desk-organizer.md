# Honeycomb Desk Organizer (3 cells)

Original design: three interlocking hexagonal cups of different heights (70 / 90 / 110 mm). Generic, not based on any real product, brand or character.

Why this one (research 2026-11-23): functional desk organizers are the most consistently cited 3D-print sellers; honeycomb/hex geometry is a popular look. No official Etsy ranking exists, so this is an informed choice from public trend articles, not a guarantee of sales.

## Snapmaker U1 print profile (0.4 mm nozzle)
- Overall size: 90.1 x 91.0 x 110 mm (U1 bed 270 x 270 x 270 mm: fits with room to spare; could print 4-6 per plate batch)
- Orientation: as modeled, open end up. Flat bottom on the bed, no supports needed, no overhangs.
- Layer height: 0.20 mm (all heights are multiples of 0.2: 2.0 floor, 70/90/110 tops)
- Walls: 1.6 mm = 4 perimeters at 0.4 mm line width; shared inner walls 3.2 mm = 8 perimeters
- Floor: 2.0 mm = 10 layers at 0.2 mm
- Infill: 10-15% (almost entirely walls)
- Supports: none
- Material: PLA / PETG; two-colour or silk PLA looks good (cells are separate faces; one colour per print is fine)
- Estimated model volume: ~74 cm3 solid (about 92 g PLA before infill savings; check in slicer)
