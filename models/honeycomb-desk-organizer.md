# Honeycomb Desk Organizer (2026-11-24)

Original design, not based on any real product, brand or character.

## Why this one
Functional WFH desk accessories (pen/tool organizers) show up in 2026 Etsy/3D-print seller guides.
Evidence is soft: the sources are mostly seller blogs and I found no hard Etsy sales data. One eBay
report says planters, vases and cookie cutters sell poorly there. Check Etsy search and eRank before listing.
The marketing/analytics MCP servers failed to connect in this run (proxy 403).

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle settings
- Size 116.8 x 112.7 x 90 mm (U1 bed is 270 x 270 x 270 mm), about 82 cm3 solid, roughly 100 g PLA.
- Walls 1.6 mm (4 x 0.4 mm lines), floor 1.2 mm (6 layers at 0.2 mm).
- All walls vertical, flat base: no supports, no brim needed.
- Suggested slicer settings: 0.2 mm layer, 3 walls, 15% gyroid infill, 3 bottom/4 top layers.
- Print orientation as exported (open side up).
- Export: 3MF, single part, millimetres.
