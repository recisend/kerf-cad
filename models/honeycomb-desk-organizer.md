# Honeycomb Desk Organizer (2026-11-09)

Original abstract design: 7 interlocking hex cells of staggered height (55-95 mm) for pens, brushes, tools or small plants. Not based on any real item, brand or character.

- Footprint 98.7 x 95 x 95 mm (Snapmaker U1 build volume 270 mm, fits easily)
- Walls 1.8 mm (about 4 lines at 0.4 mm nozzle), floor 1.6 mm (8 layers at 0.2 mm)
- Vertical walls, flat base: no supports, no overhangs, no brim needed
- Volume ~60.3 cm3, roughly 75 g PLA, at 15% gyroid infill

## Suggested Snapmaker U1 profile (0.4 mm nozzle)
- Layer height 0.2 mm, 0.4 mm line width, 3 walls, 4 top / 4 bottom layers
- Nozzle ~215 C, bed ~60 C for PLA, supports off
- Single colour; for multicolour, swap filament per cell height band

## Why this niche
Desk organizers, geometric decor and hobby storage are repeatedly named as steady Etsy 3D-print categories (vendor guides, not Etsy sales data, so treat as soft evidence). Honeycomb styling is a common visual theme. Etsy requires 3D-printed items to be the seller's own design; this one is original.
