# Twist Planter + Saucer (original design)

Not based on any real product, brand or character. NOT published anywhere.

Printer: Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle, textured PEI plate. File: twist-planter.3mf (2 parts, mm).

Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca, PLA, 0.4 mm nozzle):
- Layer height 0.20 mm (0.1-0.3 mm range per Snapmaker hot-end guide)
- Walls: 4 (1.6 mm wall) - min wall in the model is ~1.7 mm; floor 2.4 mm = 12 layers
- Top/bottom layers: 5 / 4, infill 10-15% gyroid (walls do the work)
- Nozzle 200-230 C, bed 25-65 C, part fan on
- No supports: max wall slope from vertical ~16 deg; first layer is flat hex
- Print both parts flat as placed (planter 84x76x70 mm, saucer 75x86x8 mm; fits the plate)
- Est. PLA: planter ~47 g, saucer ~19 g

Fit: planter base hex (r 38) sits in saucer recess (r 40). 4 x 6 mm drain holes.
