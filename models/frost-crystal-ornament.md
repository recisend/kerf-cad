| Size | 84.9 x 104.0 x 3.0 mm, well inside the 270 x 270 x 270 mm bed |

| Layers | 3.0 mm = 15 layers at 0.2 mm, or 10 at 0.3 mm |

| Filament | About 9.3 g body + 0.5 g inlay (PLA) |
