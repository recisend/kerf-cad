## Printer fit: Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle
- Footprint 110.3 x 106.5 mm, height 100 mm (U1 build volume is 270 mm cube, so it fits with room to spare).
- Walls 2.0 mm = 5 perimeters of 0.4 mm. Floor 2.0 mm = 10 layers at 0.20 mm. All heights (30/40/55/70/85/100) are multiples of 0.20 mm.
- Flat base, vertical walls, no overhangs steeper than 90 deg: **no supports, no brim needed**. The only overhang is the 8 mm cable notch, which is open-topped (no bridge).
- Suggested slicer settings: 0.20 mm layer, 0.4 mm line width, 5 walls, 3 top / 10 bottom layers, 10-15% gyroid infill, PLA, print as oriented (flat on the bed). ~86 cm3 of solid volume; roughly 55-70 g with sparse infill.
- Tip for sales photos: print in a two-tone/silk PLA; the stepped cells look good in a single colour with a matte finish.
