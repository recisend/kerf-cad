# Twist Facet Planter (original design, 2026-11-26)

Original geometric planter + drip saucer. No real-world product, brand, or character.

**Files:** `twist-planter.3mf` (print file), `twist-planter.kerf.json` (editable source), `preview.png`

## Print setup: Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle
- Plate: 270 x 270 x 270 mm. Both parts together are ~137 x 84 mm, so they fit on one plate.
- Planter: 77 x 77 x 70 mm, about 73 g solid-equivalent volume. Saucer: 84 x 84 x 8 mm.
- Wall thickness 1.6 mm (4 perimeters at 0.4 mm line width). Floor 1.6 mm (8 layers at 0.2 mm).
- Layer height 0.2 mm. Outward lean is about 8 degrees, so no supports are needed.
- Drain hole is 8 mm and bridges cleanly. Flat bottoms need no brim.
- Suggested: 4 walls, 15% infill (or 2 walls + 0 infill is fine for thin shells), PLA or PETG.
- Part colours in the 3MF are planter `#4f8a6b` and saucer `#e8e2d4`. The U1's 4 toolheads let you assign other colours.
