## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle settings
- Build volume 270 x 270 x 270 mm: the plate holds both parts at about 134 x 81 mm.
- Layer height 0.2 mm; line width about 0.42 mm.
- Wall thickness 2.0 mm (approx. 5 perimeters); floor 2.4 mm (12 layers at 0.2 mm). Saucer wall 2.0 mm, floor 2.0 mm.
- Supports: none. Infill: 10-15%, since walls dominate. PLA or PETG; use PETG for the saucer if it will hold water.
- Colours: the 3MF assigns planter #6F9A82 and saucer #E8E0D0, so a U1 tool changer can print both in one job. Map them to your loaded filaments in the slicer.
