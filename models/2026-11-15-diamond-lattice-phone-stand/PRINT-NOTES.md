## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle settings
- Size 80 x 74 x 107.5 mm (U1 bed 270 x 270 mm — fits easily; room for 4-6 per plate).
- Orientation: as exported, flat base on the bed. No supports needed:
  - Plate leans 20 deg from vertical; diamond cut-outs have 55 deg roofs; cable slot has 45 deg roof.
- Nozzle 0.4 mm, layer height 0.20 mm (0.16 for nicer faces), 3 walls (1.2 mm), 4 top/bottom layers.
- Infill 15% gyroid or grid. Plate is 8 mm = 20 perimeters wide, so mostly solid already.
- Material: PLA / PLA+ / PETG. Est. ~60-75 g and ~3-4 h on U1 (estimate: check in Snapmaker Orca).
- Base has a 0.8 mm bottom-edge chamfer to avoid elephant foot.
- Fits phones/cases up to ~14 mm thick (seat gap 16 mm); USB cable passes through the 16 mm slot.
- Slicer: import 3MF into Snapmaker Orca, pick "Snapmaker U1 0.4 nozzle" profile. The 3MF carries geometry only; settings are not embedded.
