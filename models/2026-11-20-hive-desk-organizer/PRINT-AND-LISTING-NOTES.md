## Snapmaker U1 slicing settings (0.4 mm nozzle, Snapmaker Orca)
- The model needs no supports and sits flat on the bed. The floor is 2.0 mm, which is 10 layers at 0.2 mm.
- Wall thickness is 1.6 mm, which is exactly 4 perimeter lines at 0.4 mm. Set 4 walls, with line width 0.4 to 0.42 mm.
- Layer height 0.2 mm. The 0.16 mm layer height also divides evenly into the 2.0 mm floor.
- Top and bottom layers: 5 bottom layers. The model has no closed top surface.
- Infill: 10 to 15% gyroid. Almost the whole part is walls.
- Brim: not needed. Add a 5 mm brim if the print shows corner lifting.
- PLA is suggested. Use PETG for a sturdier version.
- Colour: you can recolour per cup on the U1's multi-tool setup, for example a gradient. The 3MF carries a single object, so assign colours in the slicer.
