# Cottage Mushroom Pen Cup (2026-11-10)

Original design: a stylized mushroom-shaped pen / brush / makeup-brush cup. No brand, character or real-product likeness.

**Why this one:** Desk organizers and cottagecore/mushroom decor are the most consistently cited Etsy 3D-print categories (see sources below). Directional signals only — Etsy publishes no sales counts.

## Printer targets: Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle
- Size: 101.4 x 101.4 x 92 mm (U1 build volume is far larger)
- Print upright, no supports: widest overhang is a 45-degree cap underside; the dome curves upward and inward
- Stem wall 6 mm (15 perimeter lines at 0.4 mm), rim 5 mm, floor 14 mm — all multiples of line width, nothing thinner than 0.8 mm
- Suggested: 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 12-15% gyroid infill, PLA. Model volume is ~300 cm3 (cap is solid), so check print time and weight in your slicer
- Color idea: dual-color via U1 toolchanger (cream stem, red/orange cap) — add a color change at z = 40 mm in the slicer

## Known limitations
- Rim/base fillets failed in Kerf on revolved edges, so edges are sharp. Optional cosmetic fix: chamfer in slicer/mesh tool.
- Cap is solid (no internal void) — heavy; reduce infill to 8-10% to save filament.
