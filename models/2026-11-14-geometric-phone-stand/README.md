## Model
- 76 × 90 × 92 mm, ~70° viewing angle, fits phones and small tablets, 18 mm cable gap for charging
- Prints flat on its base, **no supports needed** (all faces ≤ 45° overhang, front lip is flat-topped)
- Triangular window cut through the side reduces material and gives the "geometric" look
- 1.2 mm edge fillets (hides elephant foot, nicer feel)

## Snapmaker U1 print settings (0.4 mm nozzle)
- Nozzle 0.4 mm, layer height 0.20 mm (0.16 for a finer finish), initial layer 0.2 mm
- Walls: 3 perimeters (1.2 mm), top/bottom 5 layers (1.0 mm)
- Infill: 15 % gyroid or cubic; supports: off; brim: off (large flat base)
- Material: PLA / PLA+ (nozzle ~215 °C, bed 60 °C); PETG also works
- Orientation: as exported (base on bed). Fits the U1 270 mm bed with large margin.
- Estimated filament: roughly 45–60 g at these settings (verify in slicer)
- Multi-colour idea for the U1's 4 toolheads: different colour for the front lip and window frame
