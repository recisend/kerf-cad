# Faceted LED Tealight Tree (2026-11-10)

Original design (not based on any real product or character). Hollow six-sided tree cone, open at the bottom; place it over a flameless LED tealight (max 40 mm diameter). **LED tealights only, no real flames.**

- Size: 58.9 x 68 x 110 mm (fits the Snapmaker U1's ~270 mm bed easily; several fit per plate)
- Printer: Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle, single colour
- Orientation: as exported, open base on the bed. No supports needed (walls lean inward ~14 deg; 5 mm holes are small enough to print cleanly).
- Walls: 1.6 mm = 4 perimeters at 0.4 mm line width
- Suggested: 0.20 mm layers (0.12 mm for a smoother finish), 2 top / 3 bottom layers, 0% infill (walls only), PLA, translucent or white filament lets the light glow
- Format: 3MF (mm)
