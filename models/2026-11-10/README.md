## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle notes
- Planter: 72.7 x 84 x 80 mm. Tray: 72.7 x 84 x 10 mm. Both fit the U1 bed (270 x 270 x 270) with room to spare.
- Walls and floors are 1.6 mm, which is 4 perimeters at 0.4 mm. Use 0.2 mm layers.
- The body tapers outward at about 7 degrees from vertical, so no supports are needed. Print both parts flat-side down.
- The drain holes are 8 mm and 5 mm, vertical, with a 1.6 mm floor. They print without supports.
- Suggested slicer settings: 4 walls, 3-4 top/bottom layers, 10-15% infill (barely used), Snapmaker-profile PLA or PETG. Use a vase-mode-style slow outer wall for a clean look. Verify these in the slicer, because they have not been test-printed.
- Estimated PLA mass from the model: planter about 39 g, tray about 13 g.
- The 3MF carries part names and colours only. It has no slicer profile.
