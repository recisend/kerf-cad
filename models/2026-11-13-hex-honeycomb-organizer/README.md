# Hex Honeycomb Desk Organizer (2026-11-13)

Original design (not a copy of any real product or character). Three joined hexagonal cups of different heights (70 / 90 / 110 mm) for pens, brushes, scissors and tools.

- Files: `hex-honeycomb-organizer.3mf` (print file, mm), `.kerf.json` (editable Kerf source), `preview.png`
- Overall size: 88.5 x 89.6 x 110 mm — fits the Snapmaker U1 build volume easily
- Material: ~64 cm³ (≈ 80 g PLA at 100% solid; far less with normal walls/infill)

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle optimisation
- Walls are 1.6 mm = exactly 4 perimeters of 0.4 mm, shared walls between cups are also 1.6 mm
- Floor is 2.0 mm = 10 layers at 0.2 mm
- Straight vertical walls, flat bottom: no supports, no overhangs, no bridges
- Suggested: 0.2 mm layer height, 4 walls, 2 bottom + 2 top layers, 10–15% infill (walls carry the strength), print upright
- Optional: print in a two-tone silk PLA — the U1 multi-tool setup suits colour swaps by layer height
