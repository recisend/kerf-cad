## Why this design (research, 2026-11-08)
- Third-party seller guides (not Etsy's own data) point to gifts, home decor and personalized items as the steady 3D-print sellers. Nursery and holiday decor searches spike Sept-Nov.
- Plain desk organizers are crowded (about 54k listings for "3d printed organizer", search volume reported down about 33% year on year).
- A lantern is a seasonal gift and decor item, so it fits the holiday window. Etsy reportedly wants original designs, and this one is.
- I could not verify real Etsy sales data. Check Etsy's own search and trend tools before pricing.

## Printer fit: Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle
- Size 55.4 x 64 x 70 mm, well inside the 270 x 270 x 270 mm build volume.
- Printed upright with a flat bottom. No supports: window tops are 45 deg peaks and the cavity is open at the top.
- Walls are 1.6 mm = 4 perimeters at 0.4 mm line width. The floor is 3.0 mm = 15 layers at 0.2 mm.
- 0.8 mm chamfer on the bottom edge to reduce elephant foot.
- Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca): 0.2 mm layer, 4 walls, 15 percent gyroid infill or less (mostly walls), PLA. Translucent or silk PLA glows well. Use a color change or a second tool for a gradient, which suits the U1 toolchanger.
- Estimated weight about 25 g in PLA (volume 20,247 mm3). Slice it for the real print time.

## Use
For flameless LED tealights only (about 38 mm diameter). Do not use real flames in a PLA print. State this in the listing.
