# Twist Hex Planter + Saucer (2026-11-25)

Original design (no real-world/branded item). Trend basis: planters/home decor and desk/gift items are top Etsy 3D-print categories in gift season (see sources in session summary).

## Printer: Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm stainless nozzle, Snapmaker Orca / OrcaSlicer
- Plate: planter 88x77x90 mm + saucer 68x78x8 mm, ~173 mm wide, fits 270^3 volume.
- Supports: none. Max overhang ~13 deg from vertical, flat bottoms.
- Layer height 0.20 mm, wall generator Arachne, 3 perimeters min (wall = 2.0 mm = 5 lines @0.4), 3 top / 4 bottom layers, 0 infill is fine (walls only) or 10% gyroid.
- "Spiral vase" mode NOT used: planter has a floor + drain hole; saucer is separate.
- Brim recommended by Snapmaker for U1 toolhead rails (3-5 mm) if slicing own 3MF.
- Material: PLA / PLA Silk (planter), PLA matte (saucer). Parts carry colors in the 3MF.
- Est. filament ~73 g total (58 g planter + 15 g saucer).
- Drain hole 8 mm in the planter floor, saucer catches water.
