# Hex Hive Desk Organizer (2026-11-14)

Original design, not based on any real product, brand or character.
Seven honeycomb cells of staggered height (60 to 100 mm) for pens, brushes, scissors and rulers.

**Files:** `hex-hive-desk-organizer.3mf` (print file, mm), `.kerf.json` (editable in Kerf), `preview.png`.

## Print orientation and settings (starting point, Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm)

- Orientation: as exported, flat on the bed. Vertical walls, flat bottom. **No supports needed.**
- Nozzle 0.4 mm, layer height 0.2 mm (0.16 mm for a nicer finish).
- Walls: 3 perimeters is enough for the outer wall. Shared walls fill themselves.
- Top layers: 4. Bottom layers: 5 to 6 (the 2 mm floor is 10 layers at 0.2 mm).
- Infill: 10 to 15% gyroid. The floor is the only thick solid area.
- Brim: 5 mm, since the tall narrow cells have a small footprint relative to height.
- Material: PLA or PETG. Single colour, any toolhead. For a multicolour version, change colour at the 60, 70, 80 and 90 mm layers (a tool/filament change at a layer height, no model edits needed).
- The 3MF contains only geometry and unit information (mm). Slicer profile settings are not embedded, so load the Snapmaker U1 0.4 mm profile in Snapmaker Orca and apply the values above.
- Not checked on a printer yet. Do a test print before selling.
