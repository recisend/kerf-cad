# Hexa Frost Ornament (2026-11-11)

Original abstract six-point star ornament, 69 x 80 x 3.2 mm. Not based on any real product, brand, or character.
Why: Nov-Dec is peak season for holiday ornaments/gifts, and multi-colour flat prints are quick, support-free and cheap to make.

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle settings
- File: `hexa-frost-ornament.3mf`, 2 parts: **Frame** (white) and **Inlay** (red, centre disc). Assign each part to a different U1 toolhead.
- Layer height 0.2 mm (3.2 mm = exactly 16 layers); first layer 0.2 mm.
- Walls 3 x 0.4 mm (1.2 mm); top/bottom 4 layers; infill 15-20% (part is nearly solid anyway).
- Orientation: flat on the bed, no supports, no brim needed. Hanging hole is 3.6 mm.
- Smallest feature: 1.6 mm slot walls / 3.2 mm slot width, all >= 4 extrusion widths of 0.4 mm.
- Colour change by toolhead: inlay prints as its own object, so expect a small prime tower. Keep purge volume low.
- Plate fit: ~20 ornaments per U1 plate (270 x 270 mm).
