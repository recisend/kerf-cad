# Honeycomb Hex Desk Organizer  (2026-11-23)

Original design (no real-world product/brand/character). Pen & tool cup, hexagonal, 2 compartments, honeycomb window on one face.

## Geometry
- 90 x 78 x 100 mm (fits Snapmaker U1 270 mm bed), ~71.7 cm3 -> roughly 85-90 g PLA
- Walls 2.0 mm (5 x 0.4 mm lines), floor 2.4 mm, divider 2.0 mm x 65 mm tall
- Honeycomb windows: flat-top hexagons, 2.4 mm webs (no steep overhangs)
- 0.8 mm bottom chamfer to avoid elephant foot; flat base, no supports needed

## Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle, PLA)
- Layer 0.2 mm, line width 0.42 mm, 3 walls (or 5 walls = solid 2.0 mm), top/bottom 4 layers
- Infill 10-15% (walls are nearly solid anyway), supports OFF, brim OFF
- Orient as exported (flat bottom on bed)
