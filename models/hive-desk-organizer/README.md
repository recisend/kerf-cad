# Hive Desk Organizer (original design, 2026-11-23)

Three fused hexagonal cups (95 / 75 / 58 mm tall) with a U-shaped cable notch in the short cup. Original geometric design, not based on any real-world item or brand.

## Why this product
Market research (directional only, from seller blogs, not official Etsy data):
- Desk organizers and cable management are among the most-searched functional 3D-printed categories on Etsy in 2026 (work-from-home buyers), medium competition.
- Personalized/desk gifts are positioned as strong for the holiday season; Etsy requires original designs.
- Caveat: viral niches fade fast; planters/vases sell poorly as printed items.
Sources: insightagent.app/guides/popular-3d-printed-items-etsy, hyper3d.ai/blog/best-selling-3d-printed-items, printful.com/blog/leading-trends-for-etsy-sellers

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle optimisation
- Size 97 x 98 x 95 mm (U1 volume 270^3), prints flat on the bed, no supports needed.
- Wall 1.6 mm (= 4 x 0.4 mm lines), floor 2.0 mm, shared walls 3.2 mm.
- 0.8 mm chamfer on the bottom edge to avoid elephant-foot squish.
- All walls vertical, no overhangs/bridges; cable notch is open at the top.
- Volume about 69 cm^3 (about 85 g PLA at 100% density; far less with 15% infill and 4 walls).

## Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca)
0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layer, 4 walls, 3 top / 4 bottom layers, 15% gyroid infill, no supports, seam aligned/back. Multi-colour: use the 4 toolheads to alternate colours per cup via height-range colour changes or separate prints.
