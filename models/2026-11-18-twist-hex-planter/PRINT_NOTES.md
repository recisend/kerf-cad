# Twist-Hex Planter (original design)

Original abstract geometric planter, not based on any real product, brand or character.

- Printer: Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle (build volume 270 mm cube; part is 92 x 80 x 80 mm)
- Format: 3MF (mm)
- Orientation: print as-is, open end up, no supports (flare is about 9 degrees from vertical)
- Walls 2.4 mm (6 x 0.4 mm lines), floor 2.4 mm, 8 mm drain hole
- Suggested: 0.2 mm layers, 3 to 4 walls, 10% gradient or 15% infill, PLA ~64 g
- Not yet listed anywhere. Publishing to Etsy needs the owner's approval.

Trend basis (vendor blog roundups, not Etsy sales data): home decor and planters are repeatedly listed among top 3D print categories.
