# Hexa Hive Desk Organizer — 2026-11-21

Original design (7 honeycomb cells, staggered heights). Not based on any real product, character or brand.

**Why this one:** desk/organizer items and fidget-style functional pieces had the best-supported 2026 Etsy/eBay trend signals; planters/vases were weaker. Etsy requires original designs for 3D-printed listings, so this is original geometry.

## Printer: Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle
- Size: 96.4 x 93.1 x 95 mm (fits the 270 mm bed); ~81 cm3 of material.
- Orientation: as exported (flat bottom on bed, open top). No supports, no overhangs, no bridges.
- Walls: 1.6 mm = exactly 4 perimeters at 0.4 mm line width. Floor: 2.4 mm = 12 layers at 0.2 mm.
- Suggested slicer settings: 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 5 top/bottom layers, no supports, brim optional, PLA or PETG.
