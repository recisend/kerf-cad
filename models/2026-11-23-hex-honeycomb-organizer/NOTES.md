## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm optimisation
- Size 121.5 x 117.2 x 95 mm (U1 bed is 270 mm cube) - lots of room; you can fit several only with a larger plate, so one per plate.
- Walls 1.6 mm = exactly 4 x 0.4 mm lines (no gap-fill / thin-wall artefacts). Floor 2.0 mm = 10 layers at 0.2 mm.
- Flat bottom on the bed, all walls vertical: no supports, no overhangs, no bridges.
- Recommended: 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layer, 4 walls, 15% infill (walls are the whole part), PLA ~130 g.
  Orient as exported (open end up). Use Snapmaker Orca. Multi-colour: U1 has 4 toolheads, so colour per cell or by layer height is possible.
- Single-colour part colour tag in the file: #e8a838 (PLA).
