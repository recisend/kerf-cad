# Hex Hive Desk Organizer (2026-11-09)

Original honeycomb desk organizer: seven hexagonal cups at stepped heights (45–90 mm) fused into one print. It is not based on any real product, brand or character.

**Files**
- `hex-hive-desk-organizer.3mf` is the print file (millimetres, one watertight part, 427 vertices / 850 triangles, 0 open edges).
- `hex-hive-desk-organizer.kerf.json` is the editable Kerf source.
- `preview.png` is a render.

**Size:** 111.1 × 107.2 × 90 mm. Volume is 83.9 cm³, about 104 g of PLA at 100% solid. With the slicer settings below, expect roughly 70–90 g.

## Designed for Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle
| Design choice | Why |
|---|---|
| Wall 1.6 mm (4 × 0.4 mm lines) | Whole number of perimeters, so no gap-fill and no thin-wall artefacts. The shared wall between cups is also 1.6 mm. |
| Floor 2.0 mm (10 layers at 0.2 mm) | Whole number of layers. Solid, no bridging. |
| Straight vertical walls | No overhangs, so no supports. The cup openings face up. |
| Flat base, prints upright | Best surface finish and strength on the largest face. |
| Fits easily in the U1 bed (270 × 270 × 270 mm) | Room for a brim, or two copies per plate. |

**Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca):** 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layer height, 4 wall loops, 3–4 top/bottom layers, 15% gyroid infill (the model is almost all walls, so infill barely matters), no supports, brim optional, elephant-foot compensation about 0.15 mm.

**Colour:** the file is saved in PLA amber (`#e8a33d`). Single colour is the safest. The U1's four toolheads could be used for a gradient with a layer-height colour change, which sells well as a "rainbow hive" variant.
