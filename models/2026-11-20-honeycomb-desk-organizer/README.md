# Honeycomb Desk Organizer (original design)

Original geometric 7-cell hexagonal desk organizer (pens, brushes, tools, small items). Not based on any real product, character, or brand.

- File: `honeycomb-desk-organizer.3mf` (mm, single part, PLA colour #e0a526)
- Source: `honeycomb-desk-organizer.kerf.json` (open in Kerf CAD)
- Size: 120.7 x 116.5 x 95 mm (fits Snapmaker U1's 270 x 270 x 270 mm volume with room for a brim)
- Volume ~103 cm3, ~128 g PLA

## Printer optimisation: Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle
- Walls 1.6 mm = exactly 4 x 0.4 mm perimeters; floor 2.4 mm = 12 layers at 0.2 mm.
- Flat base, vertical walls: no supports, no overhangs. Print upright, as exported.
- Suggested: 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 15% infill (floor is solid anyway), no supports, 5 mm brim optional, textured PEI plate.
- Multi-colour option: U1 has 4 toolheads; paint or colour-split cells by height in the slicer.

## Why this design (trend research, 2026-11-20)
Seller guides report that generic commodity items (flexi animals, phone stands) have collapsed in price, while functional, desk-decor, and personalisable items hold value. This is a functional desk-decor item with room to personalise (colour combos, name plate).
Caveat: sources are seller/printer-vendor blogs with unverified numbers; Etsy shows no sales ranking, so "trending" is directional, not proven.
