## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle profile
- Size 114.3 x 110 x 100 mm (U1 bed 270 x 270 x 270) – fits easily; one part, flat bottom, no supports needed.
- Walls 1.6 mm outer (4 perimeters @ 0.4), 3.2 mm shared walls, 2.4 mm floor (12 layers @ 0.2).
- Settings: 0.4 nozzle, 0.20 mm layer, 4 walls, 3-4 top/bottom layers, 10-15% infill, no supports, no brim.
- Multicolour (U1 toolchanger): paint/assign colours per layer or use colour change at heights; model is a single body.
- Volume ~114 cm3 (~140 g PLA at solid; far less with 10% infill).
- Print orientation: as modelled (open end up). Vase-mode NOT suitable (shared walls).
