- Walls 2.0 mm = 5 perimeters of 0.4 mm lines; shared walls between cells are also 2.0 mm.
- Floor 1.6 mm = 8 layers at 0.2 mm.
- Flat base, vertical walls, open top: no supports, no overhangs, no bridges.
- Volume ~75.7 cm3 (about 94 g in PLA at 100% solid; use 15% infill or fewer, the walls are mostly perimeters).
- Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca): 0.2 mm layer, 0.4 mm nozzle, 5 walls, 4 top/bottom layers, 15% gyroid infill, no supports, brim optional.
- Multi-colour idea (U1 has 4 toolheads): swap filament at layer heights ~300 / ~375 for a banded look on the tallest cell.
