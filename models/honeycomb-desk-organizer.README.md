# Honeycomb Desk Organizer (2026-11-25)

Original design, not based on any real product, brand or character.
Source: `honeycomb-desk-organizer.kerf.json`. Print file: `honeycomb-desk-organizer.3mf` (mm, single body).

## Why this model
Desk organizers and personalised or "workspace" accessories are the most consistently supported
best-seller category in current 3D-print-on-Etsy guides (Snapmaker blog, Insight Agent, Hyper3D).
These are seller guides, not Etsy-published data, so treat the ranking as directional.
Etsy requires 3D-printed items to come from the seller's own design, which this is.

## Dimensions
- Footprint 126.4 x 121.9 mm, height 100 mm (U1 volume is 270 x 270 x 270 mm).
- 7 hex cells, 40 mm across flats inside. Heights 60/60/80/80/80/100/100 mm.
- Shared wall 1.6 mm (= 4 x 0.4 mm lines), floor 2.0 mm (= 10 layers at 0.2 mm).
- All Z dimensions are multiples of 0.2 mm. No overhangs, so no supports.
- About 143 g of PLA by Kerf's estimate (before infill settings).

## Snapmaker U1 settings (0.4 mm nozzle)
- Printer/nozzle: Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm. Layer height 0.2 mm. Orientation: as exported (open end up).
- Walls: 4 loops (1.6 mm). Top layers 0 (open top), bottom layers 5. Infill: 10% (floor only).
- Supports: off. Brim: optional, 3-5 mm, for tall thin cells.
- PLA: nozzle ~220 C, bed 60 C. Matte or neutral colours suit the desk-accessory trend.
- Optional multicolour: the U1 has 4 toolheads, so the 6 outer cells can be painted per cell in the slicer.
