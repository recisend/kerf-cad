- Wall thickness 1.6 mm = 4 perimeters at 0.4 mm line width; shared inner walls are also 1.6 mm.

2-3 top / 3 bottom layers (no real infill needed - walls dominate), 15% gyroid infill, no supports,
brim optional. Approx. volume 83.8 cm3 (about 100 g PLA at solid; less with sparse infill).
