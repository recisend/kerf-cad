## Why this one
- Seasonal timing: early November is when Christmas ornament / stocking-stuffer / holiday-decor demand ramps up on Etsy. The sources found (seller guides, not official Etsy data) list ornaments, stocking stuffers and small themed gifts as recurring holiday sellers.
- Original geometric design, no brands, characters or real-world items (no licensing risk).
- Flat, support-free, ~25 min print: easy to batch several per plate and multi-colour.

## Print setup (Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle)
- Size: 76.4 x 94.0 x 3.0 mm (fits the 270 mm bed; ~9 per plate)
- Orientation: flat on bed as modelled, no supports, no brim
- Layer height 0.20 mm (15 layers), line width 0.42, 3 walls, 100% infill (part is only 3 mm)
- Smallest features: 4.8 mm branches, 1.5 mm wall around the 3.5 mm hanging hole (all >= 3x nozzle width)
- Material: PLA. Tip: white or pearl PLA, or swap filament at a layer for a two-colour version on the U1.
- Export: 3MF, millimetres (`snowflake-ornament.3mf`)
