# Honeycomb Desk Organizer — print notes (Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle)

Original design, no brand/product/IP references and no dependence on fitting any real item.

- File: `honeycomb-organizer.3mf` (mm). Source: `honeycomb-organizer.kerf.json`.
- Size: 122.6 x 99.7 x 105 mm (U1 bed 270^3: fits with room to spare). ~108 g PLA at solid volume.
- Geometry tuned for 0.4 mm nozzle: 1.6 mm walls (4 perimeters x 0.4), 2.0 mm floor (10 layers @ 0.2),
  all heights are multiples of 0.2 / 0.4 (60, 75, 90, 105), flat base, vertical walls, no overhangs, no supports.
- Suggested slicer (Snapmaker Orca): 0.2 mm layer, 4 walls, 5 bottom layers, 3 top layers (open top),
  infill irrelevant (walls ~solid), no supports, brim off, PLA ~220C / bed 60C.
- Multicolour option on U1: change filament per cell/height via colour-change at layer heights, or print single colour.
