- Infill 10-15% (walls are nearly all perimeters), no supports, no brim needed (flat base)
- Flat on bed as exported, no overhangs. Cell wall 1.2 mm, hex across-flats 34 mm inside.
- Estimated filament ~70 g PLA (about 8-10 h). Check in slicer.
