# Hex Trio Desk Organizer

Original, generic design (no real-world product, brand or character). Three
joined hexagonal cups of different heights (90 / 70 / 50 mm) for pens, brushes,
makeup tools or cables. Modular "honeycomb" desk organizers are a steady
Etsy category (see sources in the run notes); trend data from blog sources is
self-reported, so check Etsy search suggestions before pricing.

## Files
- `hex-trio-desk-organizer.3mf` – print file (mm)
- `hex-trio-desk-organizer.kerf.json` – editable Kerf CAD source

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle profile
- Size: 74.6 x 75.6 x 90 mm (fits the U1 build volume easily)
- Walls: 1.6 mm = 4 x 0.4 mm perimeters; shared walls between cups are 1.6 mm
- Floor: 2.0 mm = 10 layers at 0.2 mm (use 0.2 mm layer height; all heights are multiples of 0.2)
- No overhangs or bridges, flat base, no supports needed, no brim needed
- Suggested slicer: 4 walls, 5 top/bottom layers not needed (open top), 10-15 % infill (mostly irrelevant, walls fill the part), 0.2 mm layers, PLA ~215 C
- Colour tip: single-colour PLA/PETG silk or matte; the U1 multi-colour option could do a different colour per cup (tool changes cost time)
