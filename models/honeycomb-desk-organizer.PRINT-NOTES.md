# Honeycomb Desk Organizer: print notes (2026-11-24)

Original design, not based on any real product, brand or character.

**Printer:** Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle. Bed 270 x 270 x 270 mm, and this part is 114.3 x 110 x 80 mm.

## Geometry (designed for 0.4 mm)
- Outer walls 1.6 mm (4 perimeters at 0.4). Shared walls between cells 3.2 mm (8 perimeters).
- Floor 2.0 mm (10 layers at 0.2 mm).
- Flat bottom, vertical walls, no overhangs, no bridges, **no supports needed**.
- 7 hex cells, heights 80 / 70 / 60 mm.
- Volume about 109.8 cm3.

## Suggested slicer settings
- Layer height 0.2 mm (0.16 mm for a finer finish).
- 4 walls, 3 to 4 top/bottom layers, infill 10 to 15% gyroid (walls are nearly solid anyway).
- Supports off, brim off. Prints flat on the bed in the orientation exported.
- PLA, PETG or ASA. Silk or matte PLA looks best for listing photos.
