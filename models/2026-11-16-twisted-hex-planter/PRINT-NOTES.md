# Twisted Hex Planter (original design) — 2026-11-16

Original abstract geometric design; not a copy of any real product, brand or character.

- Size: 82 x 80 x 80 mm, ~53 g PLA (est.), 6-sided twisted lofted wall (48 deg twist), 2 mm wall, 3 mm floor, 6 mm drain hole.
- File: `twisted-hex-planter.3mf` (millimetres). Editable source: `twisted-hex-planter.kerf.json`.

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle settings
- Fits easily in the 270 mm build volume; print upright, flat base on the plate. No supports (lean stays well under 45 deg).
- 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layer height, 0.42 mm line width. Orca/Snapmaker Orca: 3 walls (or "vase mode" would NOT work: it has a floor + drain hole), 4 top/bottom layers, 10-15% infill (floor only).
- Wall 2.0 mm = ~4-5 perimeters at 0.4 mm; keep "seam" aligned/rear or scarf so it sits on a hex corner.
- Suggested filament: PLA / PLA silk / PLA marble. Silk gold/copper/pearl shows the twist best.
