# Honeycomb Desk Organizer (original design, 2026-11-27)

Original abstract geometric design — no real products, brands or characters.
Why: Etsy/seller guides repeatedly list desk organizers and geometric/parametric
home decor among consistent sellers. (Sources were blog posts, not hard sales
data — treat "trending" as directional.)

## Printer profile: Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle
- Size 101.5 x 82.6 x 95 mm (fits 270^3 build volume), single colour.
- Wall 1.6 mm (4 perimeters @ 0.4), floor 1.6 mm (8 layers @ 0.2). Pocket cuts
  keep the 1.6 mm wall everywhere, including shared walls.
- No overhangs, no bridges, no supports. Print upright, flat bottom on bed.
- Suggested: 0.2 mm layer, 4 walls, 15% infill (walls are nearly solid anyway),
  PLA. ~56 cm3 of material (~70 g PLA).
- Multi-colour option: swap filament per layer/height or paint cells per extruder.
