## Model
- Size: 83 x 96 x 74 mm (W x D x H). Fits the Snapmaker U1 (270 mm cube) with plenty of room: ~6 per plate.
- Hollow, open at the base. It stands over a standard LED tealight (~38 mm), and the diamond windows let the light out.
- Wall 1.2 mm = 3 perimeters at 0.4 mm. Model volume ~16.3 cm3 (about 20 g of PLA).
- Ledges overhang <= 4 mm and the diamond windows have 45-degree tops, so it prints with **no supports**.

## Snapmaker U1 print settings (0.4 mm nozzle)
- Orient with the open base on the bed (as exported, Z = 0 is the bed).
- Layer height 0.2 mm, line width 0.42 mm, 3 walls, 0% infill (walls only), 4 top / 4 bottom layers.
- Supports: off. Brim: not needed (large flat base ring).
- Material: PLA or PLA-silk for the best glow; use a flame-free LED tealight only.
- U1 tool-changer tip: print different tree colours on different plates, or use a single colour per tier via a colour change at z = 18, 36 and 54 mm (tier joints).

## Checks and caveats
- Export is 3MF in millimetres, with the base at z = 0.
- Mesh volume matches the CAD volume, but a strict edge check shows ~500 unmatched edges at tier ledges (tessellation T-junctions). Open it in Snapmaker Orca and confirm the slice preview looks clean before photographing or listing.
- A small sliver of geometry is visible at the top tier ledge (z ~ 54-55 mm). Check it in the slicer preview.
- Not tested on a physical printer. Print one sample first.
