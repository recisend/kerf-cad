# Frosted Fir Tealight Lantern — print notes (Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle)

Original design (generic tiered fir-tree lantern, no real products, characters or brands).
Why this product: December demand on Etsy favours Christmas decor, tea-light/LED houses & lanterns, ornaments and stocking stuffers (see session notes; seller-guide data is directional, not verified Etsy sales).

- Size: 68 x 68 x 62 mm (fits the 270^3 mm U1 bed; ~16 fit per plate if you batch)
- Orientation: open base on the bed, as exported. No supports (all overhangs >= 45 deg).
- Nozzle 0.4 mm, layer 0.16-0.20 mm, line width ~0.42-0.45 mm
- Wall 1.6 mm = 4 perimeters (0.4 mm lines), 0 % infill is irrelevant (walls only), 3-4 top layers
- Filament: white / warm-white / natural PLA (glows best), 1.75 mm. Print 210-220 C nozzle, 55-60 C bed (check your spool)
- Use for LED tea lights ONLY (PLA softens at ~55 C). Include this warning in the listing.
- Inner clearance for a 38 mm LED tea light: inner radius >= 24 mm over the first 16 mm of height.
- 4-colour idea (U1 toolheads): tiers in different greens + top in warm white; needs per-tier colour changes in the slicer.
- 3MF is geometry-only (mm). Load in Snapmaker Orca and pick the U1 / 0.4 nozzle profile; slicer settings are not embedded.
