# Celestial Moon Ornament — Snapmaker U1 print notes

Original design (no real-world product, brand, character or logo). Made in Kerf CAD, 2026-11-24.

**Size:** 73.7 x 80 x 3.0 mm | **Volume:** ~6.4 cm3 | **Est. filament:** ~8 g PLA | **File:** `celestial-moon-ornament.3mf` (mm)

## Why it suits the U1 + 0.4 mm nozzle
- Fits the 270 x 270 x 270 mm bed with huge margin; ~12 pieces fit in one plate for batch runs.
- Lies flat on the bed: no supports, no overhangs, no bridges.
- 3.0 mm thickness = 15 layers at 0.2 mm (clean layer multiple).
- Hanging hole 4.0 mm dia, ring wall 4.0 mm (>= 9 perimeters of 0.45 mm).
- Star cutouts keep >= 2.6 mm wall to every edge; smallest star is 7.2 mm across.

## Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca / OrcaSlicer, U1 0.4 mm profile)
- Layer height 0.20 mm, first layer 0.20 mm, line width 0.42-0.45 mm
- Walls: 3 perimeters, top/bottom 4-5 layers (practically solid at 3 mm)
- Infill: 100% not required; 15% gyroid is fine, parts are nearly all skin
- Supports: OFF | Brim: none (use 3 mm mouse ears only if corners lift)
- PLA ~215 C nozzle / 60 C bed, textured PEI plate
- Colours: black PLA body is the main look. Optional: matte black, or silk gold/silver
  for a premium variant. U1 multi-tool: swap to gold for last 0.4 mm of height
  (colour change at layer 14) for a gold-edged top.
