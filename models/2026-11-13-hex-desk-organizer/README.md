## Geometry
- 7 hex cells, 24 mm across flats inside; footprint 76.8 x 74.1 mm; heights 80 / 60 / 44 mm (centre / ring A / ring B).
- Walls 1.2 mm (3 x 0.4 mm lines), floor 1.6 mm (8 layers at 0.2 mm). All vertical walls and a flat bottom, so no supports and no overhangs.
- Fits a 270 x 270 x 270 mm Snapmaker U1 build volume with plenty of room. About 36.2 cm3 of material, roughly 45 g in PLA.
- Verified: the exported mesh has no open or non-manifold edges and the signed volume matches Kerf's volume (36,215 mm3).

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle settings (suggested starting point)
- Nozzle 0.4 mm, layer height 0.2 mm, line width 0.42 mm.
- Walls: 3 perimeters, 0% infill is fine because the part is all thin walls; use 3 or more top and bottom layers.
- No supports, no brim; enable elephant-foot compensation of about 0.1 mm in the slicer.
- Print upright on the bed as exported. PLA at the filament vendor's recommended temperatures.
- Print-test one on your own U1 before listing. I could not run a real slice or print here.
