# Hex Desk Organizer (original design) — 2026-11-13

Printer: Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle. File: `hex-desk-organizer.3mf` (mm, 1 part).
Size: 72.7 x 84 x 90 mm (fits the 270 mm bed with room to spare). Prints upright, no supports.

## Why it is printable for 0.4 mm
- Walls 2.0 mm = 5 perimeters at 0.4 mm; divider 2.0 mm; floor 2.0 mm (10 layers at 0.2 mm).
- Grooves 1.2 mm wide (3 lines) and 0.8 mm deep, leaving a 1.2 mm wall behind them.
- All surfaces vertical or flat: 0 overhangs, 0 bridges, no supports.

## Suggested slicer settings
0.2 mm layers, 4-5 walls, 15% gyroid infill (mostly walls anyway), 3 bottom / 4 top layers,
no supports, slight elephant-foot compensation (0.1-0.15 mm), seam on a vertex (back corner).
Printed in one color; try silk or matte PLA. Divider is 56 mm tall (pens tall side / clips small side).
