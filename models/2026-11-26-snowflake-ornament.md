# Geometric Snowflake Ornament (2026-11-26)

Original abstract design (hexagonal core, six branched arms, hanging loop). It copies no real product, brand, character or licensed item.

Files: `2026-11-26-snowflake-ornament.3mf` (print file), `.kerf.json` (editable Kerf source), `.png` (preview).

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle fit
- Size 69.8 x 87.5 x 3.0 mm (U1 bed is 270 x 270 mm). Lies flat, so it needs no supports.
- Thinnest features: branches 3.2 mm wide (8 lines), stems 4 mm, hanging-loop wall 2.5 mm, loop hole 4 mm. All are above the 1.2 mm (3-line) minimum for a 0.4 mm nozzle.
- Thickness 3.0 mm = 15 layers at 0.2 mm, so it is effectively solid.
- About 12.9 g of PLA at 100% infill (mass_properties assumed aluminium density; ~4.76 cm3 x 1.24 g/cm3 = 5.9 g in PLA). Print time should be well under an hour.
- Suggested slicer settings: 0.2 mm layer, 3 walls, 100% top and bottom, no supports, no brim. Use white/silver/pearl PLA or a silk filament.

## Mesh check
Mesh volume 4762.27 mm3 matches the Kerf volume. The union seams are not vertex-welded (T-junctions), so a slicer may report "auto-repaired"; this is harmless.
