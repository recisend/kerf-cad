# Mushroom Pen Cup (2026-11-08)

Original design, not based on any real product, brand or character.

**Why this one:** research this run pointed to functional desk organizers and mushroom/cottagecore decor as the strongest 3D-print niches on Etsy. Those sources are mostly SEO blogs and vendor pages with one hard sales figure, so treat the trend as indicated, not proven. I could not find an Etsy trend report for holiday 2026.

## Printer profile: Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle
- Size: 100 x 100 x 79.5 mm (U1 volume is 270 mm cube, so it fits with room to spare).
- Print orientation: as modeled, flat base down. No supports: the cap underside is a 45 degree cone.
- Smallest wall: about 3.5 mm at the pen holes (roughly 8 perimeters at 0.4 mm).
- Suggested slicer settings: 0.2 mm layers, 3 to 4 walls, 10 to 15% gyroid infill, 4 top layers.
- Multicolor option: the U1 has 4 toolheads, so a color change at z = 36 mm (stem to cap) gives a classic two-tone look. Not done in the file yet.
- Volume is about 265 cm3 solid, so expect roughly 90 to 120 g of filament after infill (estimate, check in the slicer).
- Holes: 6 x 10 mm and 1 x 16 mm, 3 mm floor.
