# Honeycomb Desk Organizer (original design, 2026-11-15)

Original geometric design. It copies no real product, brand, character or logo.

## Print settings (Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle)
- Size: 71.7 x 82.8 x 85 mm. This fits the U1 bed (270 x 270 x 270 mm) with plenty of room. About 8 fit on one plate.
- Orientation: as exported, flat on the bed, opening up. No supports are needed because every wall is vertical.
- Wall thickness: 2.4 mm outer and 2.4 mm between pockets, which is 6 perimeters of 0.4 mm.
- Floor: 2.4 mm, which is 12 layers at 0.2 mm.
- Bottom edge: 0.8 mm chamfer to reduce elephant foot.
- Suggested slicer settings:
  - Layer height: 0.2 mm
  - Perimeters (walls): 6, or 4 with "wall generator: Arachne"
  - Top/bottom layers: 5 / 12
  - Infill: 10-15% gyroid (the walls carry nearly all of the strength)
  - Material: PLA or PETG
- Estimated print: roughly 150 g of material and about 6 hours. Slice it to confirm, because these figures are estimates.

## Export
- `honeycomb-desk-organizer.3mf`: millimetre units, a single watertight part (292 triangles, Euler characteristic 2).
- `honeycomb-desk-organizer.kerf.json`: the editable Kerf source.
