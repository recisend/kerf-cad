# Honeycomb Desk Organizer (original design, 2026-11-15)

Four interlocking hexagonal cups of stepped heights (105 / 85 / 85 / 65 mm) for pens, brushes, scissors, rulers.
Original geometric design — not based on any real product, brand, character or existing model.

## Printer fit: Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle
- Size: 127.5 x 103.6 x 105 mm (U1 build volume is 270 mm cube) — fits with room to spare.
- Wall 1.6 mm = exactly 4 perimeters of 0.4 mm; shared walls between cups also 1.6 mm. Floor 2.0 mm (10 layers at 0.2 mm).
- 0.6 mm bottom chamfer to reduce elephant foot. No overhangs, no bridges, no supports needed.
- Print flat as exported (open side up). Suggested: 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 10-15% infill, 3-4 top/bottom layers, PLA/PETG. Volume ~92.9 cm3 solid; expect roughly 45-60 g with those settings.
- Slice in Snapmaker Orca with the U1 / 0.4 mm profile (I did not slice here; check the preview before printing).
