# Scandi Tiered Christmas Tree — print notes (original design, no real-world item/brand)

- Size: 60 x 60 x 116 mm, solid body, flat base on bed. Fits Snapmaker U1 (270^3 mm) with room for ~6 per plate.
- Printer/nozzle: Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm. Import the 3MF in Snapmaker Orca, choose the U1 0.4 mm profile.
- Supports: none needed. Every overhang is <= 45 deg from vertical (tier undersides are 45 deg cones).
- Suggested: 0.20 mm layers, 3 walls, 12-15% gyroid infill, 4 top/bottom layers, brim not required (60 mm flat base), elephant-foot compensation 0.1-0.15 mm.
- Material: PLA (matte green / white / natural wood-fill look best). Est. ~119 cm3 solid, much less filament with sparse infill.
- Sizes to list: scale 60% (70 mm tall), 100%, 130% (150 mm tall). Multi-color: swap filament per tier on the U1 toolchanger.
- Trend rationale: seasonal holiday decor is cited by seller guides as a 3-5x volume spike in Nov-Dec; minimalist tiered trees print without supports. Sources are seller blogs, not Etsy data — check Etsy search before listing.
