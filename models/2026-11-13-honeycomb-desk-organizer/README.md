# Honeycomb Desk Organizer (2026-11-13)

Original parametric design (no real-world product, brand or character). 7 interlocking hex cells at three heights (76 / 56 / 40 mm) for pens, brushes, tools, cables.

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle optimisation
- Size 103.9 x 100 x 76 mm — fits the 270 mm build volume with room to spare.
- Print upright, flat floor on the bed: no supports, no overhangs, no bridges.
- Walls 1.2 mm (3 perimeters @ 0.4 mm line width); shared inner walls 2.4 mm. Floor 2.0 mm. All Z heights are multiples of 0.2 mm (0.2 mm layers).
- Suggested: Snapmaker Orca, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layer, 3 walls, 3 top/bottom layers, 10-15% infill, PLA. ~79 g solid-equivalent upper bound (63.4 cm3).
- Colour #e8a33d is embedded in the 3MF; reassign filament per tool in the slicer if desired (e.g. height-based colour change).
