## Snapmaker U1 print settings (0.4 mm nozzle)
- File: `honeycomb-desk-organizer.3mf` (mm, 83.1 x 84 x 90 mm, fits U1 bed with room to spare)
- Orientation: as exported, flat base on bed. No supports, no brim needed.
- Layer height 0.20 mm, line width 0.42 mm
- Walls: 2.4 mm (about 6 perimeters at 0.4 mm), floor 2.4 mm (12 layers)
- Top/bottom layers: 5, infill: 10-15% gyroid (walls do most of the work)
- Material: PLA or PETG. Weight about 88 g, so roughly 4-5 h plus. Estimate in the slicer.
- Tip: set 3 to 4 wall loops min and "ironing" off. Two-colour option: change filament at layer heights 40 mm and 60 mm for a stepped colour look (U1 multi-tool).
