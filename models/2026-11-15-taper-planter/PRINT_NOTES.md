# Taper Planter + Saucer (original design, no real-world/branded item)

Printer: Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle. File: `taper-planter-saucer.3mf` (2 parts, mm).

Design choices for 0.4 mm nozzle
- Wall 1.6 mm = 4 perimeters at 0.4 mm line width. Floor 2.0-2.4 mm.
- Walls flare outward ~8 deg (no supports needed), flat bottoms, 8 mm drain hole.
- Planter 80 x 80 x 70 mm, saucer 84 x 84 x 10 mm; both lie flat, fit the U1 bed.
- Saucer inner floor (r 32.9) clears planter base (r 30).

Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca): 0.4 nozzle, 0.2 mm layer, 4 walls, 15% gyroid or
0% infill is fine (thin wall), no supports, no brim; vase-friendly PETG/PLA, PETG for wet use.
Weight ~81 g planter + ~31 g saucer (aluminum density in Kerf; real PLA is ~45% of that).
