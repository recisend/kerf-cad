# Hex Planter + Saucer (2026-11-09)

Original abstract geometric design: not based on any real product, brand or character.

- **File:** `hex-planter-u1.3mf` (2 parts: planter + saucer, laid flat, 3MF in mm)
- **Source:** `hex-planter-u1.kerf.json` (parametric; wall thickness is the `#wall` variable)
- **Planter:** 84 x 73 x 75 mm. **Saucer:** 80 x 69 x 8 mm. Both fit the U1's 270 mm cube build volume.

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle choices
- Wall 1.6 mm = 4 perimeters at 0.4 mm line width. Floor is also 1.6 mm (8 layers at 0.2 mm).
- Flat faces down on the bed, no overhangs beyond ~6 deg from vertical, **no supports needed**.
- 6 mm drain hole is vertical, so nothing to bridge.

## Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca, PLA)
0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layer, 4 walls, 3 top / 4 bottom layers, 10-15% infill (walls carry the strength),
seam aligned to a hex corner. Optionally spiral/vase mode is NOT used because of the drain hole.
