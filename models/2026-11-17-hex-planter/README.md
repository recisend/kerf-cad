Original geometric design. It is not based on any real product, brand or character.

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle optimisation
- Walls are 1.6 mm (4 x 0.4 mm lines). Floors are 1.6 mm (8 layers at 0.2 mm). Saucer wall is about 1.85 mm.
- All Z heights are multiples of 0.2 mm (80 mm planter, 9.6 mm saucer, 1.6 mm floors).
- No supports needed. Walls lean outward about 11 deg from vertical, well under 45 deg.
- Flat bottoms sit on the bed. The 8 mm drain hole is vertical.
- Plate footprint is about 202 x 100 mm, inside the U1's 270 x 270 x 270 mm volume.
- Slicer (Orca-based Snapmaker Orca): 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layer height, 4 walls,
  2-3 bottom layers, 15% gyroid infill (floor is solid anyway), no supports, Arachne walls.
- Material: PLA or PETG.
  - Multi-colour option: use a different filament per part (planter / saucer).
  - Seasonal option: silk PLA.
- Estimated weight: planter about 45 g, saucer about 11 g (PLA, from the model volume).
