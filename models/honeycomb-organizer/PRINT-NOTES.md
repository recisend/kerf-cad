# Honeycomb Desk Organizer

Original, abstract design (no real-world product, brand or character). Built with Kerf CAD MCP.

- File to print: `honeycomb-organizer.3mf` (mm, 1 part, 449 verts / 897 tris)
- Editable source: `honeycomb-organizer.kerf.json` (open in Kerf web app)
- Size: 95.5 x 92.2 x 100 mm (Snapmaker U1 bed is 270 x 270 x 270 mm, so it fits with lots of room)
- Est. filament: ~82 cm3 solid, ~100 g PLA at 100%; far less with 2-3 walls and low infill (walls are solid anyway)

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle optimisation
- Wall thickness 1.6 mm = exactly 4 x 0.4 mm perimeters (no gap-fill artifacts)
- Floor 2.4 mm = 12 layers at 0.2 mm
- All walls vertical, no overhangs, no bridges -> **no supports**
- Flat bottom with 0.6 mm chamfer on the bed edge (reduces elephant's foot)
- Single part, one colour; can be printed as-is on any one of the 4 toolheads

## Suggested slicer settings (Orca/Snapmaker Orca)
- Nozzle 0.4, layer 0.2 mm, 4 walls, 0% support, infill 10-15% (floor only), brim not needed
- PLA 215/60 C (adjust to filament). Slower outer wall (~60 mm/s) for clean hex faces.
- Colour ideas (U1 multi-colour): swap filament at layer heights for a gradient look.
