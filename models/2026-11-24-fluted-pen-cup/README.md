# Fluted Pen Cup (2026-11-24)

Original design, not based on any real product, brand or character.

**Why this one:** guides on what sells in 3D prints (directional only, not verified Etsy rankings) repeatedly list desk organizers and geometric/ribbed decor among steady sellers. Fluted/ribbed cylinders are a popular look and print without supports.

## Files
- `fluted-pen-cup.3mf` – print file (mm, single part)
- `fluted-pen-cup.kerf.json` – editable Kerf source (variables: `od`, `h`, `wall`)
- `preview.png`, `preview-top.png` – renders

## Geometry
- 85.6 mm dia x 95 mm tall (fits the Snapmaker U1 build volume, 270 mm cube)
- 2.4 mm wall (6 lines at 0.4 mm), 3 mm floor, 24 vertical ribs (2.4 mm radius)
- No overhangs, no supports, flat base. ~94 cm3, ~117 g PLA by Kerf's estimate

## Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle; verify in your slicer)
- 0.20 mm layers, 0.42 mm line width, 3 walls, 4 bottom / 5 top layers, 10-15% infill (walls dominate)
- No supports, no brim needed; print as exported (open end up)
- PLA, 0.2 mm layer: roughly 5-6 h
