# Barrel Fluted Planter (2026-11-14)

Original design (not based on any real product, brand or character). Revolved barrel vase/planter with 16 sculpted flutes and a 6 mm drainage hole. Parametric source: `barrel-fluted-planter.kerf.json`. Print file: `barrel-fluted-planter.3mf` (millimetres).

## Geometry
- Size: 80 x 80 x 80 mm (fits the Snapmaker U1's 270 x 270 x 270 mm volume with room for ~9 per plate by area).
- Wall 2.5 mm (about 6 lines at 0.4 mm), floor 2.4 mm, drainage hole 6 mm.
- Max overhang about 21 degrees from vertical, so no supports. Flat base, prints upright.
- Mesh checked: closed, manifold, no degenerate triangles, volume about 48.8 cm3. Two stray sliver triangles from the flute-cutter seam were removed from the 3MF after export; the .kerf.json still contains that cutter, so re-export and clean again if you edit the model.

## Suggested U1 settings (0.4 mm nozzle; starting points, check in Snapmaker Orca)
- Layer 0.20 mm, 4 walls, 4 top / 4 bottom layers, infill 10-15 %, no supports, no brim.
- PLA or PETG; roughly 45-60 g.
- Seam on the back or aligned. A silk or matte PLA hides layer lines best on the curve.
