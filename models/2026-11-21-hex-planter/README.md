# Hex Planter + Drip Tray (original design, 2026-11-21)

Original geometric succulent/herb planter with matching drip tray. No real-world product, brand or character is modelled.

**Why this one:** the sources I could find list planters/vases and home-decor/desk items among consistent 3D-print sellers on Etsy (Hyper3D, Elegoo and Snapmaker guides). I found no hard Etsy sales counts for November 2026, so "trending" here means "consistently listed as a top category", not verified sales data.

## Files
- `hex-planter-set.3mf` – both parts side by side on one plate (mm), as two separate objects
- `hex-planter.kerf.json` – editable Kerf source
- `preview.png` – render

## Dimensions
- Planter: 72.7 x 84 x 75 mm, tapered hexagon (68 mm to 84 mm across corners), 1.8 mm walls, 1.8 mm floor, 6 mm drain hole
- Tray: 72.7 x 84 x 9 mm, 1.8 mm walls, 2.4 mm floor, fits the planter base with clearance
- Plate footprint about 145 x 84 mm, well inside the Snapmaker U1's 270 x 270 x 270 mm volume

## Slicer settings (Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle)
- Layer 0.2 mm, line width 0.42-0.45 mm
- Walls 4 (1.8 mm), top/bottom layers 5-6, infill 10-15 % (gyroid)
- No supports, no brim needed (outward wall lean is about 6 degrees)
- PLA or PETG, one or two colours

## Caveats
- The 3MF holds geometry and part colour only. Slice it in Snapmaker Orca to generate the real print profile.
- I did not slice or test-print it, so print a sample before listing.
- Nothing is published to Etsy. That needs your go-ahead.
