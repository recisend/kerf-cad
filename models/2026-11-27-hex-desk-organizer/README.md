## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle optimisation
- Size 111.1 x 107.2 x 90 mm (U1 build volume is 270 mm per axis) — fits with room to spare.
- All walls 1.6 mm = exactly 4 perimeters at 0.4 mm line width; shared walls between cells are also 1.6 mm.
- Floor 2.0 mm = 10 layers at 0.2 mm.
- Flat base, vertical walls, open cells: no supports needed, no overhangs, no bridges.
- Suggested slicer settings: 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 15% gyroid infill (mostly wall volume anyway), 3 top/bottom layers, PLA. Enable elephant-foot compensation (~0.1–0.15 mm) in the slicer.
- Model volume 87.3 cm3 (about 108 g in PLA, upper bound).
- Note: a 0.6 mm bottom chamfer was attempted but had no effect in Kerf, so it was left out.
