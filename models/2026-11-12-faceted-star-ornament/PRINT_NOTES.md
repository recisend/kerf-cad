## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle optimisation
- Prints flat on the bed: no supports, no overhangs, no bridges
- All heights are multiples of 0.2 mm (16 + 8 layers at 0.2 mm layer height; 3.2 / 1.6 mm)
- Smallest wall/rim: overlay inset from base is >= 5 mm; ring wall 1.9 mm (>= 4 perimeters of 0.4 mm)
- Two parts = two colours: assign base to one toolhead and overlay to another (U1 multi-tool, no purge-tower waste beyond colour change on a single layer boundary at z = 3.2 mm)
- Suggested: 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 100% top/bottom solid layers (part is nearly solid), 15% infill is irrelevant at this thickness, PLA ~215 C / bed 60 C
- Tips are sharp (~25 deg); expect slightly rounded tips at 0.4 mm - this is normal
