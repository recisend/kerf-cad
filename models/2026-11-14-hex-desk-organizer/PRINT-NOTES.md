# Hex Honeycomb Desk Organizer (2026-11-14)

Original design (no real-world product, brand or character). Four interlocking hex cups
at staggered heights (40 / 56 / 72 / 88 mm) for pens, brushes, scissors, tools.

- Size: 81.5 x 117.2 x 88 mm (fits the Snapmaker U1's 270 mm cube with lots of room; ~4 fit per plate)
- Printer: Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle
- Format: 3MF (`hex-honeycomb-desk-organizer.3mf`), source `.kerf.json`
- Designed for FDM, no supports, no overhangs:
  - Walls 1.6 mm = exactly 4 perimeters at 0.4 mm line width
  - Floor 2.4 mm = 12 layers at 0.2 mm layer height
  - All heights are multiples of 0.2 mm, flat bottom, vertical walls
- Suggested slicer settings: 0.2 mm layer, 4 walls, 3 top/bottom layers, 15% infill (barely used), PLA/PETG, no supports, brim optional.
- Colour idea for U1 multi-toolhead: one colour per cup (cut at the cup boundaries) or a gradient/silk filament.
