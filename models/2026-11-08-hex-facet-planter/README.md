# Hex Facet Planter (2026-11-08)

Original design, not a copy of any real product or branded item. A tapered hexagonal planter with a drainage hole, sized for small succulents.

## Why this design
Trend research (seller guides, Nov 2026) repeatedly names geometric planters, minimalist desk and home items, and personalised gifts as steady Etsy sellers. These guides are SEO-style and largely repeat each other, so treat them as directional rather than hard sales data. Etsy also expects 3D-printed listings to be original designs, which this one is.

## Geometry
- Footprint at base: hexagon, 60 mm across corners. Top: 84 mm across corners.
- Height: 70 mm. Bounding box 72.7 x 84 x 70 mm.
- Walls and floor: 2.0 mm (5 perimeters at 0.4 mm line width).
- Drain hole: 6 mm through the floor.
- Wall flare is about 9.7 degrees from vertical, so no support is needed. The base is flat.
- Volume: 33,550 mm3, about 42 g in PLA at 100% density.

## Snapmaker U1 settings (0.4 mm nozzle)
- Fits the U1 build volume (270 x 270 x 270 mm) with plenty of margin.
- Layer height 0.20 mm, line width 0.42 mm, 5 wall loops, 3 top and 3 bottom layers.
- Infill: not needed for the walls. Use 10-15% gyroid.
- Supports: none. Orientation: as modelled (flat bottom on the plate).
- Optional: a 4-toolhead print can use a second colour for the rim by adding a colour change at the top layers in the slicer.
- Vase-mode alternative: the model is closed at the base, so spiral vase mode works. Set the line width to 0.8 mm for a faster print.
