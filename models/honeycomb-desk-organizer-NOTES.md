# Honeycomb Desk Organizer (2026-11-24)

Original design, not based on any real product, brand or character.

## Why this one
Desk organizers, fidgets and personalized gifts are the categories web sources
list as strong 3D-print sellers. Those sources are blog posts, not Etsy sales
data. Etsy's live best-seller pages could not be checked from this environment,
so check them before you list. Light-up signs with logos were avoided on
purpose because they need a licence.

## Specs
- 7 hex cells, 3 tall (85 mm) and 4 short (60 mm), overall 121.6 x 117.3 x 85 mm
- Fits the Snapmaker U1 build volume (270 mm cube) with plenty of room
- Walls 1.6 mm (4 perimeters at 0.4 mm), floor 1.6 mm, flat bottom, no overhangs,
  no supports needed
- Single solid body, one colour (can be painted per cell in a slicer for multicolour)

## Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca, U1, 0.4 mm nozzle)
- Nozzle 0.4 mm (must match the printer touchscreen)
- Layer height 0.20 mm, 4 walls, 15% gyroid infill, 3 top layers
- PLA or PETG, no supports, no brim needed
- Volume is about 97.7 cm3 (roughly 120 g of filament at 100% solid; much less with sparse infill)
