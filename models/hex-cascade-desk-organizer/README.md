# Hex Cascade Desk Organizer

Original design (generic hexagon geometry, not based on any real product, brand or character).
Four honeycomb cells stepping down in height (96 / 76 / 56 / 36 mm): pens and brushes in the tall cells, clips and cards in the low one. Printed as one piece, no supports, no assembly.

- File: `hex-cascade-desk-organizer.3mf` (mm units, single object, flat on the bed)
- Source: `hex-cascade-desk-organizer.kerf.json` (open in Kerf; heights and walls are editable)
- Size: 113.6 x 163.5 x 96 mm (U1 bed is 270 x 270 x 270 mm)
- Volume: about 127 cm3, about 157 g in PLA

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle design choices
- Outer wall 2.4 mm = 6 perimeters; shared walls 3.2 mm = 8 perimeters; floor 2.0 mm = 10 layers at 0.2 mm.
- All heights are multiples of 0.2 mm, so layers land on the exact top.
- Vertical walls only: no overhangs, bridges or supports. Printed flat side down.
- 0.6 mm chamfer on the bottom edges to prevent elephant's foot.

## Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca)
Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layer height, PLA.
- Walls: 3 loops (the 2.4 mm outer wall fills with extra perimeters), 5 top and 5 bottom layers
- Infill: 10% gyroid (the part is nearly all wall, so infill barely matters)
- Supports: off. Brim: off. Seam: aligned or back, so it sits on a corner.
- Print time: roughly 8-10 hours. Check the slicer estimate before pricing.
- Multi-colour option on the U1: change filament at layer 0 vs the top 20 layers for a two-tone rim.
