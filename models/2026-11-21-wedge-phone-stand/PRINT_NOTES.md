# Wedge Phone Stand (2026-11-21)

Original geometric design. It is not based on any real-world product, brand or character.
It is a phone stand with a 65 degree viewing angle, an 18 mm slot (fits phones in cases) and a charging-cable notch.

- Size: 75 x 80 x 99 mm. This fits the Snapmaker U1 build volume (270 mm) with room to spare.
- Volume: about 126.7 cm3 solid (about 157 g PLA solid, roughly 45-55 g at 15% infill).
- Files: `wedge-phone-stand.3mf` (millimetres, single part) and the Kerf source `wedge-phone-stand.kerf.json`.

## Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle
- Orientation: as exported, flat on the bed. No supports are needed (the steepest overhang is 25 degrees from vertical).
- Layer height 0.20 mm, line width 0.42 mm, 3 walls (1.26 mm). The plate is 8 mm thick and the base is 10 mm.
- Top/bottom layers 5/4, infill 15% gyroid or cubic.
- PLA at about 215 C nozzle, 60 C bed. No brim is needed.
- Estimated print time is about 2.5-3.5 h at standard speed. This is an estimate, not a sliced result.
- The 3MF holds geometry only. Slicer profile settings are not embedded.
