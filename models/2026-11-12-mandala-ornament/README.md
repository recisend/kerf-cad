# Mandala Ornament (2026-11-12)

Original abstract design — not based on any real product, brand, character or object.

- Files: `mandala-ornament.3mf` (print file), `mandala-ornament.kerf.json` (editable Kerf source)
- Size: 90 x 98 x 4.2 mm (Snapmaker U1 bed: 270 x 270 x 270 mm), about 43 g of solid volume before infill settings

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle optimisation
- Prints flat on the bed: no supports, no overhangs, no bridges.
- Base 3.0 mm and rim/hub 4.2 mm are multiples of 0.2 mm layers (15 / 21 layers).
- Rim and hub ring walls are 2.8 mm and 2.6 mm wide (multiples of 0.4 mm line width).
- Smallest web between cut-outs is about 2 mm; hanging-loop ring is 3.6 mm wide. Nothing thinner than 0.8 mm.
- Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca): 0.4 mm nozzle (sync nozzle info on the touchscreen), 0.2 mm layer,
  3 walls, 4 top/bottom layers, 15% infill. Use any filament; two-colour (rim/hub in a second colour) works via a colour change at layer 16.

## Why this design
Seasonal décor and desk/gift items are the consistent Etsy signals (per category pages and third-party
guides, none dated November 2026). Flexi animals are called saturated, so this avoids them. Trend data is
directional, not confirmed sales figures.
