## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle print settings
- Size 114.3 x 110 x 100 mm (U1 bed 270 x 270 x 270) — prints in one piece, no supports.
- Flat base, vertical walls, hex-grid cells; 0.6 mm chamfer on the bottom edge to reduce elephant's foot.
- Walls 1.6 mm (= 4 perimeters at 0.4 mm line width), floor 2.0 mm (10 layers at 0.2 mm).
- Suggested: 0.2 mm layer, 4 walls, 3 bottom / 4 top layers, 10-15% infill (walls carry it), no supports, no brim.
- ~64 cm3 / ~80 g PLA. Contact area on bed is large; stable without a brim.
