# Hive Desk Organizer (2026-11-08)

Original design (no real-world product, brand or character). Three fused hexagonal cups, 75 / 95 / 55 mm tall.

- Overall size: 108.5 x 109.9 x 95 mm (fits the Snapmaker U1's 270 mm cube easily)
- Printer/nozzle: Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle
- Walls 2.4 mm (6 perimeters at 0.4 mm), floor 2.0 mm (10 layers at 0.2 mm)
- No overhangs, flat base, no supports needed
- File: `hive-desk-organizer.3mf` (mm units). Source: `hive-desk-organizer.kerf.json`

## Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca, set nozzle 0.4 on the printer screen too)
0.2 mm layer (Snapmaker's recommended; 0.1-0.3 optimal for 0.4), 3 walls minimum (4 for stiffer cups), 15% gyroid infill, 4 bottom / 4 top layers, supports off, textured PEI plate.
The 3MF holds geometry and colour only; the slicer settings are not embedded, so apply these when slicing.
