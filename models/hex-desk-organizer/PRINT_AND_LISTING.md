# Hex Tower Desk Organizer + Phone Dock (original design)

Original geometry (hexagonal pen cups + angled phone slot). Not based on any real product, brand, character or other IP.

## Files
- `hex-tower-desk-organizer.3mf` – print file (mm, Z-up, flat on bed, one part)
- `hex-tower-desk-organizer.kerf.json` – editable Kerf CAD source
- `preview.png` – render

## Dimensions
96.3 x 145.0 x 95.0 mm (Snapmaker U1 build volume 270 x 270 x 270 mm: fits with room to spare). About 182 g solid, roughly 80-100 g at 15% infill.

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle optimisation
- Walls are 2.0 mm (exactly 5 x 0.4 mm perimeters), floors 2.0 mm (10 layers at 0.2 mm), slot walls >= 3.7 mm.
- Every outer face sits flat on the bed, no supports needed: slot walls lean 70 deg from horizontal (about 20 deg overhang, well under 45 deg), cable hole is vertical.
- Suggested: 0.2 mm layers, 0.4 mm line width, 5 walls (2.0 mm), 15% gyroid/grid infill, 3 top layers, no supports, brim not needed (large flat base), PLA ~210 C / bed 60 C.
- Single filament by default; the single-colour model also works well as a two-colour print with a filament change at 2 mm.
- Slot is 16 mm wide horizontally (about 15 mm perpendicular), so it fits phones in thick cases and small tablets.
