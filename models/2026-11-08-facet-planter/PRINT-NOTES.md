# Facet Planter + Saucer (2026-11-08)

Original design, no real-world item, brand or character. Hexagonal succulent planter with drain hole and matching saucer.

## Files
- `facet-planter.3mf` - print file (2 parts, mm, plate layout: planter at origin, saucer beside it)
- `facet-planter.kerf.json` - editable Kerf source (variable `#wall`)
- `preview.png` - render

## Dimensions
- Planter: 77.9 x 90 x 75 mm, 8 mm drain hole, ~49 g PLA by Kerf estimate (solid-volume, real use depends on infill settings)
- Saucer: 90 x 104 x 12 mm, ~20 g PLA
- Both fit the Snapmaker U1 build volume (270 mm per axis) with room to spare.

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle settings
- Walls are 1.6 mm = exactly 4 perimeters at 0.4 mm line width, floors 1.6 mm = 8 layers at 0.2 mm.
- Layer height 0.2 mm, 3-4 walls, 10-15% infill (walls carry the strength).
- Flat bottom, vertical walls: no supports needed. Print the planter open end up.
- Optional: vase/spiral mode is not suitable (drain hole in the floor).
