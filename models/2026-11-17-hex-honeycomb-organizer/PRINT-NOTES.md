# Hex Honeycomb Desk Organizer (original design, 2026-11-17)

Not based on any real product, brand, or character. Generic hex-cell pen/desk organizer.

**Why this one:** research for Etsy 3D-print demand (sources are SEO blogs, so directional only) points to
desk/organization items and geometric/honeycomb looks; generic downloadable designs are saturated, so this is an original parametric design.
Verify demand in Etsy search / eRank / Marmalead before listing.

## Printer profile: Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle
- Size 89.5 x 86.4 x 95 mm (U1 volume 270^3) - fits with huge margin; ~66 cm3 solid.
- Walls 1.6 mm = exactly 4 x 0.4 mm perimeters; floor 2.0 mm = 10 layers @ 0.2 mm.
- All walls vertical, flat bottom: no supports, no bridges, no overhangs.
- Suggested: 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 3 top/bottom layers, 10-15% infill (walls are ~all perimeters), PLA/PETG.
- Multicolour (U1 toolchanger): add a colour change / paint-on colouring per cell height in the slicer.
- 3MF exported from Kerf (mm).
