## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle optimisation
- Walls 2.0 mm = 5 × 0.4 mm perimeters (exact multiple of line width); floors 2.4 mm = 12 layers at 0.2 mm.
- Saucer is 10 mm tall with 2.0 mm walls/2.4 mm floor.
- No supports required: planter taper is outward (~5.7° from vertical), drain hole is vertical, everything sits flat on the bed.
- Footprint 80 × 92 mm per part; whole plate ≈ 160 × 92 × 80 mm — well inside the U1 270 mm cube.
- Suggested slicer settings: 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, 5 walls, 15% gyroid/cubic infill (or 3 top / 4 bottom layers), seam on a hex corner, print planter and saucer together or separately.
- Drain hole Ø7 mm; saucer pocket clears the planter base by ~5 mm per side.
- Material: PLA/PETG (PETG recommended for wet use). Tip: use a different U1 toolhead colour for the saucer.
