## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle optimisation
- Footprint 111.1 x 107.2 x 100 mm: fits the U1's 270 x 270 x 270 mm volume with room for a brim; fine for the plate.
- Walls 1.6 mm = exactly 4 perimeters at 0.4 mm line width (shared walls between cells, no thin-wall gaps).
- Floor 2.4 mm = 12 layers at 0.2 mm; all cell floors sit flat on the bed.
- Fully vertical walls, no overhangs, no bridges: prints with **no supports**.
- Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker Orca): 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layer, 4 walls, 3 bottom / 4 top layers, 10-15 % infill (walls dominate), PLA or PETG. Use "Spiral vase" **off** (cells are joined). Est. ~124 g PLA.
- Multi-colour idea (U1 toolchanger): colour-swap per cell height or print the 85 mm cells in a contrasting colour.
