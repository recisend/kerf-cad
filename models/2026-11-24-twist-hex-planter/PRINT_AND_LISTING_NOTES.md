## Dimensions
- Planter: 97.8 x 99.5 x 90 mm, ~86 g PLA, 2.4 mm radial wall (about 5-6 perimeters at 0.4 mm), 3 mm floor, 8 mm drain hole. Fits a ~3" (75 mm) nursery pot / small succulent.
- Saucer: 104 x 104 x 10 mm, ~32 g PLA, 2.4 mm floor, 2.4 mm wall.

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle profile
- Nozzle 0.4 mm, layer height 0.20 mm (0.16 mm for smoother twist faces), first layer 0.20-0.25 mm.
- Walls: 3-4 perimeters (line width 0.42-0.45), top/bottom 5 layers, infill 10-15% gyroid (floor mostly solid).
- Supports: none. Max lean ~8 degrees from vertical, flat bottoms. Drain hole is a vertical bore, no bridging.
- Planter ("spiral vase" mode is NOT suitable: it has a floor and rim). Use seam = aligned/back or randomized to hide it on a corner.
- Colours (3MF part colours): planter #3f8f7a teal-green, saucer #e8e2d4 cream — one part per tool on the U1.
- PLA or PETG (PETG for wet use). Print the saucer in PETG if the plant is watered often.
- Orientation: as exported (Z up, both parts on bed).
