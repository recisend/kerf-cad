# Hex Hive Desk Cup (original design)

Abstract hexagonal pen/brush cup. Not based on any real product, brand or character.

- Printer: Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle. Size 72.7 x 84 x 90 mm (fits 270^3 build volume).
- Orientation: as exported (flat on bed, opening up). No supports: windows are pointy-top hexagons (60 deg roof).
- Walls 2.4 mm = 6 x 0.4 mm lines; floor 3.0 mm; window ribs >= 8 mm.
- Suggested: 0.2 mm layer, Arachne walls, 3 walls (rim/walls are 2.4 mm -> 6 perimeters fills solid), 15% infill (floor), no supports, brim 0.8-0.9 mm (U1 guidance), enable elephant-foot compensation ~0.15 mm.
- Est. mass ~69 g solid PLA; less with infill.
