# Hex Flute Pen Cup (original design, not based on any real product)

- Printer: Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers. Build volume 270 mm per side; the part is 69 x 80 x 90 mm.
- Orientation: print upright on its flat base. No supports needed; flute grooves open to the top so there are no roofs.
- Walls: 3.0 mm minimum, a multiple of the 0.4 mm line width. Floor 2.4 mm, a multiple of the 0.2 mm layer height.
- Suggested slicer settings: 3 to 4 walls, 15% gyroid or 3 to 4 walls with 0 infill, 4 top and bottom layers. Solid volume is about 121.6 cm3, so about 150 g of PLA at 100% infill (less with sparse infill).
