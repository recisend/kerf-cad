# Hex Lattice Lantern (2026-11-26)

Original abstract design: a six-sided lantern with diamond windows that holds a battery (LED) tealight. It is not based on any real product, brand or character.

**Files:** `hex-lattice-lantern.3mf` (print file, mm), `.kerf.json` (editable source), `.png` (preview).

## Why this design
- Home décor, candle/tealight and gift items are among the most consistently listed categories on Etsy's "best selling 3D printed items" pages. The seasonal fit is strong (holiday and winter décor around late November).
- Caveat: the trend sources are seller-guide blogs and Etsy category pages, not live sales data for this month. Check eRank or Etsy search before listing. Articulated figures were also reported, but the sources describe that category as saturated.

## Specs
- Size: 62.4 x 72 x 70 mm. Fits the Snapmaker U1's 270 x 270 x 270 mm bed with room for many copies per plate.
- Walls 1.6 mm (four lines at 0.4 mm), floor 3 mm (about 15 layers at 0.2 mm).
- Cavity apothem about 29.6 mm, so a standard 38 mm LED tealight fits with room to spare.
- Windows are diamonds with 58 degree overhangs: no supports needed.
- Volume 28.8 cm3. Estimated 36 g solid PLA, less with sparse infill (walls are thin, so infill barely matters).

## Suggested slicer settings (Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm stainless nozzle)
- Layer height 0.2 mm, 4 wall loops, 3 top / 3 bottom layers, 10-15% infill.
- Supports: off. Brim: not needed.
- PLA, or a silk/translucent PLA, which glows nicely around the windows.
- Multicolor idea: the U1 has four toolheads, so a colour change on the lantern or a gradient is possible. The export is a single-colour part.
- Safety: use a battery LED tealight only. A real flame can melt PLA. Put this in the listing.
