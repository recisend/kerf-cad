# Honeycomb Desk Organizer — 2026-11-10

Original design (no brands, characters or real-world products). Not published anywhere; Etsy listing is up to you.

## Why this one
Desk organizers and hex/honeycomb storage show up consistently in 2026 "what sells on Etsy" roundups (mostly seller/vendor blogs, so treat as directional — Etsy publishes no sales counts). Evergreen gift item, easy to colour-customise, prints with no supports.

## Model
- 7 hex cells (centre 90 mm, tall ring 72 mm, short ring 54 mm), staggered heights
- Footprint 104.8 x 101.2 x 90 mm (Snapmaker U1 build volume is 270 mm cube)
- Wall 1.6 mm (4 perimeters at 0.4 mm line width), shared walls single thickness; floor 2.0 mm
- ~79 cm³ solid volume, ~98 g PLA at 100% (less with sparse infill/perimeter-only)

## Print settings (Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle)
- Orient as exported (flat on bed), no supports, no brim needed
- Layer 0.2 mm, 4 walls, 10 bottom layers (2.0 mm floor), 5 top layers, infill 10% (walls are almost all perimeter)
- PLA ~210 °C nozzle / 60 °C bed; vase-style variants not needed
- Suggested colourways for listing photos: silk gold, matte black, sage green
