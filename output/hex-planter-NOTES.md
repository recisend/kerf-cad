# Hex Planter + Saucer (2026-11-26)
Original geometric design - no real-world items, brands or characters.
- Planter: hex, 90 mm across corners, 75 mm tall, 2 mm walls/floor, 6 mm drain hole, 1 mm base chamfer.
- Saucer: 102 mm across corners, 12 mm tall, ~1.9 mm clearance per side.
- Both parts lie flat on the plate: no supports needed. Fits Snapmaker U1 (270 mm cube).
- Suggested 0.4 mm nozzle settings: 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls (Arachne), 15% infill (floor 5 bottom layers), PLA/PETG, textured PEI. Verify nozzle = 0.4 mm in slicer and on printer.
- Trend note: planters/organizers are consistently listed among Etsy 3D-print sellers, but I could not verify live Etsy sales data. Not published anywhere.
