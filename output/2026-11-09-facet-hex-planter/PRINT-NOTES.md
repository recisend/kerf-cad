# Facet Hex Planter (original design, 2026-11-09)

Original geometric hexagonal planter + matching drip saucer. Not based on any real product, brand or character.
Why this: geometric/parametric planters and desk/home decor are consistently named among top Etsy 3D-print categories
(directional evidence from SEO/marketing blogs, not hard sales data).

## Printer: Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle (270 x 270 x 270 mm bed)
- Fits easily: both parts together occupy ~80 x 188 mm footprint, 80 mm tall. Print both on one plate.
- Orient as modeled (open top up, saucer flat). No supports needed: wall taper is ~4 deg from vertical, drain hole is in a flat floor.
- Slicer (Snapmaker Orca): 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layer, 3 walls (wall thickness is 1.6 mm = 4 x 0.4 mm lines, so use 4 walls for clean single-pass geometry), 4 top/bottom layers, 15% gyroid infill (mostly walls anyway).
- Material: PLA / PLA Silk / PETG. Drainage hole is 8 mm. Not food-safe; for plants only.
- Estimated mass in PLA: planter ~46 g, saucer ~14 g.
- Fit: saucer inner apothem 36.5 mm vs planter base apothem 32.9 mm (3.6 mm clearance).
- Parameters: wall variable `#wall` = 1.6 mm in the .kerf.json.
