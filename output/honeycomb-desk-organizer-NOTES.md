## Model
- 7 hexagonal cells (1 center + 6 ring), shared walls, stepped heights 100 / 75 / 55 mm.
- Footprint 102.4 x 98.8 mm, height 100 mm. Fits the Snapmaker U1 bed (270 x 270 x 270 mm) easily.
- Walls 1.6 mm = exactly 4 perimeters at 0.4 mm line width. Floor 2.0 mm = 10 layers at 0.2 mm.
- No overhangs, no bridges, no supports. Flat bottom, prints upright.

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle settings
- Nozzle 0.4 mm, layer height 0.2 mm (0.16 mm for a smoother finish), line width 0.42-0.45 mm.
- Walls: 4 perimeters (wall thickness is 1.6 mm so everything is solid perimeters; infill barely used).
- Top/bottom layers: bottom 5, top 5. Infill 10-15 % gyroid.
- Supports: off. Brim: optional 3-5 mm if bed adhesion is a worry (many thin walls).
- Material: PLA or PLA+ (verify temperatures against your filament profile / Snapmaker Orca profile).
- Rough estimate: ~80.5 cm3 of material; check actual time/weight in the slicer.
- Colour idea: single-colour amber/honey, or a different colour per ring height on the multi-toolhead U1.
