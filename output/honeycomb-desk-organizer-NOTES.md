# Honeycomb Desk Organizer (2026-11-15)

Original parametric design, not based on any real product or brand. 7 hex cells in three heights (95 / 70 / 52 mm). Footprint 109.6 x 105.8 x 95 mm.

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle optimisation
- Fits the 270 mm build volume with room to spare, so it can go on the plate with other items.
- Walls are 1.6 mm: 4 perimeters at the 0.4 mm line width, and shared walls between cells are also exactly 1.6 mm.
- Floor is 2 mm: 10 layers at 0.2 mm.
- Flat bottom and vertical walls only: no supports, no overhangs, no bridging.
- Suggested slicer settings: 0.2 mm layer height, 4 walls, 3-4 top/bottom layers, 15% infill (the walls are essentially solid), PLA, no supports, brim not needed.
