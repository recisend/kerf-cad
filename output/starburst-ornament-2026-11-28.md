# Starburst Ornament (2026-11-28)

Original geometric design (12-point star with raised 6-point core). It is not based on any real product, character or brand.

- File: `starburst-ornament-2026-11-28.3mf` (source: `.kerf.json`)
- Size: 88 x 90.5 x 4.4 mm, about 14.4 cm3, roughly 18 g in PLA (Kerf's mass figure assumes aluminium, ignore it)
- Printer: Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle. Fits easily in the 270 mm build volume; you can print 6+ per plate.

## Suggested slicer settings (Orca / Snapmaker Orca)
- Layer height 0.2 mm (base = 16 layers, core = 22 layers; 3.2 mm and 4.4 mm are exact multiples)
- 0.4 mm line width, 3 walls, 4 top and 4 bottom layers; infill is almost irrelevant at this thickness
- No supports needed; the flat face goes on the plate
- Minimum feature: the star tips are the sharpest points. Enable "detect thin walls"; the hanger-hole wall is 3.7 mm
- Hanger hole is 3.6 mm: fits twine, ribbon or a small hook
- Multicolour idea (U1 toolchanger): colour change at layer 16 (3.2 mm) for a different core-star colour

## Market notes (honest caveat)
