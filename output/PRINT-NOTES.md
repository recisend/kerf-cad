# Ribbed Barrel Planter + Drip Saucer (original design)

Files: ribbed-planter-set.3mf (2 parts, mm), ribbed-planter-set.kerf.json (editable source), ribbed-planter-set.png (preview)

Size: planter 76 x 76 x 90 mm (Ø8 mm drain hole), saucer Ø80 x 10 mm. Both lie flat on the bed; fits the U1's 270 mm volume.

Suggested Snapmaker U1 / Snapmaker Orca settings (0.4 mm nozzle) - verify in the slicer:
- Printer/process preset: Snapmaker U1 (0.4 nozzle), 0.20 mm layer
- Walls: 3 (planter wall is 2.4 mm = 6 line widths; use 4 walls for a watertight pot), 5 top / 4 bottom layers, infill 10-15%
- Supports: none needed (max overhang ~20 deg from vertical)
- Brim not required; textured PEI plate
- Part colours (grey planter, gold saucer) are in the 3MF; map to U1 toolheads for a two-colour print.
- Material: PETG recommended for wet use; PLA fine for dry/decor. Not food-safe.

Market note: ribbed/fluted planters are a long-running home-decor category on Etsy; search-volume figures come from seller blog posts, not Etsy data, so treat trend claims as estimates.
