# Honeycomb Desk Organizer (2026-11-11)

Original generic design (3 hex cells, 100/80/65 mm tall). Not based on any real-world item.

- Printer: Snapmaker U1, 0.4 mm nozzle. Footprint 101 x 100 x 100 mm (bed is 270 mm, fits easily).
- Walls: 1.6 mm outer (4 perimeters), 3.2 mm shared walls between cells; 2.0 mm floor (10 layers at 0.2 mm).
- No overhangs, no bridges, flat base: print with NO supports, upright.
- Suggested slicer: 0.2 mm layer, 4 walls, 3 top/bottom layers, 10-15% gyroid infill (mostly walls anyway), PLA.
- Mass estimate: ~96 g solid PLA (slicer will be lower with sparse infill).
- Colour: set to amber in the 3MF; change per filament in Orca/Snapmaker Orca as desired.
