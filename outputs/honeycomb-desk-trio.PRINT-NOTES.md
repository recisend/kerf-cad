# Honeycomb Desk Trio (2026-11-13)
Original geometric desk organizer: three fused hex cells (60 / 85 / 110 mm tall). Not based on any real product.

Trend basis: desk accessories / organizers and honeycomb-geometric decor are listed as strong 3D-print categories on Etsy in 2026 (seller-blog sources; demand figures are not independently verified).

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm nozzle optimisation
- Footprint 110.9 x 112 mm, height 110 mm (U1 volume 270^3) - prints flat, no supports needed (all vertical walls, flat floor).
- Walls 1.6 mm = 4 x 0.4 mm lines (shared walls 3.2 mm = 8 lines); floor 2.0 mm = 10 layers at 0.2 mm.
- 0.6 mm bottom chamfer to offset elephant foot.
- Suggested slicer (Snapmaker Orca): 0.4 nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, Arachne walls, 4 walls, 4 top/bottom layers, 0 % infill is fine (wall-dominated), no supports, brim optional (per community note, brim helps with U1).
- Colour per cell is possible via separate filament assignment in the slicer (single-part 3MF).
