# Honeycomb Desk Organizer (2026-11-11)

Original design, not based on any real product, brand or character.
Files: `honeycomb-desk-organizer.3mf` (print file), `.kerf.json` (editable in Kerf), `*-iso.png` / `*-top.png` (previews).

## Model
- 7 hex cells (1 centre + 6 around), staggered heights 50-95 mm, footprint 89.5 x 86.5 mm, so it is well inside the U1's 270 mm cube.
- Cell inner width 27.7 mm across flats; fits pens, brushes, makeup brushes, tools.
- Wall 1.6 mm = 4 perimeters at 0.4 mm line width. Floor 1.6 mm = 8 layers at 0.2 mm.
- Flat base on the bed, vertical walls, open top: no overhangs, no supports needed.
- About 65 cm3 solid, roughly 80 g of PLA at 100% (less with the settings below).

## Snapmaker U1 / 0.4 mm settings (Snapmaker Orca)
- Nozzle 0.4 mm, layer height 0.20 mm, line width 0.42 mm
- Walls: 4 (Arachne wall generator), top/bottom layers 0 / 8
- Infill: 0-5% (walls are the whole part; the floor is solid)
- Supports: off. Brim: optional 3-5 mm for the tall cells; the U1 community advice is a brim object gap of 0.8-0.9 mm
- Material: PLA, PLA+ or PETG; silk / dual-tone PLA looks best in photos
- Single colour. Print time estimate: 4-6 h (check in the slicer)
